<Field id="dto-title" title="DIRECT TO annunciation">
	`DIRECT TO:` at the top of the Direct To page shows that the page makes the waypoint you approve the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="dto-ident" title="Default waypoint">
	The [default waypoint](/kln90b/operation/direct-to/#the-default-waypoint), the waypoint that the page offers, flashing in inverse video while the unit waits for <kbd>ENT</kbd> to approve the waypoint page on the right side. <kbd>ENT</kbd> makes the direct-to to it. The left inner knob starts a new entry, which replaces the waypoint and drops the approval.
</Field>

<Field id="ident" title="Identifier of the waypoint shown for approval">
	The identifier of the waypoint page shown for approval, which is the default waypoint. The cursor cannot change the identifier here.
</Field>

<Field id="name" title="Airport name">
	The name of the airport in two rows.
</Field>

<Field id="pos" title="Airport position">
	The position of the airport: latitude above, longitude below, in degrees, minutes and hundredths of a minute.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on. The Direct To page opens with its cursor on.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation, which shows the mode of the unit. <Term t="ENR-LEG">`ENR-LEG`</Term> is Enroute-Leg mode.
</Field>

<Field id="ent" title="Enter prompt">
	<Term t="ENT">`ENT`</Term> flashes while the unit waits for <kbd>ENT</kbd> to approve what it shows, such as a waypoint page shown for approval.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side: APT 1 of the waypoint shown for approval. The right inner knob turns to the other pages of the airport before you approve it.
</Field>
