<Field id="from" title="Direct To symbol">
	The Direct To symbol stands in place of the from waypoint: the active leg is a [direct-to](/kln90b/operation/direct-to/) that starts where you made it. The arrow after it marks the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="to" title="Active waypoint">
	The identifier of the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint). A direct-to waypoint that FPL 0 does not hold stays active after you pass it.
</Field>

<Field id="cdi" title="D-bar">
	The D-bar shows how far the aircraft is from the active leg, on a scale of five dots on each side of the center; the bar stands on the side you must fly to. The center of the scale is the TO/FROM indicator, which shows whether the active waypoint lies ahead (TO) or behind you (FROM).
</Field>

<Field id="dis" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	`DIS` is the direct distance to the active waypoint in NM, with one decimal below 100 NM. Past the waypoint it counts up again.
</Field>

<Field id="gs" title="Groundspeed">
	<Term t="GS">Your groundspeed</Term> in kt.
</Field>

<Field id="ete" title="Estimated time en route">
	The <Term t="ETE">time to the active waypoint</Term> at your present groundspeed. Below one hour the hours stay blank, and `:01` is 1 minute. Past the waypoint it is still the distance at your groundspeed, so it grows as you fly away.
</Field>

<Field id="brg" title="Bearing to the active waypoint">
	<Term t="BRG">`BRG`</Term> is the magnetic bearing from your position to the active waypoint, in whole degrees. Past the waypoint it points back to it.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side: `NAV 1`.
</Field>
