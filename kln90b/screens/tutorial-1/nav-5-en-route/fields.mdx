<Field id="orientation" title="Map orientation">
	The map orientation: which direction points to the top of the map. `N^` means north up; the unit draws the `^` as an arrow that points up. With the cursor on the field, the inner knob chooses another map orientation, and the map follows at once. [The map orientation](/kln90b/pages/nav/#the-map-orientation) explains the choices.
</Field>

<Field id="range" title="Range scale">
	The range scale in NM: the distance from the aircraft to the top of the map. With the cursor on the field, the inner knob steps through the range scales, and the map follows at once. [The range scale](/kln90b/pages/nav/#the-range-scale) lists them.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the left side: `NAV 5`. NAV 5 is the same page on the left and on the right side.
</Field>

<Field id="leg" title="Direct-to course">
	The course of a [direct-to](/kln90b/operation/direct-to/): an arrow from the point where you made the direct-to to the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="active-waypoint" title="Active waypoint">
	The [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint) as a star. The unit draws the star for an active waypoint that FPL 0 does not hold ([What NAV 5 draws](/kln90b/pages/nav/#what-nav-5-draws)).
</Field>

<Field id="aircraft" title="Aircraft">
	The aircraft, a diamond in the middle of the map in north up.
</Field>
