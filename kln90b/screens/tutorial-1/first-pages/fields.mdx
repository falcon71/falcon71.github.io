<Field id="title" title="Title">
	The title of NAV 2. The rows below give the position of the aircraft as a radial and a distance from a VOR and as latitude and longitude.
</Field>

<Field id="vor" title="Nearby VOR and radial">
	The nearby VOR that the position is related to and the magnetic radial from that VOR to the aircraft, in whole degrees (`FR` is from). The nearby VOR is the nearest low or high altitude VOR, which the unit looks for again while the page is shown. The field shows `---` and `---°` without a position and when no VOR is in range.
</Field>

<Field id="dist" title="Distance to the nearby VOR">
	The direct distance from the aircraft to the nearby VOR, in NM with tenths.
</Field>

<Field id="lat" title="Latitude">
	The latitude of the present position as hemisphere, degrees and minutes to hundredths. It shows dashes without a position, never the last position.
</Field>

<Field id="lon" title="Longitude">
	The longitude of the present position as hemisphere, degrees and minutes to hundredths. It shows dashes without a position, never the last position.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the left side: `NAV 2`, the page the left side shows after the power-on pages.
</Field>
