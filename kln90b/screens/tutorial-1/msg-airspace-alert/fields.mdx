<Field id="message" title="Airspace alert">
	The first line of a message: the track ahead meets an area of special use airspace, or one is close. The two lines below it name the area and give its limits. [`AIRSPACE ALERT:`](/kln90b/operation/alerts/#airspace-alert) explains the message.
</Field>

<Field id="name" title="Name of the area">
	The name of the area, cut to fit the line. An area that the database stores in several parts gives one message for each part it warns of. {/* kln90b#523: describes the current behavior; the fix makes the unit give one message per area */}
</Field>

<Field id="type" title="Type of the area">
	The type of the area at the right end of the line: `CL B` for Class B, `CL C` for Class C, and the codes of the other areas of special use airspace that [`AIRSPACE ALERT:`](/kln90b/operation/alerts/#airspace-alert) lists.
</Field>

<Field id="limits" title="Limits of the area">
	The vertical limits of the area in feet above mean sea level. Class B and C show the ceiling only, as `BELOW` and the ceiling.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation, which shows the mode of the unit. <Term t="ENR-LEG">`ENR-LEG`</Term> is Enroute-Leg mode.
</Field>

<Field id="msg" title="Message prompt">
	<Term t="MSG">`MSG`</Term> steady: you have read every message the unit holds; it flashes while one is unread ([The message prompt](/kln90b/operation/messages/#the-message-prompt)). <kbd>MSG</kbd> shows the next page of messages or closes the page after the last.
</Field>
