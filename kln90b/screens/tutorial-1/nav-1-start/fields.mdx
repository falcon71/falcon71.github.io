<Field id="from" title="Direct To symbol">
	The Direct To symbol stands in place of the from waypoint: the active leg is a [direct-to](/kln90b/operation/direct-to/) that starts where you made it. The arrow after it marks the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="to" title="Active waypoint">
	The identifier of the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint). The distance, the time and the bearing of the page refer to it.
</Field>

<Field id="cdi" title="D-bar">
	The D-bar shows how far the aircraft is from the active leg, on a scale of five dots on each side of the center; the bar stands on the side you must fly to. Centered, it shows that the aircraft is on the leg, which is where a direct-to starts. The center of the scale is the TO/FROM indicator, which shows whether the active waypoint lies ahead (TO) or behind you (FROM).
</Field>

<Field id="dis" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	`DIS` is the direct distance to the active waypoint in NM, with one decimal below 100 NM.
</Field>

<Field id="gs" title="Groundspeed">
	Your groundspeed in kt.
</Field>

<Field id="ete" title="Estimated time en route">
	The time to the active waypoint at your present groundspeed. It shows `--:--` when you are stopped or nearly so, so it stays dashed while the aircraft stands still.
</Field>

<Field id="brg" title="Bearing to the active waypoint">
	`BRG` is the magnetic bearing from your position to the active waypoint, in whole degrees.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side: `NAV 1`, which the right side shows after a direct-to.
</Field>
