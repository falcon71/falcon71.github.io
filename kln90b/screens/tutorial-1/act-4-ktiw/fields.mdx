<Field id="arrow" title="Active waypoint arrow">
	The arrow before the identifier marks the waypoint as the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint). It flashes during [waypoint alerting](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#waypoint-alerting).
</Field>

<Field id="number" title="Number in the flight plan">
	The number of the waypoint in the active flight plan. It is blank for a direct-to waypoint that is not in FPL 0.
</Field>

<Field id="ident" title="Airport identifier">
	The identifier of the active waypoint. It cannot be changed on the ACT pages.
</Field>

<Field id="type" title="Waypoint type">
	`A` for airport, the waypoint type of the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="approach" title="Approach control frequency">
	The frequency of approach control (frequency type `APR`), in MHz.
</Field>

<Field id="atis" title="ATIS frequency">
	The ATIS frequency (frequency type `ATIS`), in MHz.
</Field>

<Field id="ctaf" title="CTAF frequency">
	The common traffic advisory frequency of the airport (frequency type `CTAF`), in MHz.
</Field>

<Field id="ground" title="Ground control frequency">
	The frequency of ground control (frequency type `GRND`), in MHz.
</Field>

<Field id="tower" title="Tower frequency">
	The frequency of the tower (frequency type `TWR`), in MHz.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side. `ACT+4` is APT 4 of the active airport, and the `+` means that it has more than one page, because the airport has more than five frequencies; the right inner knob steps to the next one.
</Field>
