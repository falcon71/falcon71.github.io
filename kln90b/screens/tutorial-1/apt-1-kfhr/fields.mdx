<Field id="ident" title="Characters of the identifier">
	The characters of the identifier left of the cursor. The right outer knob moves the cursor back to them, and the right inner knob changes the character under the cursor. When you change a character, the unit fills in the characters to the right of it with the first airport that matches.
</Field>

<Field id="char" title="Character under the cursor">
	The character that the right inner knob changes. The change takes effect at once and needs no <kbd>ENT</kbd>, and the page shows the airport the identifier names.
</Field>

<Field id="name" title="Airport name">
	The name of the airport in two rows. A long name runs on into the second row where the first is full, even in the middle of a word.
</Field>

<Field id="pos" title="Airport position">
	The position of the airport, latitude above and longitude below. You cannot change it for an airport of the database.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the right cursor is on. The right knobs then work the cursor instead of selecting pages. Pressing the right <kbd>CRSR</kbd> turns the cursor off again, and the page name returns.
</Field>
