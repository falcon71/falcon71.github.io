<Field id="ident" title="Airport identifier">
	The identifier of the airport, an airport of the [nearest list](/kln90b/operation/nearest/).
</Field>

<Field id="rank" title="Rank in the nearest list">
	The rank of the airport in the list of the nearest airports, shown as `NR` (for nearest) and a number, `NR 1` for the nearest ([The nearest lists](/kln90b/operation/nearest/#the-nearest-lists)). The rank flashes while the cursor is elsewhere.
</Field>

<Field id="name" title="Airport name">
	The name of the airport in two rows. A long name runs on into the second row where the first is full, even in the middle of a word.
</Field>

<Field id="length" title="Length of the longest runway">
	The length of the longest runway in feet, followed by `'`.
</Field>

<Field id="surface" title="Surface of the longest runway">
	The surface of the longest runway as a three-letter code, as on APT 3: `HRD` for a hard surface.
</Field>

<Field id="lighting" title="Lighting of the longest runway">
	The lighting of the longest runway: `L` for lighting from sunset to sunrise, `LPC` for pilot-controlled lighting, `LPT` for part-time lighting. The field is blank when the runway has no lighting or the lighting is not known.
</Field>

<Field id="bearing" title="Bearing to the airport">
	The magnetic bearing from the aircraft to the airport, in three digits and followed by `TO`.
</Field>

<Field id="distance" title="Distance to the airport">
	The distance from the aircraft to the airport in NM, with one decimal place and a leading zero below 10 NM.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side: APT 1 of the nearest airport.
</Field>
