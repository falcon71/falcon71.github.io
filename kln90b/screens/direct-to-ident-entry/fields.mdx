<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`DIRECT TO:` shows that the page makes the waypoint you enter the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="ident" title="Identifier being entered">
	The identifier that you are entering, in five cells. The unit shows an entry that is not accepted yet in inverse video, and the cell under the cursor flashes. The left inner knob changes the character, and the left outer knob moves to the next one. The unit fills in the cells to the right of the cursor with the first waypoint that matches. <kbd>ENT</kbd> accepts the entry. <kbd>CLR</kbd> blanks it, and turning the cursor off drops the entry.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>
