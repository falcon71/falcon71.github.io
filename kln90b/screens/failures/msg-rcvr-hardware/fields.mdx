<Field id="message" title="Receiver hardware error">
	The GPS receiver reports a fault of its own or of the antenna, with its code in four digits on the second line. `0010` and `0110` are a failed antenna or antenna cable; the other codes are faults inside the receiver. Several faults show as one code that combines theirs. The message stays while the fault lasts, and comes back unread at every power-on. See [The receiver and the antenna](/kln90b/operation/failures/#the-receiver-and-the-antenna).
</Field>
<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The navigation mode; `ENR-LEG` is Enroute-Leg mode. It stays in the status line while the MSG page is shown.
</Field>
<Field id="msg" title="Message prompt">
	<Term t="MSG" /> in inverse video: the unit holds messages. It is steady once you have read them all and flashes while one is unread. Pressing <kbd>MSG</kbd> shows the next page of messages or closes the MSG page.
</Field>
