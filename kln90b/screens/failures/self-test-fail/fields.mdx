<Field id="dis" title="Test distance">
	A fixed test value, 34.5 NM, in place of the distance to the active waypoint. While the Self Test page is shown, the left page carries test values in all its fields except `OBS IN`, and the unit sends them to the instruments of the aircraft that it drives.
</Field>

<Field id="cdi" title="Test deflection of the D-bar">
	A fixed test deflection of the D-bar. It shows that the bar and the instruments that the unit drives respond.
</Field>

<Field id="obs" title="OBS IN field">
	`OBS IN` is the course that the unit reads from the OBS setting of the external indicator in the aircraft, in degrees magnetic.
</Field>

<Field id="out" title="OBS OUT field">
	`OBS OUT` is a fixed test value, 315°, for the desired track that the unit sends out.
</Field>

<Field id="rmi" title="RMI field">
	A fixed test value, 130°, for the bearing to the active waypoint that the unit sends to the RMI, the radio magnetic indicator, of the aircraft.
</Field>

<Field id="testfail" title="Failed self test">
	`TEST FAIL` flashes in place of `ANNUN ON` when the self test has found a fault: a failed part of the unit or of its installation, or an error the GPS receiver reports. It does not stop the power-on: you approve the page as usual, and the MSG page then says what failed. See [When the unit notices a failure](/kln90b/operation/failures/#when-the-unit-notices-a-failure).
</Field>

<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	The title of the right page.
</Field>

<Field id="date" title="Date">
	The date of the unit: day, month and two-digit year, in the system time zone shown on the line below. You change it with the cursor and accept it with <kbd>ENT</kbd>. Correcting a wrong date restarts the search for satellites from it while the unit has no position.
</Field>

<Field id="time" title="Time">
	The time of the unit in hours and minutes, in the system time zone beside it. You change it with the cursor and accept it with <kbd>ENT</kbd>; the seconds then start at 00. Correcting a wrong time restarts the search for satellites from it while the unit has no position.
</Field>

<Field id="seconds" title="Seconds">
	The seconds of the time. They count on by themselves and cannot be changed on their own.
</Field>

<Field id="zone" title="Time zone">
	The code of the system time zone, in which the unit shows the date and the time (`UTC` for universal time; [The time zone](/kln90b/operation/trip-planning/#the-time-zone) lists the zones). With the cursor on it, the right inner knob steps through the time zones.
</Field>

<Field id="alt" title="Indicated altitude">
	`ALT` is the altitude that the aircraft's altitude input gives, rounded to 100 ft. It shows dashes while the unit receives no altitude.
</Field>

<Field id="baro" title="Altimeter baro setting">
	`BARO` is the altimeter baro setting of the unit in inches of mercury, which the unit uses for altitude. You change it digit by digit with the cursor. It cannot be changed where the aircraft transmits the setting to the unit.
</Field>

<Field id="approve" title="Approval of the self test">
	Pressing <kbd>ENT</kbd> with the cursor on `APPROVE?` approves the self test and ends it. The unit then shows the next power-on page.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation, which shows the mode of the unit. <Term t="ENR-LEG">`ENR-LEG`</Term> is Enroute-Leg mode, en route navigation along a leg.
</Field>

<Field id="prompt" title="Message and enter prompt">
	The three cells after the mode annunciation. A flashing <Term t="ENT">`ENT`</Term> means that <kbd>ENT</kbd> accepts what the cursor is on. <Term t="MSG">`MSG`</Term> flashes in the same cells while the unit holds a message you have not read, and is steady once you have read every message it holds ([The message prompt](/kln90b/operation/messages/#the-message-prompt)). They are blank when neither applies.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> shows that the right cursor is on. This page shows no page identifications. Pressing the right <kbd>CRSR</kbd> button turns the cursor off.
</Field>
