<Field id="ident" title="Airport identifier">
	The airport of the APT pages, followed by <Term t="IAP" />. A three-character identifier keeps the blank of the
	fourth character. To look at another airport, turn the cursor on and enter its identifier here.
</Field>

<Field id="none" title="No approaches">
	The database has no approach that the unit can fly for this airport. Approaches the unit cannot fly, such as ILS
	and localizer approaches, are left out, so an airport that has only such approaches shows this text too.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page name">
	`APT 8` names the page.
</Field>
