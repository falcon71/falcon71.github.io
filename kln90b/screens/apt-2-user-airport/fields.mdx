<Field id="ident" title="Identifier of the user-defined airport">
	The identifier of a [user-defined airport](/kln90b/operation/waypoints/#user-waypoints).
</Field>

<Field id="elevation" title="Elevation of the user-defined airport">
	The elevation of the user-defined airport above sea level in feet. It shows `_____` until you enter a value. With the cursor on the field, the right inner knob enters the first digit and starts the entry, the right outer knob moves between the five digits, and <kbd>ENT</kbd> stores the value. The range is 0 to 99,999 ft, in steps of 1 ft. <kbd>CLR</kbd> with an entry open cancels it and restores the old value.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side.
</Field>
