<Field id="dis" title="Test distance">
	A fixed test value, 34.5 NM, in place of the distance to the active waypoint. Every field of the left page except `OBS IN` holds a fixed test value, which the unit also sends to the instruments of the aircraft that it drives, so that you can check that they respond.
</Field>

<Field id="cdi" title="Test deflection of the D-bar">
	A fixed test deflection of the D-bar: half scale to the right, with the TO/FROM indicator on FROM. The CDI or HSI of the aircraft shows the same deflection when the unit drives it.
</Field>

<Field id="obs" title="OBS setting of the aircraft">
	`OBS IN` is the course that the unit reads from the OBS setting of the external indicator in the aircraft, in degrees magnetic. It is the one live value of the left page: turn the OBS knob and it follows.
</Field>

<Field id="out" title="OBS OUT test course">
	`OUT`, the `OBS OUT` course, is a fixed test value, 315°, for the desired track that the unit sends out.
</Field>

<Field id="rmi" title="Test bearing for the RMI">
	A fixed test value, 130°, for the bearing to the active waypoint that the unit sends to the RMI, the radio magnetic indicator, of the aircraft and to its autopilot.
</Field>

<Field id="annun" title="Annunciator test">
	`ANNUN` `ON`, annunciators on, means that the unit lights its remote mounted annunciators, as far as the aircraft has them, so that you can check them. A flashing `TEST FAIL` takes its place when the self test has found a fault.
</Field>

<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`DATE/TIME` heads the right page: the clock of the unit, the altitude and the baro setting.
</Field>

<Field id="date" title="Date">
	The date of the unit: day, month and year in two digits, in the system time zone shown on the line below. The years 88 to 99 are 1988 to 1999 and 00 to 87 are 2000 to 2087. You change the date with the cursor and accept it with <kbd>ENT</kbd>. While the unit is not NAV ready, a new date makes its search for satellites start over from it. Once the unit is NAV ready, the satellites set the clock again.
</Field>

<Field id="time" title="Time">
	The time of the unit in hours and minutes, 00:00 to 23:59, in the system time zone beside it. You change it with the cursor and accept it with <kbd>ENT</kbd>; the seconds then start at 00. It acts on the search for satellites like the date.
</Field>

<Field id="seconds" title="Seconds">
	The seconds of the time. They count on by themselves and cannot be entered.
</Field>

<Field id="zone" title="System time zone">
	The code of the system time zone in which the unit shows the date and the time ([The time zone](/kln90b/operation/trip-planning/#the-time-zone) lists the zones). With the cursor on it, the right inner knob steps through the zones. The unit keeps the zone while it is off.
</Field>

<Field id="alt" title="Indicated altitude">
	`ALT` is the altitude that the aircraft's altitude input gives, corrected for the baro setting and rounded to 100 ft. It shows dashes while the unit receives no altitude.
</Field>

<Field id="baro" title="Altimeter baro setting">
	`BARO` is the altimeter baro setting of the unit, in inches of mercury or in millibars as SET 7 chooses. [The baro setting](/kln90b/operation/alerts/#the-baro-setting) explains it.
</Field>

<Field id="approve" title="Approval of the self test">
	Pressing <kbd>ENT</kbd> with the cursor on `APPROVE?` approves the self test and ends it. The unit then shows the next power-on page.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation, which shows the mode of the unit. <Term t="ENR-LEG">`ENR-LEG`</Term> is Enroute-Leg mode, en route navigation along a leg.
</Field>

<Field id="prompt" title="Message and enter prompt">
	The three cells after the mode annunciation. <Term t="MSG">`MSG`</Term> in inverse video means that the unit holds a message, and a flashing <Term t="ENT">`ENT`</Term> means that <kbd>ENT</kbd> accepts what the cursor is on. They are blank when neither applies.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> shows that the right cursor is on. This page shows no page identifications. Pressing the right <kbd>CRSR</kbd> button turns the cursor off.
</Field>
