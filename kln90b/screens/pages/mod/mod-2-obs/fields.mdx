<Field id="title" title="Mode is active">
	`ACTIVE MODE` shows that the unit navigates in [OBS mode](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#obs-mode), the course mode of this page.
</Field>

<Field id="course" title="Selected course">
	The selected course of OBS mode, as a magnetic course. The colon after `OBS` shows that you set it on the unit: with the cursor on it, each step of the left inner knob turns the course by 1°, and the unit lays the new course through the active waypoint at once, without <kbd>ENT</kbd>. See [The selected course](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-selected-course).
</Field>

<Field id="scale" title="CDI scale factor">
	The [CDI scale factor](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-cdi-scale): the cross-track distance at which the D-bar and the external indicator reach full scale. With the cursor on it, the left inner knob selects another one.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the left page, `MOD 2`.
</Field>
