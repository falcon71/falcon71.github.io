<Field id="title" title="Mode is active">
	`ACTIVE MODE` shows that the unit navigates in [OBS mode](/kln90b/operation/navigation-modes/#obs-mode), the course mode of this page.
</Field>

<Field id="course" title="Selected course">
	The selected course of OBS mode, as a magnetic course. Without a colon after `OBS` it is the course of the external indicator, which the unit reads: turn the indicator to change it. The cursor skips it. See [The selected course](/kln90b/operation/navigation-modes/#the-selected-course).
</Field>

<Field id="scale" title="CDI scale factor">
	The [CDI scale factor](/kln90b/operation/navigation-modes/#the-cdi-scale): the cross-track distance at which the D-bar and the external indicator reach full scale. With the cursor on it, the left inner knob selects another one.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the left page, `MOD 2`.
</Field>
