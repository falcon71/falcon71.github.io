<Field id="title" title="Mode is active">
	`ACTIVE MODE` shows that the unit navigates in [leg mode](/kln90b/operation/navigation-modes/#leg-mode), the course mode of this page. In Approach Arm-Leg mode and Approach Active-Leg mode the page shows it as well.
</Field>

<Field id="leg" title="Leg mode">
	`LEG` names the course mode of this page. MOD 1 is the page of [leg mode](/kln90b/operation/navigation-modes/#leg-mode), MOD 2 the page of [OBS mode](/kln90b/operation/navigation-modes/#obs-mode).
</Field>

<Field id="scale" title="CDI scale factor">
	The [CDI scale factor](/kln90b/operation/navigation-modes/#the-cdi-scale): the cross-track distance at which the D-bar and the external indicator reach full scale. With the cursor on it, the left inner knob selects another one.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the left page, `MOD 1`.
</Field>
