<Field id="title" title="Prompt to change the mode">
	`PRESS ENT` `TO ACTIVATE`: the unit is in [OBS mode](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#obs-mode). <kbd>ENT</kbd> changes it to [leg mode](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#leg-mode), with the cursor on or off, and the enter prompt `ENT` shows in the status line while this page is shown. This prompt appears only without a GPS CRS switch; with one, the page shows `PRESS GPS` `CRS FOR` and <kbd>ENT</kbd> does nothing.
</Field>

<Field id="leg" title="Leg mode">
	`LEG` names the course mode of this page. MOD 1 is the page of [leg mode](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#leg-mode), MOD 2 the page of [OBS mode](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#obs-mode).
</Field>

<Field id="scale" title="CDI scale factor">
	The [CDI scale factor](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-cdi-scale-factor): the cross-track error at which the D-bar and the external indicator reach full scale. With the cursor on it, the left inner knob selects another one.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the left page, `MOD 1`.
</Field>
