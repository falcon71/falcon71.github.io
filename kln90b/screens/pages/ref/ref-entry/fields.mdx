<Field id="use" title="Questions to activate the plan">
	`USE? INVRT?` stands on the top line of a flight plan that holds waypoints.
</Field>

<Field id="first" title="First waypoint of the flight plan">
	The first waypoint of the flight plan on the left side.
</Field>

<Field id="second" title="Second waypoint of the flight plan">
	The second waypoint of the flight plan on the left side.
</Field>

<Field id="blank" title="Blank waypoint position">
	The blank waypoint position after the last waypoint, where a waypoint is added at the end.
</Field>

<Field id="prompt" title="Prompt for the waypoint you enter">
	`ENTER REF` stands on the REF page while an FPL page is on the left side.
</Field>

<Field id="entry" title="Identifier of the waypoint you enter">
	The identifier of the waypoint you want a waypoint abeam of, of any type. <kbd>ENT</kbd> shows its waypoint page for approval.
</Field>

<Field id="left-page" title="Page identification of the left side">
	The page identification on the left side. FPL 1 is a numbered flight plan.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the right cursor is on.
</Field>

<Field id="ent" title="Enter prompt">
	<Term t="ENT">`ENT`</Term> flashes while an entry waits for <kbd>ENT</kbd>.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation, which shows the mode of the unit. `ENR-LEG` is Enroute-Leg mode.
</Field>
