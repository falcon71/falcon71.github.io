<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`RAIM STATUS`: the page predicts <Term t="RAIM" /> for a destination at a time.
</Field>

<Field id="dest" title="Destination">
	`DEST:` and the identifier of the waypoint that the prediction is for.
</Field>

<Field id="eta" title="Estimated time of arrival">
	`ETA:` and the time of day that the middle bar of the bar graph is for, in the time zone below it.
</Field>

<Field id="zone" title="Time zone">
	The system time zone, in which the ETA shows.
</Field>

<Field id="result" title="Bar graph">
	Seven bars, one for each of seven times: the ETA, and 15, 10 and 5 minutes before and after it. A bar above the line means that RAIM is predicted to be available for an approach at the destination at that time, a bar below the line that it is predicted not to be. [Predicting RAIM](/kln90b/operation/gps-receiver/#predicting-raim) explains what the unit does with the result.
</Field>

<Field id="scale" title="Scale of the bar graph">
	The scale under the bars: `-15` under the first bar, `0` under the fourth, the ETA, and `15` under the last. The numbers are minutes before and after the ETA.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the left side.
</Field>
