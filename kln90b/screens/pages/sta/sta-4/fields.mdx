<Field id="time" title="Total operational time">
	`TOTAL TIME` and the time the unit has been on, in whole hours, rounded down. The value does not count up while the page is shown.
</Field>

<Field id="cycles" title="Number of power-ons">
	`PWR CYCLES` and the number of times the unit has been turned on, including the power-on at the start of a flight in an aircraft that starts with the unit on.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the left side.
</Field>
