<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`ESTIMATED POSN ERROR`: the page shows the estimated position error.
</Field>

<Field id="epe" title="Estimated position error">
	The <Term t="EPE" /> in nautical miles, rounded to hundredths. Two decimals, for example `0.01NM`. The unit accepts no position with an error above 3.80 NM, so 3.80 NM is the largest value the page shows. Without a position the page shows dashes instead of the number. [The estimated position error](/kln90b/operation/gps-receiver/#the-estimated-position-error) explains what the number is made of and what makes it large or small.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the left side.
</Field>
