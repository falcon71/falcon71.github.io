<Field id="state" title="GPS state">
	`STATE` and `FAILR`, receiver failure: the receiver has failed. This state takes precedence over every other state, also while the unit still has a position for a moment.
</Field>

<Field id="header" title="Column headings">
	The headings of the satellite list. A failed receiver tracks no satellite, so the list under the headings stays empty and the page has no second page.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the left side. The name is `STA 1` without a `+`, because the list is empty.
</Field>
