<Field id="plan" title="Flight plan on the left page">
	The waypoints of FPL 0, one per row and numbered from 1, in the same rows as the values of D/T 1. The aircraft is on the ground at the first waypoint, which is the from waypoint of the first leg.
</Field>

<Field id="header" title="Column headings">
	`DIS` is the distance and `ETE` the estimated time en route.
</Field>

<Field id="active" title="The active waypoint">
	The distance in NM from your present position to the waypoint. The time shows dashes, because the unit has no estimated time en route while the groundspeed is that low ([Dashes and blank rows](/kln90b/pages/dt/#dashes-and-blank-rows)). The row of the from waypoint is blank.
</Field>

<Field id="next" title="A later waypoint">
	The distance from your present position to the waypoint along the flight plan. The time shows dashes for the same reason as on the row of the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="lname" title="Page identification of the left side">
	The page identification on the left side. FPL 0 is the active flight plan.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation, which shows the mode of the unit. <Term t="ENR-LEG">`ENR-LEG`</Term> is Enroute-Leg mode.
</Field>

<Field id="rname" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification on the right side.
</Field>
