<Field id="plan" title="Flight plan on the left page">
	The waypoints of FPL 0, one per row and numbered from 1, in the same rows as the values of D/T 2. The [active leg symbol](/kln90b/pages/fpl/#the-active-leg-symbol) in front of the first two waypoints shows the leg the unit flies.
</Field>

<Field id="header" title="Column heading">
	<Term t="DIS">`DIS`</Term> is the distance. The column beside it gives the <Term t="ETA">estimated time of arrival</Term> at the waypoint in the same row of the flight plan.
</Field>

<Field id="zone" title="Time zone, with the cursor on">
	The time zone of the ETAs below it, with the cursor on it. The right inner knob chooses the next zone clockwise and the previous one counterclockwise, and after the last zone the first follows. The times change with every step, and you need no <kbd>ENT</kbd>. The unit never switches to daylight time by itself: `PDT` is a zone of its own beside `PST`.
</Field>

<Field id="active" title="The active waypoint">
	The distance in NM to the waypoint and the ETA there, in the time zone of the field at the top.
</Field>

<Field id="next" title="A later waypoint">
	The distance in NM along the flight plan and the ETA at the waypoint, in the time zone of the field at the top.
</Field>

<Field id="lname" title="Page identification of the left side">
	The page identification on the left side. FPL 0 is the active flight plan.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation, which shows the mode of the unit. <Term t="ENR-LEG">`ENR-LEG`</Term> is Enroute-Leg mode.
</Field>

<Field id="cursor" title="The CRSR annunciation">
	`CRSR` takes the place of the page identification of the right side while that side's cursor is on.
</Field>
