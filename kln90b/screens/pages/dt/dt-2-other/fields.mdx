<Field id="active" title="The active waypoint">
	The arrow marks the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint) and flashes during [waypoint alerting](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#waypoint-alerting). The number is the waypoint's place in FPL 0 and is blank for a waypoint outside it. The identifier follows.
</Field>

<Field id="active-dis" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	The distance in NM from your present position to the active waypoint. It shows dashes while the unit has no active waypoint or no position.
</Field>

<Field id="active-eta" title="ETA at the active waypoint">
	The `ETA` at the active waypoint: the present time plus the estimated time en route at your present groundspeed, as hours and minutes of a 24-hour clock. While the unit has no estimated time en route, it shows dashes ([Dashes and blank rows](/kln90b/pages/dt/#dashes-and-blank-rows)).
</Field>

<Field id="zone" title="Time zone">
	The time zone of the ETAs on this page. The right <kbd>CRSR</kbd> puts the cursor on it, and the right inner knob chooses another zone, which applies at once. It is the time zone of every page that shows a time ([Trip planning and calculators](/kln90b/operation/trip-planning/)).
</Field>

<Field id="dest" title="The destination waypoint">
	The number in FPL 0 and the identifier of the destination waypoint, the last waypoint of FPL 0 or, with an approach in FPL 0, its <Term t="MAP">missed approach point</Term>. These three rows are blank while the destination waypoint is the active waypoint, while the active waypoint is a direct-to waypoint outside the flight plan and while the unit has no active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="dest-dis" title="Distance to the destination waypoint">
	The distance in NM from your present position to the destination waypoint along the flight plan: to the active waypoint and from there along each leg in a straight line.
</Field>

<Field id="dest-eta" title="ETA at the destination waypoint">
	The ETA at the destination waypoint, with the time zone beside it as plain text: the present time plus the estimated time en route to it, as hours and minutes of a 24-hour clock.
</Field>

<Field id="rname" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification on the right side. Beside a page that is not an FPL page, D/T 2 shows the active waypoint and the destination waypoint; beside an FPL page it lines its rows up with the rows of that page.
</Field>
