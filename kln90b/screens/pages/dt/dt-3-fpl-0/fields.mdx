<Field id="plan" title="Flight plan on the left page">
	The waypoints of FPL 0, one per row and numbered from 1, in the same rows as the values of D/T 3. The [active leg symbol](/kln90b/pages/fpl/#the-active-leg-symbol) in front of the first two waypoints shows the leg the unit flies.
</Field>

<Field id="header" title="Column headings">
	<Term t="DIS">`DIS`</Term> is the distance and <Term t="DTK">`DTK`</Term> the desired track. When the left page is an FPL page, each row of D/T 3 gives both for the waypoint in the same row of the flight plan.
</Field>

<Field id="active" title="The active waypoint">
	The distance in NM from your present position to the waypoint, and the desired track of the active leg as a magnetic course. In OBS mode the desired track is the selected course. The row of the from waypoint and the rows of the waypoints you have passed stay blank.
</Field>

<Field id="next" title="A later waypoint">
	The distance from your present position to the waypoint along the flight plan, that is, to the active waypoint and from there along each leg in a straight line. The desired track is the magnetic course of the leg that ends at the waypoint, from the waypoint before it. A waypoint that FPL 0 holds twice in a row repeats the values of the row above.
</Field>

<Field id="lname" title="Page identification of the left side">
	The page identification on the left side. FPL 0 is the active flight plan.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation, which shows the mode of the unit. <Term t="ENR-LEG">`ENR-LEG`</Term> is Enroute-Leg mode.
</Field>

<Field id="rname" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification on the right side. While the left page is an FPL page, D/T 3 lines its rows up with the rows of that page; beside any other page it shows the active waypoint and the next waypoint.
</Field>
