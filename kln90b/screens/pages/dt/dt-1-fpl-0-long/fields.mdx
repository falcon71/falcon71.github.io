<Field id="plan" title="Flight plan on the left page">
	The waypoints of FPL 0 that the page shows. FPL 0 scrolls by itself so that the from waypoint stands in the top row and the active waypoint below it, which puts the from waypoint in the row of the column headings. The waypoints that FPL 0 holds between the fifth row and the last one are out of view.
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="The last waypoint">
	The last waypoint of FPL 0 always stands in the bottom row while the plan is longer than the page, with the waypoints in between scrolled out of view.
</Field>

<Field id="header" title="Column headings">
	<Term t="DIS">`DIS`</Term> is the distance and <Term t="ETE">`ETE`</Term> the estimated time en route. The waypoint in the top row of the left page gets no value.
</Field>

<Field id="active" title="The active waypoint">
	The distance in NM from your present position to the waypoint, and the time it takes at your present groundspeed.
</Field>

<Field id="next" title="A later waypoint">
	The distance from your present position to the waypoint along the flight plan, and the time at your present groundspeed.
</Field>

<Field id="total" title="The last waypoint">
	The distance and the time to the last waypoint. The distance includes the legs of the waypoints that are out of view, so that it is the distance to the end of the flight plan.
</Field>

<Field id="lname" title="Page identification of the left side">
	The page identification on the left side. FPL 0 is the active flight plan.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation, which shows the mode of the unit. <Term t="ENR-LEG">`ENR-LEG`</Term> is Enroute-Leg mode.
</Field>

<Field id="rname" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification on the right side.
</Field>
