<Field id="plan" title="Flight plan on the left page">
	The waypoints of FPL 0. With a single waypoint and no direct-to the unit has no leg to fly, so the page shows no active leg symbol.
</Field>

<Field id="header" title="Column headings">
	`DIS` is the distance and <Term t="ETE">`ETE`</Term> the estimated time en route.
</Field>

<Field id="dashes" title="Waypoint without an active waypoint">
	The row of a waypoint shows dashes in both columns while the unit has no [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint), because the column has no active waypoint to count from.
</Field>

<Field id="lname" title="Page identification of the left side">
	The page identification on the left side. FPL 0 is the active flight plan.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation, which shows the mode of the unit. <Term t="ENR-LEG">`ENR-LEG`</Term> is Enroute-Leg mode.
</Field>

<Field id="rname" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification on the right side.
</Field>
