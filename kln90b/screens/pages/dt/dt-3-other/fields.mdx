<Field id="active" title="The active waypoint">
	The arrow marks the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint) and flashes during [waypoint alerting](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#waypoint-alerting). The number is the waypoint's place in FPL 0 and is blank for a waypoint outside it. The identifier follows.
</Field>

<Field id="active-dis" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	The distance in NM from your present position to the active waypoint. It shows dashes while the unit has no active waypoint or no position.
</Field>

<Field id="active-dtk" title="Desired track of the active leg">
	The <Term t="DTK">desired track</Term> of the active leg as a magnetic course; in OBS mode the selected course, and during a direct-to the course of the direct-to. It shows dashes while the unit has no active waypoint and while the unit flags its navigation.
</Field>

<Field id="next" title="The next waypoint">
	The number in FPL 0 and the identifier of the next waypoint, the one after the active waypoint. These three rows are blank while the active waypoint is the last waypoint of FPL 0, while it is a direct-to waypoint outside the flight plan and while the unit has no active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="next-dis" title="Distance to the next waypoint">
	The distance in NM from your present position to the next waypoint, by way of the active waypoint: the distance to the active waypoint plus the length of the leg from it.
</Field>

<Field id="next-dtk" title="Desired track of the next leg">
	The magnetic course of the leg from the active waypoint to the next waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="rname" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification on the right side. Beside a page that is not an FPL page, D/T 3 shows the active waypoint and the next waypoint; beside an FPL page it lines its rows up with the rows of that page.
</Field>
