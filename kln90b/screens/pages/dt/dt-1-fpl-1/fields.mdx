<Field id="top" title="Questions of the numbered flight plan">
	The top line of a numbered flight plan, which the left cursor reaches to activate or invert it. D/T 1 gives no value in this row.
</Field>

<Field id="plan" title="Flight plan on the left page">
	The waypoints of FPL 1, one per row and numbered from 1, in the same rows as the values of D/T 1. A numbered flight plan has no active leg, so it shows no active leg symbol.
</Field>

<Field id="header" title="Column headings">
	`DIS` is the distance and `ETE` the estimated time en route. Beside a numbered flight plan only `DIS` has values; the column of `ETE` stays blank, because the aircraft does not fly this plan.
</Field>

<Field id="dis" title="Distance from the first waypoint">
	The distance in NM from the first waypoint of the flight plan to the waypoint in the same row, added up along the legs in straight lines. The row of the first waypoint is blank. The distance does not depend on your position.
</Field>

<Field id="lname" title="Page identification of the left side">
	The page identification on the left side. FPL 1 is the first numbered flight plan.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation, which shows the mode of the unit. <Term t="ENR-LEG">`ENR-LEG`</Term> is Enroute-Leg mode.
</Field>

<Field id="rname" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification on the right side.
</Field>
