<Field id="dest" title="The destination waypoint">
	The identifier of the destination waypoint, the last waypoint of the active flight plan or, with an approach in FPL 0, its <Term t="MAP">missed approach point</Term>; with a direct-to outside the flight plan, the direct-to waypoint. While the destination waypoint is the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint), an arrow stands in front of it, and during a direct-to to the destination waypoint the Direct To symbol stands there instead. The identifier is blank while the unit has no destination waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="zone" title="Time zone">
	The time zone of the departure time, the present time and the ETA. The right <kbd>CRSR</kbd> puts the cursor on it, and the right inner knob chooses another zone, which applies at once. It is the time zone of every page that shows a time ([Trip planning and calculators](/kln90b/operation/trip-planning/)).
</Field>

<Field id="dep" title="Departure time">
	`DEP` is the time at which the flight timer started. It shows dashes until then, and the unit keeps the time until it is turned off.
</Field>

<Field id="time" title="Present time">
	`TIME` is the present time, as hours and minutes of a 24-hour clock.
</Field>

<Field id="eta" title="ETA at the destination waypoint">
	`ETA` is the estimated time of arrival at the destination waypoint: the present time plus the estimated time en route below. It shows dashes while the unit has no estimated time en route ([Dashes and blank rows](/kln90b/pages/dt/#dashes-and-blank-rows)) or no destination waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="flt" title="Flight time">
	`FLT` is the time the flight timer has counted, as hours and minutes; `:01` is one minute. It reads `:00` after power-on, and nothing on the unit resets it.
</Field>

<Field id="ete" title="Time to the destination waypoint">
	`ETE` is the estimated time en route to the destination waypoint at your present groundspeed, along the flight plan from the active waypoint. From one hour on it shows the hours (`2:28`).
</Field>

<Field id="rname" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification on the right side. D/T 4 looks the same beside every left page.
</Field>
