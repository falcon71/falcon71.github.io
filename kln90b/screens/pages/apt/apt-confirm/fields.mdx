<Field id="dto-title" title="Direct To page heading">
	`DIRECT TO:` heads the Direct To page.
</Field>

<Field id="dto-ident" title="Direct-to waypoint">
	The identifier of the waypoint to fly direct to, under the left cursor. The first <kbd>ENT</kbd> shows the waypoint page on the right side for approval; the second approves it and starts the direct-to.
</Field>

<Field id="ident" title="Identifier of the airport shown for approval">
	The identifier of the airport on the waypoint page shown for approval. The cursor cannot change it here.
</Field>

<Field id="name" title="Airport name">
	The name of the airport in two rows of 11 characters. The unit shows only letters, digits, blanks and hyphens and drops every other character, such as `/`, without leaving a blank. A name of more than 22 characters loses the words `CITY OF`, `GREATER` and `THE` and gets short forms (`NORTH` becomes `N`, `INTERNATIONAL` becomes `INT`, `REGIONAL` becomes `REG`, `SAINT` becomes `ST`, among others); the first word keeps its full form unless it is longer than 8 characters; what is still longer than 22 characters is cut off. The break between the rows falls after the eleventh character, in the middle of a word if need be.

	A user-defined airport has no name, and the rows stay blank.
</Field>

<Field id="class" title="Airspace and type of the airport">
	The row under the name. On the left it shows `CL B` or `CL C` when the airport lies inside the lateral outline of a class B or class C airspace, and `TMA` or `CTA` when it lies inside an airspace whose name contains TMA (terminal area) or CTA (control area). Class B takes precedence over class C, class C over `TMA`, and `TMA` over `CTA`. Airspaces of other classes show nothing. At the right end of the row the unit shows `MILTRY` for a military airport and `PRIVAT` for a private one; a public airport shows nothing there.

	The unit compares only the outline on the map and not the floor of the airspace, so an airport below a class B shelf also shows `CL B`. The row is blank for a user-defined airport, and it can stay blank for a moment after the page appears.
</Field>

<Field id="pos" title="Airport position">
	The position of the airport: latitude above, longitude below, as `N 47°26.99'` and `W122°18.71'` (degrees, minutes and hundredths of a minute).
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	`CRSR` replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation, which shows the mode of the unit. `ENR-LEG` is Enroute-Leg mode.
</Field>

<Field id="ent" title="Enter prompt">
	`ENT` flashes while the unit waits for <kbd>ENT</kbd> to approve what it shows, such as a waypoint page shown for approval.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side. The right inner knob turns to the other APT pages of the airport before you approve it.
</Field>
