<Field id="ident" title="Airport identifier">
	The identifier of the airport. On a nearest page it is an airport of the [nearest list](/kln90b/operation/nearest/).
</Field>

<Field id="rank" title="Rank in the nearest list">
	The rank of the airport in the list of the nearest airports, shown as `NR` (for nearest) and a number, from `NR 1` for the nearest to `NR 9`. The rank flashes while the cursor is elsewhere. The number changes when another airport comes nearer, and it disappears a few seconds after the airport has dropped out of the list.

	With the cursor on the rank, the page keeps showing whichever airport holds that rank, so it follows the list as the aircraft moves.
</Field>

<Field id="name" title="Airport name">
	The name of the airport, in two rows of 11 characters.
</Field>

<Field id="length" title="Length of the longest runway">
	The length of the longest runway in feet, as a whole number of feet, followed by `'`.
</Field>

<Field id="surface" title="Surface of the longest runway">
	The surface of the longest runway as a three-letter code. The codes are the ones that APT 3 uses: `HRD` for a hard surface, `TRF` for turf, and so on.
</Field>

<Field id="lighting" title="Lighting of the longest runway">
	The lighting of the longest runway: `L` for a runway lighted from sunset to sunrise, `LPC` for pilot-controlled lighting, which you turn on by radio, `LPT` for part-time lighting. The field is blank when the runway has no lighting or the lighting is not known.
</Field>

<Field id="bearing" title="Bearing to the airport">
	The magnetic bearing from the aircraft to the airport, in three digits and followed by `TO` (`002°TO`). It shows `---°TO` while the aircraft is exactly over the airport.
</Field>

<Field id="distance" title="Distance to the airport">
	The distance from the aircraft to the airport in NM, with one decimal place and a leading zero below 10 NM (`03.3NM`).
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side.
</Field>
