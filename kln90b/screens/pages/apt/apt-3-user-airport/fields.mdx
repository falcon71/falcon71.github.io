<Field id="ident" title="Identifier of the user-defined airport">
	The identifier of a [user-defined airport](/kln90b/operation/user-waypoints/).
</Field>

<Field id="title" title="Runway of the user-defined airport">
	`RWY LEN` labels the one runway that a user-defined airport has. Its length and surface are the two entries below it.
</Field>

<Field id="length" title="Runway length of the user-defined airport">
	The length of the runway in feet. It shows `_____` until you enter a value. The range is 0 to 99,999 ft, in steps of 1 ft. With the cursor on the field, the right inner knob starts the entry and changes a digit, the right outer knob moves between the digits, and <kbd>ENT</kbd> stores the length and moves the cursor to the surface. Whether the airport is in the nearest list depends on it ([the criteria](/kln90b/operation/nearest/#setting-the-nearest-airport-criteria)).
</Field>

<Field id="surface" title="Runway surface of the user-defined airport">
	The surface of the runway: `HRD` for a hard surface or `SFT` for a soft one. The right inner knob switches between the two with the cursor on the field, and <kbd>ENT</kbd> stores the choice and leaves the cursor on it. A soft runway shows as `TRF` in the nearest list of APT 1. It also decides whether the airport is in the nearest list ([the criteria](/kln90b/operation/nearest/#setting-the-nearest-airport-criteria)).
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side.
</Field>
