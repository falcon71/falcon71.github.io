<Field id="ident" title="Airport identifier">
	The identifier of the airport. The airport remarks belong to this identifier, so they show whenever the airport is shown, also for a user-defined airport.
</Field>

<Field id="title" title="Airport remarks heading">
	`REMARKS:` heads the three lines in which you can write a note about the airport.
</Field>

<Field id="remark" title="Remark line">
	One of the three lines of airport remarks. You write the lines yourself, and the unit keeps them when it is off.

	The entry takes effect with <kbd>ENT</kbd> ([Entries that take effect with ENT](/kln90b/unit/controls-and-display/#entries-that-take-effect-with-ent)). The line shows `A` in its first cell and `_` in the others, also when it already holds text, which the entry replaces. <kbd>ENT</kbd> stores the line, turns cells you did not touch into blanks and moves the cursor to the next line; on the last line the cursor stays. <kbd>CLR</kbd> with an entry open cancels it and keeps the old text.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side.
</Field>
