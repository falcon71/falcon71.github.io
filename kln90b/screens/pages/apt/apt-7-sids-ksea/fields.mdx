<Field id="ident" title="Airport identifier">
	The airport whose SIDs and STARs APT 7 shows, the same airport as on the other APT pages. To change it,
	turn the right outer knob counterclockwise from the first entry of the list until the cursor is on the identifier,
	and enter the new one. On ACT 7 the identifier cannot be changed.
</Field>

<Field id="heading" title="List heading">
	`SELECT SID` heads the list of the airport's <Term t="SID" />s. On the page of STARs it reads
	`SELECT STAR`.
</Field>

<Field id="bangr9" title="SID under the cursor">
	A SID with its number in the list. The cursor marks the whole entry. Press <kbd>ENT</kbd> to select the
	SID; the unit then asks for its runway or its transition, or shows its waypoints when there is nothing to ask.
</Field>

<Field id="sids" title="SIDs in the list">
	The SIDs the unit offers for the airport, numbered from 1 in the order of the database. The list has four rows;
	turn the right outer knob to move the cursor through it, which scrolls the rows above the bottom one. SIDs the
	unit cannot fly are not listed and get no number.
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Last SID in the list">
	The bottom row always shows the last SID of the list, so its number tells you how many there are. It keeps
	that entry while the rows above it scroll, until the cursor reaches the end of the list.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	`CRSR` shows that the right cursor is on. With the cursor off, this field shows the page identification, `APT+7` or `APT 7`.
</Field>
