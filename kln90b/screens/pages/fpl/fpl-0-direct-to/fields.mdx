<Field id="before" title="Waypoint before the direct-to waypoint">
	A waypoint of FPL 0 in front of the direct-to waypoint. It has no symbol: a direct-to starts where you made it, not at a waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="to" title="Direct-to waypoint">
	The waypoint of FPL 0 that you fly direct to, with the arrow in front of it in place of the head of the active leg symbol. The arrow flashes during [waypoint alerting](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#waypoint-alerting). No waypoint has a tail.
</Field>

<Field id="rest" title="The other waypoints">
	The other rows of FPL 0: the first waypoint, and the blank waypoint position at the end.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`FPL 0`, the active flight plan.
</Field>
