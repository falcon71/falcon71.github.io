<Field id="from" title="From waypoint">
	The waypoint that the active leg starts from, the from waypoint, with the tail of the active leg symbol in front of it, its number in FPL 0, a colon and its identifier. The number counts the legs of FPL 0 from 1.
</Field>

<Field id="to" title="Active waypoint">
	The [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint), the one the unit flies to, with the head of the active leg symbol in front of it. The head flashes during [waypoint alerting](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#waypoint-alerting).
</Field>

<Field id="wpt" title="Waypoint of the flight plan">
	A waypoint of FPL 0 that is not an end of the active leg: its number, a colon for a waypoint that you entered, and its identifier.
</Field>

<Field id="blank" title="Blank waypoint position">
	The blank waypoint position: the next number, with a blank identifier. A waypoint that you add at the end of FPL 0 goes here.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`FPL 0`, the active flight plan: the flight plan that the unit flies.
</Field>
