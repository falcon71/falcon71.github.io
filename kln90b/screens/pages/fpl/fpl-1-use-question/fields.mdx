<Field id="use" title="Question to activate the flight plan">
	`USE?` flashes while the cursor is on it. <kbd>ENT</kbd> copies this flight plan into FPL 0 in its own order and shows FPL 0 with the cursor off ([Activating a flight plan](/kln90b/operation/flight-plans/#activating-a-flight-plan)).
</Field>

<Field id="invert" title="Rest of the top line">
	`INVRT?` is the rest of the top line `USE? INVRT?`. The cursor marks it together with `USE?` one step of the left outer knob further.
</Field>

<Field id="plan" title="Waypoints of the flight plan">
	The waypoints of the numbered flight plan in flight order.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>
