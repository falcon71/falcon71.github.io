<Field id="first" title="Waypoint of the flight plan">
	A waypoint of FPL 0. While an entry is open, the page shows no active leg symbol.
</Field>

<Field id="entry" title="New waypoint">
	The entry of a new waypoint in front of the waypoint that the cursor was on, opened by a step of the left inner knob. The entry is not part of the flight plan until you approve its waypoint page with <kbd>ENT</kbd>. <kbd>CLR</kbd> blanks the identifier, and the new row goes when you then press <kbd>ENT</kbd> or turn the cursor off.
</Field>

<Field id="rest" title="The other waypoints">
	The waypoints behind the new one, each with its number moved up by one, and the blank waypoint position at the end.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>
