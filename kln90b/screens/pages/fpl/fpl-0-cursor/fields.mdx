<Field id="wpt" title="Waypoint under the cursor">
	The waypoint that the left cursor marks, here the first of FPL 0, with the tail of the active leg symbol in front of it. The left outer knob moves the cursor from waypoint to waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="rest" title="The other waypoints">
	The other waypoints of FPL 0 in flight order, with the head of the active leg symbol in front of the active waypoint, and the blank waypoint position at the end, where the next waypoint is added.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>
