<Field id="top" title="Questions to activate the flight plan">
	`USE? INVRT?`, the top line of a flight plan that has waypoints.
</Field>

<Field id="plan" title="First waypoints">
	The first waypoints of the flight plan. With the cursor off, a numbered flight plan shows its first four waypoints; the cursor scrolls the list.
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Last waypoint">
	The last waypoint of the flight plan. It stays in the bottom row, so that you see where the flight plan ends. Its number is its place in the plan, so it tells how many waypoints the plan holds.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of a numbered flight plan. From `FPL10` on, the status line writes the number without a space.
</Field>
