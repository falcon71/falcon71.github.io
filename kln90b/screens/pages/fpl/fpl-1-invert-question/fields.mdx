	`USE? INVRT?` flashes while the cursor is on it. <kbd>ENT</kbd> copies this flight plan into FPL 0 in reverse order and shows FPL 0 with the cursor off ([Activating a flight plan inverted](/kln90b/operation/flight-plans/#activating-a-flight-plan-inverted)).

</Field>
