	The <Term t="MAP" /> of the approach, here the runway, with the suffix `-M`.
</Field>

<Field id="noseq" title="No waypoint sequencing">
	The fence: `*NO WPT SEQ` follows the missed approach point. The unit does not sequence on to the waypoints behind it by itself ([When the unit does not sequence](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#when-the-unit-does-not-sequence)).
</Field>

<Field id="missed" title="Waypoints of the missed approach">
	The waypoints of the missed approach, each with its number in the flight plan.
</Field>

<Field id="mahp" title="Missed approach holding point">
	The waypoint under the left cursor, here the <Term t="MAHP" />, where the missed approach ends, with the suffix `-H`.
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Last waypoint">
	The last waypoint of FPL 0, here the destination airport. While the list is longer than the page, the last waypoint stays in the bottom row and the rows between scroll.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>
