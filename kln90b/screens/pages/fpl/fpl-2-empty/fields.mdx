<Field id="load" title="Question to load FPL 0">
	`LOAD FPL 0?` stands on the top line of a numbered flight plan that holds no waypoint. <kbd>ENT</kbd> on it copies FPL 0 into this flight plan ([Storing FPL 0](/kln90b/operation/flight-plans/#storing-fpl-0)).
</Field>

<Field id="blank" title="Blank waypoint position">
	The blank waypoint position, with a blank identifier. The left cursor comes on here, and a waypoint that you enter goes here.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of a numbered flight plan: `FPL` and its number. From 10 on, the status line writes the number without a space (`FPL10`).
</Field>
