<Field id="top" title="Questions to activate the flight plan">
	`USE? INVRT?`, the top line of a flight plan that has waypoints. The cursor marks `USE?` alone, which activates the flight plan in its own order, or the whole line, which activates it inverted.
</Field>

<Field id="plan" title="Waypoints of the flight plan">
	The waypoints of the numbered flight plan in flight order, each with its number and a colon. Only FPL 0 draws the active leg symbol.
</Field>

<Field id="blank" title="Blank waypoint position">
	The blank waypoint position, with a blank identifier. A waypoint that you add at the end goes here. A full flight plan has none.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of a numbered flight plan: `FPL` and its number.
</Field>
