<Field id="from" title="From waypoint">
	The waypoint that the active leg starts from, with the tail of the active leg symbol in front of it. The leg runs through the header of the procedure below it to the first waypoint of the procedure.
</Field>

<Field id="header" title="Procedure header">
	The header of a SID: the name of the <Term t="SID" />, a dash and `SID`. The shaft of the active leg symbol in front of it shows that the active leg runs through this header, from the waypoint with the tail to the waypoint with the head. A header counts as no waypoint and has no number.
</Field>

<Field id="to" title="Active waypoint">
	The first waypoint of the <Term t="SID" />, which is the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint), with the head of the active leg symbol in front of it. A period in place of the colon shows that the waypoint belongs to a SID or a <Term t="STAR" />.
</Field>

<Field id="sid" title="Waypoints of the SID">
	The next waypoints of the <Term t="SID" />, each with its number in the flight plan and a period.
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Last waypoint">
	The last waypoint of FPL 0. While the list is longer than the page, the last waypoint stays in the bottom row and the rows between scroll. The numbers count every leg of the flight plan, the legs of procedures included.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`FPL 0`, the active flight plan.
</Field>
