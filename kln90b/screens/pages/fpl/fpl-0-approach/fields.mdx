<Field id="header" title="Approach header">
	The header of an approach: a letter for the type of the approach (`R` for an <Term t="RNAV" /> approach), the runway, a dash and the airport. An approach header is a line of its own, as the headers of <Term t="SID">SIDs</Term> and <Term t="STAR">STARs</Term> are.
</Field>

<Field id="start" title="Waypoints of the approach">
	The waypoints of the approach, each with its number in the flight plan. A waypoint of an approach has a blank in place of the colon, and the suffix after the identifier marks the role of the waypoint: `-I` the <Term t="IAF" />, `-F` the <Term t="FAF" />, `-M` the <Term t="MAP" /> and `-H` the <Term t="MAHP" />. The cursor can rest on these waypoints, but it cannot change them.
</Field>

<Field id="wpt" title="Waypoint under the cursor">
	The waypoint of the approach that the left cursor marks, here the final approach fix with its suffix `-F`.
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Last waypoint">
	The last waypoint of FPL 0, here the destination airport. While the list is longer than the page, the last waypoint stays in the bottom row and the rows between scroll.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>
