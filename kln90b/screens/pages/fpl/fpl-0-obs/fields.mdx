<Field id="first" title="Waypoint before the active waypoint">
	A waypoint of FPL 0 in front of the active waypoint. In OBS mode no waypoint has a tail, because the selected course passes through the active waypoint from no waypoint in particular.
</Field>

<Field id="to" title="Active waypoint in OBS mode">
	The [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint), with the arrow in front of it in place of the head of the active leg symbol. The arrow flashes during [waypoint alerting](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#waypoint-alerting).
</Field>

<Field id="rest" title="The other waypoints">
	The remaining waypoints of FPL 0 and the blank waypoint position at the end.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`FPL 0`, the active flight plan.
</Field>
