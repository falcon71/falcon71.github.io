<Field id="ident" title="Identifier of the center waypoint">
	The identifier of the center waypoint: the identifier of the nearest VOR and a two-digit number. `new` follows it for a waypoint that the computation created.
</Field>

<Field id="centers" title="The two Centers">
	The Center that the route leaves and the Center that it enters at the waypoint, each as the unit abbreviates it, followed by `CTR`.
</Field>

<Field id="vor" title="Reference VOR and radial">
	The VOR the waypoint is measured from, and the radial from it to the waypoint, in degrees magnetic. The radial is converted with the published magnetic variation of the VOR.
</Field>

<Field id="dis" title="Distance from the VOR">
	The distance from the VOR to the waypoint in NM, with one decimal below 100 NM and in whole NM from there on.
</Field>

<Field id="lat" title="Latitude of the center waypoint">
	The latitude of the waypoint in degrees, minutes and hundredths of a minute.
</Field>

<Field id="lon" title="Longitude of the center waypoint">
	The longitude of the waypoint in degrees, minutes and hundredths of a minute.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side. `CTR+2` shows that CTR 2 has more than one page, one for each center waypoint.
</Field>
