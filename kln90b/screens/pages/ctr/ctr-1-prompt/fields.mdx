<Field id="use" title="Questions to activate the plan">
	The two questions on the top line of a numbered flight plan that holds waypoints: `USE?` activates the plan in its own order and `INVRT?` activates it inverted ([`USE? INVRT?`](/kln90b/pages/fpl/#use-invrt)).
</Field>

<Field id="plan" title="Waypoints of the plan">
	The waypoints of the flight plan on the left side, the plan that CTR 1 computes the center waypoints for.
</Field>

<Field id="blank" title="Blank waypoint position">
	The blank waypoint position after the last waypoint, where a waypoint is added at the end.
</Field>

<Field id="prompt" title="Prompt to compute the center waypoints">
	<kbd>ENT</kbd> starts the computation of the center waypoints for the flight plan on the left side.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation, which shows the mode of the unit. `ENR-LEG` is Enroute-Leg mode.
</Field>

<Field id="ent" title="Enter prompt">
	`ENT` flashes while the unit waits for <kbd>ENT</kbd>, on CTR 1, to start the computation of the center waypoints.
</Field>

<Field id="left-page" title="Page identification of the left side">
	The page identification on the left side. FPL 1 is a numbered flight plan.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side. CTR 1 computes center waypoints for the flight plan on the left side.
</Field>
