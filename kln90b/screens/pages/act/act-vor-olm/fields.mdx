<Field id="arrow" title="Active waypoint arrow">
	The arrow marks the VOR as the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint). It flashes during [waypoint alerting](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#waypoint-alerting). When the page shows another waypoint of FPL 0, the arrow is gone.
</Field>

<Field id="number" title="Number in the flight plan">
	The number of the VOR in the active flight plan, the same number that FPL 0 shows in front of it. It is blank when the VOR is a direct-to waypoint that is not in FPL 0.

	With the right inner knob pulled out, the page steps through the waypoints of FPL 0 instead of through the database, and the number follows. The scan stops at the first and at the last waypoint of FPL 0.
</Field>

<Field id="ident" title="VOR identifier">
	The identifier of the VOR that the ACT page shows. It cannot be changed here.
</Field>

<Field id="dme" title="DME of the station">
	`D` means that the station has distance measuring equipment: a VOR/DME, a VORTAC or a DME without a VOR. A station without DME shows a blank. A user-defined VOR never shows `D`, because the unit does not know whether it has DME.
</Field>

<Field id="type" title="Waypoint type">
	`V` for VOR, the waypoint type the ACT page shows.
</Field>

<Field id="name" title="Station name">
	The name of the station, cut to 11 characters. A user-defined VOR has no name, and the row stays blank.
</Field>

<Field id="class" title="Class of the station">
	The class of the station: `T` for a terminal VOR, `L` for a low altitude VOR and `H` for a high altitude VOR. A station of another class, and every user-defined VOR, shows `U` for undefined.
</Field>

<Field id="freq" title="Frequency">
	The frequency of the station in MHz with two decimals. A user-defined VOR shows dashes until you have entered a frequency.
</Field>

<Field id="magvar" title="Published magnetic variation">
	The magnetic variation that the station carries, in whole degrees and followed by `E` or `W`. It can differ from the variation that the unit uses at the position of the aircraft. A user-defined VOR shows dashes until you have entered one.
</Field>

<Field id="lat" title="VOR latitude">
	The latitude of the VOR, as `N 46°58.30'` (degrees, minutes and hundredths of a minute).
</Field>

<Field id="lon" title="VOR longitude">
	The longitude of the VOR, as `W122°54.11'`.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side. The ACT page of a VOR is a single page and reads `ACT`.
</Field>
