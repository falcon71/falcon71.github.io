<Field id="ident" title="Intersection identifier">
	The identifier of the intersection. A `›` in front of it means that the intersection is the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint). With the right cursor on, you change the identifier as on every waypoint page ([Entering an identifier](/kln90b/operation/waypoints/#entering-an-identifier)); an identifier that no intersection has offers to create a [user-defined intersection](/kln90b/operation/user-waypoints/).
</Field>

<Field id="ref" title="Reference waypoint">
	The waypoint that `RAD` and `DIS` are measured from. The unit fills it in with the VOR nearest to the intersection, which it finds after a search; the field shows dashes until then, and when it finds none ([The reference waypoint](/kln90b/pages/int/#the-reference-waypoint)).

	With the cursor on, you can enter another waypoint of any type as the reference. The unit shows its waypoint page for approval, and the page then shows the radial and the distance from it. The reference is not stored.
</Field>

<Field id="rad" title="Radial from the reference waypoint">
	The magnetic course from the reference waypoint to the intersection, in degrees and tenths, from `000.0` to `359.9`. It is magnetic with the variation of the reference: the published variation of a VOR, and for any other waypoint the variation at its position. The radial of an intersection of the database cannot be changed.
</Field>

<Field id="dis" title="Distance from the reference waypoint">
	The distance from the reference waypoint to the intersection in NM, to a tenth. From 1,000 NM on, the unit shows whole NM. The distance of an intersection of the database cannot be changed.
</Field>

<Field id="lat" title="Latitude of the intersection">
	The latitude of the intersection, in degrees and minutes to hundredths. It cannot be changed for an intersection of the database.
</Field>

<Field id="lon" title="Longitude of the intersection">
	The longitude of the intersection, in degrees and minutes to hundredths. It cannot be changed for an intersection of the database.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side.
</Field>
