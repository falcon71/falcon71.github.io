<Field id="orientation" title="Map orientation">
	The map orientation: which direction points to the top of the map. `N^` means north up; the unit draws the `^` as an arrow that points up. Desired track up (`DTK^`), actual track up (`TK^`) and heading up (`HDG^`) show the direction that is up instead, as a magnetic course in whole degrees. With the cursor on the field, the inner knob chooses between `N^`, `DTK^`, `TK^` and, where the unit has a heading, `HDG^`, and the map follows at once. [The map orientation](/kln90b/pages/nav/#the-map-orientation) explains the choices.
</Field>

<Field id="range" title="Range scale">
	The range scale in NM: the distance from the aircraft to the top of the map. With the cursor on the field, the inner knob steps through the range scales from 1 NM to 1,000 NM, wraps around, and the map follows at once. [The range scale](/kln90b/pages/nav/#the-range-scale) lists them.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	While the cursor of the left side is on, <Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> stands in place of its page identification ([The status line](/kln90b/unit/controls-and-display/#the-status-line)).
</Field>

<Field id="leg" title="Direct-to course">
	The course of a [direct-to](/kln90b/operation/direct-to/): an arrow from the point where you made the direct-to to the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="active-waypoint" title="Active waypoint">
	The [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint) as a star. The unit draws the star for an active waypoint that FPL 0 does not hold; the waypoints of FPL 0 carry their numbers instead ([What NAV 5 draws](/kln90b/pages/nav/#what-nav-5-draws)).
</Field>

<Field id="aircraft" title="Aircraft">
	The aircraft: a diamond in north up and desired track up, an aircraft symbol in actual track up and heading up. In north up it is in the middle of the map, in the other map orientations three quarters of the way down.
</Field>
