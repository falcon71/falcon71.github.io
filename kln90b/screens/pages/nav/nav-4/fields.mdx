<Field id="title" title="VNAV state">
	The state of VNAV, in the top line of the page: `VNV INACTV` while VNAV is inactive. [Flying the vertical path](/kln90b/operation/vnav/#flying-the-vertical-path) says what each state means.
</Field>

<Field id="ind" title="Indicated altitude">
	The indicated altitude from the altitude input of the aircraft, with the baro setting applied, rounded to 100 ft. It shows five digits and `00000` below 0 ft, and you cannot change it.
</Field>

<Field id="sel" title="Selected altitude">
	The altitude to climb or descend to, in steps of 100 ft from 0 to 99,900 ft. The cursor stops on the ten-thousands, thousands and hundreds digits, and a digit takes effect at once. Changing it stops VNAV. The altitude alert reads the selected altitude too ([Altitude alerting](/kln90b/operation/alerts/#altitude-alerting)).
</Field>

<Field id="wpt" title="VNAV waypoint">
	The waypoint the descent or climb ends at, the active waypoint until you enter another one. [Which waypoint VNAV can use](/kln90b/operation/vnav/#which-waypoint-vnav-can-use) says which waypoints the field takes. A different waypoint stops VNAV.
</Field>

<Field id="dist" title="Offset">
	The distance before the VNAV waypoint where the descent or climb should end, in NM from 0 to 99. The cursor stops on the tens and units digits. Changing it stops VNAV.
</Field>

<Field id="angle" title="Angle">
	The angle of the vertical path, negative for a descent, in tenths of a degree up to 9.9°. Before VNAV starts it shows the angle that takes you from the altitude above to the selected altitude at the offset point. [The angle](/kln90b/operation/vnav/#the-angle) and [Starting VNAV](/kln90b/operation/vnav/#starting-vnav) say the rest.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the side that shows the page: `NAV 4`. NAV 4 is the same page on the left and on the right side.
</Field>
