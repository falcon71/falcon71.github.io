<Field id="from" title="From waypoint">
	The waypoint at the start of the active leg. On a direct-to, the leg starts where you started the direct-to, and the field shows a `D` right in front of the arrow.
</Field>

<Field id="arrow" title="Active leg arrow">
	The `›` between the two waypoints marks the leg you are flying. It points from the waypoint on the left to the active waypoint on the right.
</Field>

<Field id="to" title="Active waypoint">
	The waypoint you are flying to. The page measures distance, groundspeed, time and bearing to it. It is blank when there is no active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="cdi" title="D-bar">
	The D-bar is a scale of dots with a bar that shows how far you are from the desired track; the center of the scale is on course. The ends of the scale are full deflection at the [CDI scale factor](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-cdi-scale-factor), which you select on MOD 1 and MOD 2 and the unit changes for approaches. The TO/FROM indicator in the center shows whether you fly to or from the active waypoint. An inverse `F L A G` replaces the middle of the scale when the unit has no valid navigation.
</Field>

<Field id="dis" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	<Term t="DIS">`DIS`</Term> is the distance to the active waypoint, in NM. It shows one decimal below 100 NM and whole numbers from 100 NM on, and dashes when the unit has no distance.
</Field>

<Field id="gs" title="Groundspeed">
	Your groundspeed in kt, from the GPS. It shows dashes until the unit is NAV ready.
</Field>

<Field id="ete" title="Estimated time en route">
	The time to the active waypoint at your present groundspeed, as hours and minutes. Below one hour the hours stay blank (`:22` is 22 minutes). It shows `--:--` when the unit cannot calculate it.
</Field>

<Field id="brg" title="Bearing to the active waypoint">
	<Term t="BRG">`BRG`</Term> is the magnetic bearing from your position to the active waypoint, in degrees. It shows `---°` when the unit has no bearing.
</Field>

<Field id="ident" title="Airport identifier">
	The identifier of the airport. A `›` in front of it means that the airport is the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint). With the right cursor on, you change it with the right knobs.
</Field>

<Field id="name" title="Airport name">
	The name of the airport in two rows. A user-defined airport has no name, and the rows stay blank.
</Field>

<Field id="pos" title="Airport position">
	The position of the airport: latitude above, longitude below, as `N 47°26.99'` and `W122°18.71'` (degrees, minutes and hundredths of a minute). For an airport of the database the position cannot be changed.
</Field>

<Field id="class" title="Airspace and type of the airport">
	The airspace the airport lies in (`CL B` and `CL C` for class B and class C airspace, `TMA` for a terminal area, `CTA` for a control area) on the left, and `MILTRY` for a military airport or `PRIVAT` for a private one on the right where they apply. The row is blank for a user-defined airport.
</Field>

<Field id="lpage" title="Page identification of the left side">
	The page identification on the left side. A `+` in place of the space (`STA+1`) means that the page has more than one page with this page name. While the left cursor is on, <Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation, which shows the mode of the unit. <Term t="ENR-LEG">`ENR-LEG`</Term> is Enroute-Leg mode: en route navigation along a leg, from one waypoint to the next. In [OBS mode](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/) the field shows <Term t="ENR:">`ENR:`</Term> and the course you selected, and for approaches <Term t="ARM-LEG">`ARM-LEG`</Term>, <Term t="ARM:">`ARM:`</Term> or <Term t="APR-LEG">`APR-LEG`</Term>. While the unit shows a short message, such as `NO SUCH WPT`, it replaces this field and the next one ([The status line](/kln90b/unit/controls-and-display/#the-status-line)).
</Field>

<Field id="prompt" title="Message and enter prompt">
	The three cells after the mode annunciation are blank while the unit holds no message and nothing waits for <kbd>ENT</kbd>. <Term t="MSG">`MSG`</Term> in inverse video means that the unit holds a message; it flashes while you have not read all of them. A flashing <Term t="ENT">`ENT`</Term> means that <kbd>ENT</kbd> accepts what you have entered.
</Field>

<Field id="rpage" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification on the right side. A `+` in place of the space (`APT+3`) means that the page has more than one page with this page name. While the right cursor is on, <Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification.
</Field>
