<Field id="dtk" title="Desired track">
	<p><Term t="DTK">`DTK`</Term> is the magnetic course of the active leg at the point abeam the aircraft, in whole degrees. It shows `---°` without a position or an active waypoint, and in approach active mode when the unit has lost its integrity. The unit gives it with the magnetic variation at the aircraft, not a VOR's published variation, so a leg along a charted VOR radial can read a few degrees off it ([Magnetic variation](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#magnetic-variation)). The cursor does not stop on it in leg mode.</p>
	<p>The number flashes while an external indicator that the unit reads is more than 10° off it ([The course messages](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-course-messages)).</p>
</Field>

<Field id="tk" title="Actual track">
	`TK` is your actual track, magnetic, in whole degrees. It shows `---°` without a position and at a groundspeed below 2 kt. In [OBS mode](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#obs-mode) the unit uses the magnetic variation of the active waypoint, so that the track compares with the selected course.
</Field>

<Field id="fly" title="Direction to fly and cross-track error">
	<p>The direction to fly to get back on the course, `L` or `R`, and the <Term t="XTK">cross-track error</Term>. `L0.00` is exactly on course. The distance shows hundredths of a NM below 1 NM, tenths from 1 NM and whole NM from 10 NM. During an anticipated turn it refers to the curved path of the [transition](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#turn-anticipation).</p>
	<p>The field shows `FLY - -.-nm` while the unit has no cross-track error: without a position, without an active waypoint, or in approach active mode when the unit has lost its integrity.</p>
</Field>

<Field id="msa" title="Minimum safe altitude">
	The minimum safe altitude of the one-degree sector the aircraft is in, in whole hundreds of feet, with a clearance above the terrain of the sector. It shows `-----` without a position and outside the data of the unit. [Minimum safe altitudes](/kln90b/pages/nav/#minimum-safe-altitudes) explains the sectors.
</Field>

<Field id="esa" title="Minimum en route safe altitude">
	The minimum en route safe altitude: the highest minimum safe altitude of every sector between the aircraft and the active waypoint and, with an active waypoint of FPL 0 in leg mode, along the rest of FPL 0 to the destination or the missed approach point. It is never lower than `MSA`. It shows `-----` without an active waypoint and when a sector lies outside the data of the unit. Without a position it still shows a value, worked out from the last position the unit knew. {/* kln90b#502: describes the current behavior; the fix makes ESA show dashes without a position */} [Minimum safe altitudes](/kln90b/pages/nav/#minimum-safe-altitudes) explains the sectors.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the side that shows the page: `NAV 3`. NAV 3 is the same page on the left and on the right side.
</Field>
