<Field id="orientation" title="Map orientation">
	The map orientation, here actual track up, with dashes in place of the track: the unit has no track below a groundspeed of 2 kt, and in actual track up it draws no map without one ([The map is blank in actual track up](/kln90b/pages/nav/#the-map-is-blank-in-actual-track-up)).
</Field>

<Field id="range" title="Range scale">
	The range scale in NM: the distance from the aircraft to the top of the map. With the cursor on the field, the inner knob steps through the range scales from 1 NM to 1,000 NM, wraps around, and the map follows at once. [The range scale](/kln90b/pages/nav/#the-range-scale) lists them.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the side that shows the page: `NAV 5`. NAV 5 is the same page on the left and on the right side.
</Field>
