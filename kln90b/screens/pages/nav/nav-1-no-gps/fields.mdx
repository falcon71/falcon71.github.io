<Field id="from" title="From waypoint">
	The waypoint at the start of the active leg. After a [direct-to](/kln90b/operation/direct-to/) the Direct To symbol stands in its place, drawn as a small D right in front of the arrow; the leg then starts where you made the direct-to. The field is blank when the leg has no from waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="arrow" title="Active waypoint arrow">
	The `›` marks the waypoint after it as the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint). It flashes during [waypoint alerting](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#waypoint-alerting) and is blank when there is no active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="to" title="Active waypoint">
	The active waypoint. The distance, the time and the bearing of the page refer to it. A waypoint of an approach shows without its suffix; Super NAV 1 shows the suffix. The field is blank when there is no active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="cdi" title="D-bar">
	<p>The D-bar shows how far the aircraft is from the active leg: a bar on a scale of five dots on each side of the center. The outer dot is full deflection, the [CDI scale factor](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-cdi-scale-factor); each dot is a fifth of it, and the bar moves in steps smaller than a dot. The bar stands on the side you must fly to, so a bar left of the center means that the leg lies to your left. Beyond full deflection it stays on the outer dot. During an anticipated turn the bar shows the distance from the curved path of the [transition](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#turn-anticipation).</p>
	<p>The center of the scale is the TO/FROM indicator: it shows whether the active waypoint lies ahead (TO) or behind you (FROM). An inverse `F L A G` replaces the middle of the scale when the unit has no cross-track error: without a position, without an active waypoint, or in approach active mode when the unit has lost its integrity.</p>
</Field>

<Field id="dis" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	<Term t="DIS">`DIS`</Term> is the direct distance to the active waypoint in NM, never the distance along the route or the arc. It shows one decimal below 100 NM and whole NM from 100 NM. It shows `--.-` without a position or an active waypoint, and in approach active mode when the unit has lost its integrity.
</Field>

<Field id="gs" title="Groundspeed">
	<Term t="GS">Your groundspeed</Term> in kt, from the GPS, in whole knots from 0 to 999. It shows dashes without a position.
</Field>

<Field id="ete" title="Estimated time en route">
	The <Term t="ETE">time to the active waypoint</Term> at your present groundspeed. From one hour it shows hours and minutes (`1:05`); below one hour the hours stay blank and `:22` is 22 minutes. It shows `--:--` at a groundspeed of 2 kt or less, without a position or an active waypoint, in approach active mode when the unit has lost its integrity, and from 100 hours.
</Field>

<Field id="brg" title="Bearing to the active waypoint">
	<Term t="BRG">`BRG`</Term> is the magnetic bearing from your position to the active waypoint, in whole degrees. It shows `---°` without a position or an active waypoint, in approach active mode when the unit has lost its integrity, and while the aircraft is exactly on the waypoint. In [OBS mode](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#obs-mode) it is the course to the waypoint as measured at the waypoint, with the magnetic variation of the waypoint, so that it equals the selected course while you are on the course line.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the side that shows the page: `NAV 1`. NAV 1 is the same page on the left and on the right side; with NAV 1 on both sides the unit shows Super NAV 1 on the whole screen.
</Field>
