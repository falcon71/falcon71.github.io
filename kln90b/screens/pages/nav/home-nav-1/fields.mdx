<Field id="from" title="From waypoint">
	The waypoint at the start of the active leg. After a [direct-to](/kln90b/operation/direct-to/) the Direct To symbol stands in its place, drawn as a small D right in front of the arrow; the leg then starts where you made the direct-to. The field is blank when the leg has no from waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="arrow" title="Active waypoint arrow">
	The `›` marks the waypoint after it as the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint). It flashes during [waypoint alerting](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#waypoint-alerting) and is blank when there is no active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="to" title="Active waypoint">
	The active waypoint. The distance, the time and the bearing of the page refer to it. A waypoint of an approach shows without its suffix; Super NAV 1 shows the suffix. The field is blank when there is no active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="cdi" title="D-bar">
	<p>The D-bar shows how far the aircraft is from the active leg: a bar on a scale of five dots on each side of the center. The outer dot is full deflection, the [CDI scale factor](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-cdi-scale-factor); each dot is a fifth of it, and the bar moves in steps smaller than a dot. The bar stands on the side you must fly to, so a bar left of the center means that the leg lies to your left. Beyond full deflection it stays on the outer dot. During an anticipated turn the bar shows the distance from the curved path of the [transition](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#turn-anticipation).</p>
	<p>The center of the scale is the TO/FROM indicator: it shows whether the active waypoint lies ahead (TO) or behind you (FROM). An inverse `F L A G` replaces the middle of the scale when the unit has no cross-track error: without a position, without an active waypoint, or in approach active mode when the unit has lost its integrity.</p>
</Field>

<Field id="dis" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	`DIS` is the direct distance to the active waypoint in NM, never the distance along the route or the arc. It shows one decimal below 100 NM and whole NM from 100 NM. It shows `--.-` without a position or an active waypoint, and in approach active mode when the unit has lost its integrity.
</Field>

<Field id="gs" title="Groundspeed">
	Your groundspeed in kt, from the GPS, in whole knots from 0 to 999. It shows dashes without a position.
</Field>

<Field id="ete" title="Estimated time en route">
	The time to the active waypoint at your present groundspeed. From one hour it shows hours and minutes (`1:05`); below one hour the hours stay blank and `:22` is 22 minutes. It shows `--:--` at a groundspeed of 2 kt or less, without a position or an active waypoint, in approach active mode when the unit has lost its integrity, and from 100 hours.
</Field>

<Field id="brg" title="Bearing to the active waypoint">
	`BRG` is the magnetic bearing from your position to the active waypoint, in whole degrees. It shows `---°` without a position or an active waypoint, in approach active mode when the unit has lost its integrity, and while the aircraft is exactly on the waypoint. In [OBS mode](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#obs-mode) it is the course to the waypoint as measured at the waypoint, with the magnetic variation of the waypoint, so that it equals the selected course while you are on the course line.
</Field>

<Field id="ident" title="Airport identifier">
	The identifier of the airport. A `›` in front of it means that the airport is the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint). With the right cursor on, you change it with the right knobs.
</Field>

<Field id="name" title="Airport name">
	The name of the airport in two rows. A user-defined airport has no name, and the rows stay blank.
</Field>

<Field id="pos" title="Airport position">
	The position of the airport: latitude above, longitude below, as `N 47°26.99'` and `W122°18.71'` (degrees, minutes and hundredths of a minute). For an airport of the database the position cannot be changed.
</Field>

<Field id="lpage" title="Page identification of the left side">
	The page identification on the left side. A `+` in place of the space (`STA+1`) means that the page has more than one page with this page name. While the left cursor is on, <Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation, which shows the mode of the unit. <Term t="ENR-LEG">`ENR-LEG`</Term> is Enroute-Leg mode: en route navigation along a leg, from one waypoint to the next. In [OBS mode](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/) the field shows <Term t="ENR:">`ENR:`</Term> and the course you selected, and for approaches <Term t="ARM-LEG">`ARM-LEG`</Term>, <Term t="ARM:">`ARM:`</Term> or <Term t="APR-LEG">`APR-LEG`</Term>. While the unit shows a short message, such as `NO SUCH WPT`, it replaces this field and the next one ([The status line](/kln90b/unit/controls-and-display/#the-status-line)).
</Field>

<Field id="rpage" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification on the right side. A `+` in place of the space (`APT+3`) means that the page has more than one page with this page name. While the right cursor is on, <Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification.
</Field>
