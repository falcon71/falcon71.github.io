<Field id="plan" title="Flight plan number">
	`FP` and the number of the flight plan the page works on, from ` 0`, the active flight plan, to `25`. The cursor starts on the number, and the inner knob goes through the numbers around the ends. The unit keeps it while it is off, and TRI 5 shows the same number.
</Field>

<Field id="esa" title="Minimum en route safe altitude">
	`ESA` is the highest minimum safe altitude of the one-degree sectors that all legs of the flight plan pass through ([Minimum safe altitudes](/kln90b/pages/nav/#minimum-safe-altitudes)). It shows dashes when a part of the route lies outside the data of the minimum safe altitudes. The line appears together with the list.
</Field>

<Field id="area-1" title="The first airspace of the list">
	An airspace that the route crosses, or starts in, on two lines. The first line holds the first 11 characters of its name. The second holds the rest of the name, as far as it fits, and then the type. The type follows at the end of the second line: `CL B` and `CL C` for Class B and Class C airspace, and `ALRT`, `DNGR`, `MOA`, `PROH`, `REST`, `TRNG` and `WARN` for the other areas of special use airspace ([The airspaces along the route](/kln90b/pages/tri/#the-airspaces-along-the-route)).
</Field>

<Field id="area-2" title="The second airspace of the list">
	An airspace that the route crosses, or starts in, on two lines. The first line holds the first 11 characters of its name. The second holds the rest of the name, as far as it fits, and then the type. The type follows at the end of the second line: `CL B` and `CL C` for Class B and Class C airspace, and `ALRT`, `DNGR`, `MOA`, `PROH`, `REST`, `TRNG` and `WARN` for the other areas of special use airspace ([The airspaces along the route](/kln90b/pages/tri/#the-airspaces-along-the-route)).
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification with a plus sign">
	`TRI+6`: the page identification of the left side while the list of airspaces has more than one page. The left inner knob turns through the pages while the cursor is off ([Pages with a plus sign](/kln90b/unit/controls-and-display/#pages-with-a-plus-sign)).
</Field>
