<Field id="to" title="The waypoint the route ends at">
	`P.POS-` stands for your present position, and the waypoint after it is where the trip ends. Enter it like any waypoint of a left page: the cursor starts on it, <kbd>ENT</kbd> shows the waypoint page of the identifier on the right, and a second <kbd>ENT</kbd> approves it. The unit keeps it while it is off, and TRI 2 shows the same waypoint. <kbd>CLR</kbd> and then <kbd>ENT</kbd> blank it.
</Field>

<Field id="distance" title="Distance">
	The length of the great circle between the two ends of the route in whole NM, right-aligned in front of `nm`. It shows dashes while the route has no two ends, and for 10,000 NM or more.
</Field>

<Field id="bearing" title="Bearing">
	The magnetic bearing from your present position to the waypoint, in degrees, with the magnetic variation that the unit uses for your present position. It shows dashes while the route has no length.
</Field>

<Field id="gs" title="Groundspeed">
	`GS` is the groundspeed in knots, three digits, followed by `kt`. The unit works it out from the true airspeed and the wind of TRI 0 on the course of the route; the unit holds the course against the wind, so a crosswind lowers it. The inner knob changes the digit under the cursor and the page takes the groundspeed you typed: the time and the fuel required follow at once. The unit forgets the typed value when you leave the page or enter another waypoint. Above 999 kt it shows dashes, but the time and the fuel required are still computed from it; it shows `000` while the route has no two ends.
</Field>

<Field id="ete" title="Estimated time en route">
	`ETE` is the distance divided by the groundspeed, rounded to the whole minute, as hours and minutes. Below one hour the hours show as 0 (`0:52`). It shows dashes while the groundspeed is `000`, and for 100 hours or more.
</Field>

<Field id="ff" title="Fuel flow">
	`FF` is the fuel flow per hour, as a number from 00000.0 to 99999.9 in the unit you use for the fuel; the unit shows none. The inner knob changes a digit at once, without <kbd>ENT</kbd>. The unit keeps it while it is off. TRI 1, TRI 3 and TRI 5 show the same entry, and it is not a reading of a fuel management system.
</Field>

<Field id="res" title="Reserve fuel">
	`RES` is the reserve fuel, as a number from 00000.0 to 99999.9 in the unit you use for the fuel flow; the unit shows none. The inner knob changes a digit at once, without <kbd>ENT</kbd>. The unit adds it once to the fuel of the trip and keeps it while it is off. TRI 1, TRI 3 and TRI 5 show the same entry, and it is not that of OTH 5 to OTH 8.
</Field>

<Field id="freq" title="Fuel required">
	`F REQ` is the fuel the trip needs: the estimated time en route at the fuel flow, plus the reserve. It is in the unit of the fuel flow and shows a tenth below 100 (`20.4`) and whole units from 100. It shows dashes while the time shows dashes and from 100,000 on. With a fuel flow of 0 it equals the reserve.
</Field>

<Field id="cursor" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> takes the place of the page identification of the left side while that side's cursor is on.
</Field>
