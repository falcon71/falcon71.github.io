<Field id="to" title="The waypoint the route ends at">
	`P.POS-` stands for your present position, and the waypoint after it is where the trip ends. Enter it like any waypoint of a left page: the cursor starts on it, <kbd>ENT</kbd> shows the waypoint page of the identifier on the right, and a second <kbd>ENT</kbd> approves it. The unit keeps it while it is off, and TRI 2 shows the same waypoint. <kbd>CLR</kbd> and then <kbd>ENT</kbd> blank it.
</Field>

<Field id="distance" title="Distance">
	The length of the great circle between the two ends of the route in whole NM, right-aligned in front of `nm`. It shows dashes while the route has no two ends, and for 10,000 NM or more.
</Field>

<Field id="bearing" title="Bearing">
	The magnetic bearing from your present position to the waypoint, in degrees, with the magnetic variation that the unit uses for your present position. It shows dashes while the route has no length.
</Field>

<Field id="gs" title="Groundspeed with no solution">
	<Term t="GS">`GS`</Term> shows three dashes in front of `kt`: the wind leaves the aircraft no groundspeed on this course, because the crosswind is stronger than the true airspeed or the wind is at least as strong as the true airspeed and comes from ahead of the wingtips. The unit shows `000` instead while there is no route at all.
</Field>

<Field id="ete" title="Estimated time en route without a groundspeed">
	<Term t="ETE">`ETE`</Term> shows dashes in place of the hours and minutes while the groundspeed shows dashes or is `000`.
</Field>

<Field id="ff" title="Fuel flow">
	`FF` is the fuel flow per hour, as a number from 00000.0 to 99999.9 in the unit you use for the fuel; the unit shows none. The cursor stops on every digit, the tenth included, and the inner knob changes a digit at once, without <kbd>ENT</kbd>. The unit keeps it while it is off. TRI 1, TRI 3 and TRI 5 show the same entry, and it is not a reading of a fuel management system.
</Field>

<Field id="res" title="Reserve fuel">
	`RES` is the reserve fuel, as a number from 00000.0 to 99999.9 in the unit you use for the fuel flow; the unit shows none. The cursor stops on every digit, the tenth included, and the inner knob changes a digit at once, without <kbd>ENT</kbd>. The unit adds it once to the fuel of the trip and keeps it while it is off. TRI 1, TRI 3 and TRI 5 show the same entry, and it is not that of OTH 5 to OTH 8.
</Field>

<Field id="freq" title="Fuel required without a time">
	Dashes while the time shows dashes: the fuel required needs the time en route.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the left side: `TRI 1`. TRI 1 plans a trip from your present position to a waypoint.
</Field>
