<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`TRIP PLAN ESTIMATES` names TRI 0, the page that holds the entries of the trip planning pages.
</Field>

<Field id="tas" title="True airspeed">
	`TAS` is the true airspeed in knots, from 000 to 999 kt, that TRI 1, TRI 3 and TRI 5 plan with. The inner knob changes a digit at once, without <kbd>ENT</kbd>. The unit keeps it while it is off. It is no reading of the aircraft, and not the true airspeed of CAL 2.
</Field>

<Field id="wind-dir" title="Wind direction">
	`WIND` is the direction the wind blows from, in degrees true, from `000` to `359`. The true north symbol after the degree sign marks the direction as relative to true north. The cursor stops first on the two digits of the tens of degrees (`00` to `35`) and then on the last digit; the inner knob changes them at once, without <kbd>ENT</kbd>. The unit keeps it while it is off.
</Field>

<Field id="wind-speed" title="Wind speed">
	The speed of the wind in knots, from 000 to 999 kt, with the direction above. At `000` kt the groundspeed equals the true airspeed. The inner knob changes a digit at once, without <kbd>ENT</kbd>, and the unit keeps it while it is off.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the left side: `TRI 0`. TRI 0 holds the true airspeed and the wind for the planning on the other TRI pages.
</Field>
