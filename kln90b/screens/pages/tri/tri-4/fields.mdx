<Field id="route" title="The from waypoint and the to waypoint">
	The from waypoint and the to waypoint of the route, with a hyphen between them. The cursor starts on the from waypoint and moves on to the to waypoint when you approve the first. A waypoint is entered like any waypoint of a left page (<kbd>ENT</kbd> shows its waypoint page, a second <kbd>ENT</kbd> approves it) and kept while the unit is off. TRI 3 shows the same two waypoints. <kbd>CLR</kbd> and then <kbd>ENT</kbd> blank one.
</Field>

<Field id="esa" title="Minimum en route safe altitude">
	`ESA` is the highest minimum safe altitude of the one-degree sectors that the route passes through ([Minimum safe altitudes](/kln90b/pages/nav/#minimum-safe-altitudes)). It shows dashes when a part of the route lies outside the data of the minimum safe altitudes. On this page and on the other two pages with airspaces, the line appears together with the list.
</Field>

<Field id="area-1" title="The first airspace of the list">
	An airspace that the route crosses, or starts in, on two lines. The first line holds the first 11 characters of its name. The second holds the rest of the name, as far as it fits, and then the type. The type follows at the end of the second line: `CL B` and `CL C` for Class B and Class C airspace, and `ALRT`, `DNGR`, `MOA`, `PROH`, `REST`, `TRNG` and `WARN` for the other areas of special use airspace ([The airspaces along the route](/kln90b/pages/tri/#the-airspaces-along-the-route)).
</Field>

<Field id="area-2" title="The second airspace of the list">
	An airspace that the route crosses, or starts in, on two lines. The first line holds the first 11 characters of its name. The second holds the rest of the name, as far as it fits, and then the type. The type follows at the end of the second line: `CL B` and `CL C` for Class B and Class C airspace, and `ALRT`, `DNGR`, `MOA`, `PROH`, `REST`, `TRNG` and `WARN` for the other areas of special use airspace ([The airspaces along the route](/kln90b/pages/tri/#the-airspaces-along-the-route)).
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the left side: `TRI 4`. TRI 4 shows the minimum en route safe altitude and the airspaces on the route of TRI 3.
</Field>
