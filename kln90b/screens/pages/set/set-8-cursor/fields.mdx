<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`AIRSPACE ALERT` heads the page of the airspace alert.
</Field>

<Field id="enable" title="Airspace alert">
	`ENABLE` turns the airspace alert on, `DISABLE` turns it off. The left outer knob moves the cursor from here to the digits of the vertical buffer.
</Field>

<Field id="buffer" title="Vertical buffer">
	`VERT BUFFER` and the vertical buffer: the height in ft by which the unit widens the floor and the ceiling of an airspace. The cursor visits the ten thousands, the thousands and the hundreds, one step of the left outer knob each, and the left inner knob changes the digit under it at once, without <kbd>ENT</kbd>. The tens and the ones stay 0.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on. Pressing the left <kbd>CRSR</kbd> button turns the cursor off again, and the page name returns.
</Field>
