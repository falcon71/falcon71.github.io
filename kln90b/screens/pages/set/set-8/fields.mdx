<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`AIRSPACE ALERT` heads the page of the airspace alert.
</Field>

<Field id="enable" title="Airspace alert">
	`ENABLE` turns the airspace alert on, `DISABLE` turns it off. With the alert off, the vertical buffer is hidden and the cursor skips it. With the cursor on, the left inner knob changes the setting at once, without <kbd>ENT</kbd>.
</Field>

<Field id="buffer" title="Vertical buffer">
	`VERT BUFFER` and the vertical buffer: the height in ft by which the unit widens the floor and the ceiling of an airspace, shown with five digits after the plus-minus sign. The buffer goes up to 99,900 ft in steps of 100 ft, and the cursor visits only the ten thousands, the thousands and the hundreds. The tens and the ones stay 0. Each digit turns around with the left inner knob, and the change takes effect at once.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the left page, `SET 8`.
</Field>
