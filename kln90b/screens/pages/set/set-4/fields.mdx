<Field id="run" title="When the flight timer runs">
	{/* kln90b#522: describes the current behavior (30 kt or more); the fix makes it above 30 kt */}
	`RUN WHEN` and the condition. `GS > 30kt` runs the timer while the <Term t="GS">groundspeed</Term> is 30 kt or more, and `POWER IS ON` runs it for as long as the unit is on. With the cursor on, the left inner knob changes the choice at once, and the field needs no <kbd>ENT</kbd>.
</Field>
