<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	The name of the setting: the criteria that an airport must meet to appear in the [nearest list](/kln90b/operation/nearest/) of airports.
</Field>

<Field id="length" title="Minimum runway length">
	The shortest runway, in ft, that an airport needs to appear in the nearest list. With the cursor on, the left inner knob steps through the values from 1,000 ft to 5,000 ft in steps of 100 ft, and the change takes effect at once. The field needs no <kbd>ENT</kbd>.
</Field>

<Field id="surface" title="Runway surface">
	`HRD SFT` accepts airports with hard or soft runways, `HRD` only airports with a hard runway. The left outer knob moves the cursor here from the minimum length, and the left inner knob changes the choice at once.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> takes the place of the page identification of the left side while that side's cursor is on.
</Field>
