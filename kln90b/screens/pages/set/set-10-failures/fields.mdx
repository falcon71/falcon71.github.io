<Field id="gps" title="GPS acquisition mode">
	`GPS:` and the mode the receiver acquires in, `REAL` or `FAST`. The cursor starts here when you turn it on. [Real and fast acquisition](/kln90b/operation/gps-receiver/#real-and-fast-acquisition) explains the setting.
</Field>

<Field id="failures" title="Button to the failure pages">
	The button `FAILURES?` opens FLR 1, the first of the three FLR pages. Press <kbd>ENT</kbd> to open the pages; the unit turns the cursor off as it does.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> takes the place of the page identification of the left side while that side's cursor is on.
</Field>
