<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`ALTITUDE ALERT VOLUME:` heads the page that sets the volume of the aural alarm.
</Field>

<Field id="volume" title="Volume">
	The volume as two digits, from 00 to 99. With the cursor on, the left inner knob changes the digit under it, around from 9 to 0, and the left outer knob moves to the other digit. The setting changes at once, without <kbd>ENT</kbd>; whether the tones get louder depends on the installation.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the left page, `SET 9`.
</Field>
