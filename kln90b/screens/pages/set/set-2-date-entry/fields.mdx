<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`DATE/TIME` heads the page: the clock of the unit and its time zone.
</Field>

<Field id="date" title="Date">
	The date of the unit: day, month and year in two digits, in the time zone shown below it. While the unit is not NAV ready, the cursor can change it, and the new date takes effect with <kbd>ENT</kbd>. The years 88 to 99 are 1988 to 1999 and 00 to 87 are 2000 to 2087. Once the unit is NAV ready, the cursor skips the date, because the satellites supply it. An entry that is open shows underscores in the cells that you have not entered yet.
</Field>

<Field id="time" title="Time">
	The time of the unit in hours and minutes, 00:00 to 23:59, in the time zone beside it. It works like the date: the cursor can change it while the unit is not NAV ready, and the seconds start at 00 after <kbd>ENT</kbd>.
</Field>

<Field id="seconds" title="Seconds">
	The seconds of the time. They count on by themselves and cannot be entered.
</Field>

<Field id="zone" title="Time zone">
	The code of the system time zone, in which the unit shows the date and the time ([The time zone](/kln90b/operation/trip-planning/#the-time-zone) lists the zones). The left inner knob steps through the zones with the cursor on the zone, also when the unit is NAV ready, and the change takes effect at once. The same zone applies on every page that shows a time.
</Field>

<Field id="zonename" title="Name of the time zone">
	The name of the time zone in words, such as `CORD UNIV/Z` for `UTC` and `PACIFIC STD` for `PST`.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> takes the place of the page identification of the left side while that side's cursor is on.
</Field>
