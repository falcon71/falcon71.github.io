<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`FUEL DATA` heads the page of endurance, range and fuel efficiency.
</Field>

<Field id="endurance" title="Endurance">
	`ENDUR` is how long the fuel on board lasts at the present fuel flow once the fuel reserve is taken off, in hours and minutes. Below one hour the hours are blank. The field shows dashes while the fuel flow is 0 or missing, when the fuel reserve is more than the fuel on board and from 100 hours up.
</Field>

<Field id="range" title="Range">
	`RANGE` is the distance you fly in the endurance at the present groundspeed, in NM, with one decimal below 100 NM and as a whole number from 100 NM up. Dashes whenever the endurance is dashed.
</Field>

<Field id="efficiency" title="Fuel efficiency">
	The fuel efficiency: the NM that you fly on one fuel unit at the present groundspeed and fuel flow; the label names the fuel unit. The figure has one decimal below 10 and is a whole number of three digits from 10 up (`012`), to 999. Dashes while the fuel flow is 0 or missing. A fuel reserve larger than the fuel on board does not dash it.
</Field>

<Field id="res" title="Fuel reserve">
	`RES:` is the fuel reserve, five digits in fuel units that you enter. The figure counts at once, without <kbd>ENT</kbd>. OTH 5 shows and changes the same figure, and the unit keeps it while it is off ([Setting the fuel reserve](/kln90b/pages/oth/#setting-the-fuel-reserve)).
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the left page, `OTH 6`.
</Field>
