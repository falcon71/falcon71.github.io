<Field id="center" title="Name of the Center">
	The name of the Center whose airspace contains the present position. Where the airspace of several Centers overlaps, the name is the one that goes with the lowest of the frequencies.
</Field>

<Field id="ctr" title="Center">
	`CTR` says that the name above is a Center.
</Field>

<Field id="frequency" title="Center frequency">
	A frequency of the Center in MHz, with two decimals. The page lists the frequencies of every Center airspace that contains the present position, lowest first and each only once, up to four. The altitude plays no part. The unit renews the Center every few seconds while the page is shown.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the left side.
</Field>
