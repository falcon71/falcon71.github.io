<Field id="message" title="Refusal to delete a waypoint">
	`USED IN FPL` replaces the mode annunciation when you press <kbd>CLR</kbd> on a waypoint that a flight plan holds. The unit asks no question and deletes nothing. `ACTIVE WPT` is the refusal for the active waypoint, which the unit checks first.
</Field>
