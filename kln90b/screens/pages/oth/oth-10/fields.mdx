<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`AIR DATA` heads the pages of the air data system.
</Field>

<Field id="sat" title="Static air temperature">
	`SAT` is the static air temperature, the temperature of the surrounding air undisturbed by the motion of the aircraft, in °C, in whole degrees, with a minus sign below zero. Dashes while the value is missing.
</Field>

<Field id="tat" title="Total air temperature">
	`TAT` is the temperature of the air that the aircraft's probe brings to rest, in °C, in whole degrees, with a minus sign below zero. Dashes while the value is missing.
</Field>

<Field id="prs" title="Pressure altitude">
	`PRS` is the pressure altitude in ft, rounded to 100 ft, from 0 to 65,600 ft. Below 0 the field shows 0. Dashes while the value is missing.
</Field>

<Field id="den" title="Density altitude">
	`DEN` is the density altitude in ft, which the unit works out from the pressure altitude and `SAT` and rounds to 100 ft, from 0 to 65,600 ft. Dashes while the pressure altitude or `SAT` is missing.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the left side. An installation with a fuel management system calls the second air data page `OTH10`; one without calls it `OTH 6`.
</Field>
