<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`FUEL USED` heads the page of the fuel used.
</Field>

<Field id="unit" title="Fuel unit">
	The fuel unit of the fuel used: `GAL`, `IMP`, `L`, `KG` or `LB`. The installation decides it.
</Field>

<Field id="total" title="Total fuel used">
	The fuel that the engine has used, or all engines together where the aircraft has more than two, in whole units of fuel, up to 99999. The aircraft counts it; the unit has no reset and no entry for it. Dashes while the value is missing and throughout where the installation does not send the fuel used.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the left side.
</Field>
