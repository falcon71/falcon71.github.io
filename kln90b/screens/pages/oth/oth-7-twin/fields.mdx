<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`FUEL FLOW` heads the page of the fuel flow.
</Field>

<Field id="unit" title="Fuel unit">
	The fuel unit of the fuel flow, `GAL`, `IMP`, `L`, `KG` or `LB`, per hour. The installation decides it.
</Field>

<Field id="engines" title="Fuel flow of an engine">
	The fuel flow of engine 1 and of engine 2, in whole units of fuel an hour, up to 9999. Dashes while the value is missing.
</Field>

<Field id="total" title="Total fuel flow">
	The fuel flow of both engines together, in whole units of fuel an hour, up to 9999. Dashes while the value is missing.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the left side.
</Field>
