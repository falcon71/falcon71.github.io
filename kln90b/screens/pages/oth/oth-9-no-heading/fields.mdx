<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`AIR DATA` heads the pages of the air data system.
</Field>

<Field id="tas" title="True airspeed">
	`TAS` is the true airspeed in kt, from 0 to 999. Dashes while the value is missing.
</Field>

<Field id="mach" title="Mach number">
	`MACH` is the Mach number, two decimals without the leading zero, up to `.99`. Dashes while the value is missing.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the left side. An installation with a fuel management system calls the first air data page `OTH 9`; one without calls it `OTH 5`.
</Field>
