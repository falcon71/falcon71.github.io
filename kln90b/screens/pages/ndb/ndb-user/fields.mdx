<Field id="ident" title="Identifier of the user-defined NDB">
	The identifier of the user-defined NDB. A new identifier on the page looks up another NDB and does not rename this one; [Editing a user waypoint](/kln90b/operation/user-waypoints/#editing-a-user-waypoint) says how to rename a waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="freq" title="Frequency">
	The frequency of the NDB in kHz after `FREQ`, to a tenth (`FREQ  328.0`), or dashes when you have not entered one. With the cursor on, you enter it with the knobs, and it takes effect with <kbd>ENT</kbd>. The unit accepts frequencies from 190.0 to 1750.0 kHz and answers `INVALID ENT` to any other frequency.
</Field>

<Field id="pos" title="Position of the NDB">
	The position of the NDB: latitude above, longitude below, as `N 47°30.00'` and `W122°00.00'` (degrees, minutes and hundredths of a minute). With the cursor on, a new value takes effect with <kbd>ENT</kbd> and moves the NDB for every flight plan and page that uses it.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side.
</Field>
