<Field id="ident" title="NDB identifier">
	The identifier of the NDB. A `›` in front of it means that the NDB is the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint). [Entering an identifier](/kln90b/operation/waypoints/#entering-an-identifier) explains how the page looks up an NDB.

	The identifier is the only field that you can change on an NDB of the database. If you enter an identifier that no NDB has, the page shows no waypoint and empty fields, so that you can define a user-defined NDB ([A user-defined NDB](/kln90b/pages/ndb/#a-user-defined-ndb)).
</Field>

<Field id="name" title="Station name">
	The name of the station, cut to 11 characters. A user-defined NDB has no name, and the row stays blank.
</Field>

<Field id="freq" title="Frequency">
	The frequency of the NDB in kHz after `FREQ`; the unit does not show the unit. An NDB on a whole kilohertz shows the whole number (`FREQ    284`), and one on a half kilohertz shows the tenth (`FREQ  284.5`). It cannot be changed on an NDB of the database.
</Field>

<Field id="pos" title="Position of the station">
	The position of the station: latitude above, longitude below, as `N 48°30.73'` and `W123°01.43'` (degrees, minutes and hundredths of a minute). It cannot be changed on an NDB of the database.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side.
</Field>
