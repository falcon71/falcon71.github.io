<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`SUNRISE/SET` heads the page that gives the time of sunrise and sunset at a waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="wpt" title="Waypoint">
	The waypoint whose sunrise and sunset the page gives. The first time CAL 7 is shown after power-on, it holds the destination waypoint of the active flight plan (where FPL 0 holds an approach, its missed approach point while that lies ahead), or the waypoint of a direct-to outside the flight plan, and it is blank where there is neither. You enter another waypoint with its identifier and <kbd>ENT</kbd>, as in any field of a left page; an identifier that is in no database raises [`NO SUCH WPT`](/kln90b/lists/status-line-messages/#no-such-wpt). A waypoint that you enter stays until the unit is turned off; a blank field takes the destination again the next time the page is shown.
</Field>

<Field id="date" title="Date">
	The date for which the page gives sunrise and sunset: day, month and year in two digits. The first time CAL 7 is shown after power-on, it is today's date in the time zone that the page shows. You enter another with the cursor, and it takes effect with <kbd>ENT</kbd>; a date that does not exist, such as 30 FEB, raises [`INVALID ENT`](/kln90b/lists/status-line-messages/#invalid-ent). The years 88 to 99 are 1988 to 1999 and 00 to 87 are 2000 to 2087. The date does not follow the clock past midnight. An entry that is open shows underscores in the cells that you have not entered yet.
</Field>

<Field id="zone" title="Time zone">
	The code of the time zone ([The time zone](/kln90b/operation/trip-planning/#the-time-zone) lists them). With the cursor on the zone, the inner knob steps through them around the list, and the times move with the zone while the date stays. The unit starts the page with the system time zone, and choosing another zone here does not change the system time zone.
</Field>

<Field id="rise" title="Time of sunrise">
	`RISE` is the time of sunrise at the waypoint on the date, in the time zone shown, to the minute. It shows dashes (`--:--`) where the page has no waypoint and where the sun does not rise on that date.
</Field>

<Field id="set" title="Time of sunset">
	`SET` is the time of sunset at the waypoint on the date, in the time zone shown, to the minute. It shows dashes (`--:--`) where the page has no waypoint and where the sun does not set on that date.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the left page, `CAL 7`.
</Field>
