<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`ALTITUDE` heads the page that works out the pressure altitude and the density altitude from an indicated altitude, an altimeter setting and a temperature.
</Field>

<Field id="ind" title="Indicated altitude">
	`IND:` is the altitude that the altimeter shows, in feet. You enter it with the cursor in steps of 100 ft, from 0 to 99,900 ft: the cursor visits only the ten thousands, the thousands and the hundreds, and the value counts at once, without <kbd>ENT</kbd>. The unit takes no altitude from the aircraft here. CAL 2 shows the same value as `ALT:`.
</Field>

<Field id="baro" title="Altimeter setting">
	`BARO:` is the altimeter setting that the altimeter has, in inches (`00.00"` to `30.99"`) or in millibars (`MB`, 700 to 1099), as SET 7 chooses. With the cursor, the first two digits of the inches step as one cell, from 00 to 30. The value counts at once, and CAL 2 uses the same value. It is not the baro setting of the unit that the ALT page holds: the two do not change each other.
</Field>

<Field id="prs" title="Pressure altitude">
	`PRS` is the pressure altitude: the altitude that the standard setting of 29.92" would show at the indicated altitude and the altimeter setting above. The unit works it out and rounds it to 100 ft; it shows at least 0 ft and at most 65,600 ft. The cursor skips it.
</Field>

<Field id="temp" title="Outside air temperature">
	`TEMP:` is the outside air temperature in °C, from -99 to 99. The first cell is the sign, a choice of `-` and `0`, so a temperature above zero shows with a leading zero (`008°C`) and a temperature below zero with a minus (`-05°C`). The value counts at once. CAL 2 has a temperature of its own.
</Field>

<Field id="den" title="Density altitude">
	`DEN` is the density altitude: the pressure altitude corrected for the outside air temperature, rounded to 100 ft. It shows 0 ft where the calculation gives less and 65,600 ft where it gives more. The cursor skips it.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> takes the place of the page identification of the left side while that side's cursor is on.
</Field>
