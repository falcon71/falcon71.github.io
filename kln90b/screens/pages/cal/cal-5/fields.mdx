<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`TEMP/SPEED` heads the page that converts temperatures and speeds.
</Field>

<Field id="celsius" title="Temperature in °C">
	A temperature in °C, from -99 to 99, with the sign in the first cell, a choice of `-` and `0`. Entering it sets the temperature in °F below it, and entering that one sets this one. A converted value of 100 or more shows all three digits (`104`). A converted value that does not fit shows dashes, and an entry in such a field starts from 000.
</Field>

<Field id="fahrenheit" title="Temperature in °F">
	A temperature in °F, from -99 to 99, with the sign in the first cell, a choice of `-` and `0`. Entering it sets the temperature in °C above it, and entering that one sets this one. A converted value of 100 or more shows all three digits (`104`). A converted value that does not fit shows dashes, and an entry in such a field starts from 000.
</Field>

<Field id="knots" title="Speed in kt">
	A speed in kt, from 000 to 999. Entering it sets the speed in mph below it, and entering that one sets this one. A converted value that does not fit shows dashes, and an entry in such a field starts from 000.
</Field>

<Field id="mph" title="Speed in mph">
	A speed in statute miles per hour, from 000 to 999. Entering it sets the speed in kt above it, and entering that one sets this one. A converted value that does not fit shows dashes, and an entry in such a field starts from 000.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the left page, `CAL 5`.
</Field>
