<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`TIME CONV` heads the page that converts a time from one time zone to another.
</Field>

<Field id="time-top" title="Time of the upper zone">
	The time in the zone to its right, in hours and minutes of a 24-hour clock. You enter it with the cursor, hour, tens of minutes and minutes, and the time of the lower zone changes with it at once, without <kbd>ENT</kbd>. Both times are the same moment. The page shows no date.
</Field>

<Field id="zone-top" title="Upper time zone">
	The code of the time zone ([The time zone](/kln90b/operation/trip-planning/#the-time-zone) lists them). With the cursor on the zone, the inner knob steps through them around the list. The time of the upper zone moves to the new zone and the lower one stays. The unit starts the page with the system time zone, and choosing another zone here does not change the system time zone.
</Field>

<Field id="name-top" title="Name of the upper time zone">
	The name of the upper time zone in words, such as `CORD UNIV/Z` for `UTC` and `PACIFIC DAY` for `PDT`.
</Field>

<Field id="time-bottom" title="Time of the lower zone">
	The time in the zone to its right, in hours and minutes of a 24-hour clock. You enter it with the cursor, and the time of the upper zone changes with it at once, without <kbd>ENT</kbd>. Both times are the same moment.
</Field>

<Field id="zone-bottom" title="Lower time zone">
	The code of the time zone ([The time zone](/kln90b/operation/trip-planning/#the-time-zone) lists them). With the cursor on the zone, the inner knob steps through them around the list. The time of the lower zone moves to the new zone and the upper one stays. The unit starts the page with `UTC`.
</Field>

<Field id="name-bottom" title="Name of the lower time zone">
	The name of the lower time zone in words, such as `CORD UNIV/Z` for `UTC`.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the left page, `CAL 6`.
</Field>
