<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`WIND` heads the page that works out the wind from your true airspeed, your heading and your movement over the ground.
</Field>

<Field id="tas" title="True airspeed">
	`TAS` is the true airspeed in kt, from 000 to 999, which the wind calculation uses. Showing CAL 2 puts its true airspeed here; you can also enter one with the cursor, and it then stays until CAL 2 is shown again. The value counts at once. A value above 999 kt, which CAL 2 can hand over, shows dashes here and in the three wind lines.
</Field>

<Field id="component" title="Headwind or tailwind component">
	`HDWND` is the part of the wind that blows against your heading, `TLWND` the part that blows from behind, in kt. The label shows `HDWND` for a headwind and for no component at all. The values follow your true airspeed, the heading of the aircraft and the groundspeed and track of the GPS, and change with them. The line above, which holds the heading in an installation without a heading input, stays blank.
</Field>

<Field id="direction" title="Wind direction">
	`WIND` is the direction that the wind blows from, in degrees. The symbol after the degrees marks it as a true direction, not a magnetic one.
</Field>

<Field id="speed" title="Wind speed">
	The speed of the wind in kt, worked out from the same values as the direction.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the left page, `CAL 3`.
</Field>
