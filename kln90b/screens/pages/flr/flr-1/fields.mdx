<Field id="mtbf" title="Mean time between random failures">
	`MTBF:` and the setting for random failures: `OFF` or a whole number of hours from 1 to 999, written `15H`. With a number, a failure happens on average once in that many hours of simulated time with the unit on. `OFF` is the setting until you change it. [Random failures](/kln90b/operation/failures/#random-failures) says which failures can come this way.
</Field>

<Field id="battery" title="Charge of the internal battery">
	`BATTERY` and the charge of the internal battery in percent. A new battery shows 100, and any charge above zero shows at least 1. You cannot change the value on the page; `SERVICE?` sets it back to 100. [The battery](/kln90b/operation/failures/#the-battery) explains how the charge falls and what the battery keeps.
</Field>

<Field id="service" title="SERVICE? field">
	The `SERVICE?` field has the unit serviced. <kbd>ENT</kbd> on it asks for confirmation first. [Service](/kln90b/operation/failures/#service) lists what it does.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the left side.
</Field>
