<Field id="mtbf" title="Mean time between random failures">
	`MTBF:` and the hours in the entry, hundreds first, with the entry inverse and the marked digit flashing. The first turn of the left inner knob opens the entry with `0__H`; the first turn on each further digit shows 0, and later turns count up to 9 and wrap. The left outer knob moves between the three digits. A digit that you do not set counts as 0. <kbd>ENT</kbd> stores the hours and moves the cursor to `SERVICE?`; <kbd>CLR</kbd> leaves the entry and restores the old value. The range is `OFF` (000) to 999 hours.
</Field>

<Field id="battery" title="Charge of the internal battery">
	`BATTERY` and the charge of the internal battery in percent, 100 for a new battery.
</Field>

<Field id="service" title="SERVICE? field">
	The `SERVICE?` field, which has the unit serviced after a confirmation. [Service](/kln90b/operation/failures/#service) lists what it does.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`CRSR` in place of the page name tells you that the left cursor is on.
</Field>
