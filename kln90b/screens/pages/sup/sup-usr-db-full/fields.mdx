<Field id="ident" title="Identifier that no waypoint has">
	The identifier that you entered, which the unit could not store as a waypoint. The page keeps what you typed.
</Field>

<Field id="offer" title="Offer to create a waypoint">
	`CREATE NEW WPT AT:` stays on the page, because nothing was created.
</Field>

<Field id="user" title="Create at a position you enter">
	`USER POS?` asks for the position of the waypoint. With the user database full, the unit refuses when you complete it.
</Field>

<Field id="pres" title="Create at the present position">
	`PRES POS?` is refused at once while the user database is full.
</Field>

<Field id="left-page" title="Page identification of the left side">
	The page identification on the left side.
</Field>

<Field id="message" title="Message that the database is full">
	`USR DB FULL` replaces the mode annunciation when the unit cannot store another [user waypoint](/kln90b/operation/user-waypoints/#usr-db-full). Delete a waypoint on OTH 3 to make room.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the right cursor is on.
</Field>
