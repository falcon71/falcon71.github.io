<Field id="title" title="Approach selected">
	The approach you selected: the first letter of its kind, the runway, a dash and the airport. FPL 0 shows the same name
	above the approach's waypoints once it is loaded.
</Field>

<Field id="jalon" title="Final approach fix">
	The final approach fix, marked with `-f` after the identifier. The unit uses the <Term t="FAF" /> to switch
	the approach from armed to active.
</Field>

<Field id="hovos" title="Waypoint of the final approach">
	A waypoint between the final approach fix and the missed approach point, without a mark.
</Field>

<Field id="rw34l" title="Missed approach point">
	The missed approach point, marked with `-m` after the identifier. On an RNAV approach to a runway it is usually
	the runway threshold, named <Term t="RW" /> and the runway. The waypoints after the <Term t="MAP" /> are the missed approach.
</Field>

<Field id="lofal" title="Missed approach holding point">
	The last waypoint of the approach. When the missed approach ends in a hold there, `-h` after the identifier marks it
	as the <Term t="MAHP" />.
</Field>

<Field id="load" title="Load prompt">
	`LOAD IN FPL` loads the approach into FPL 0 when you press <kbd>ENT</kbd>. The list stays scrolled when the cursor
	moves back down to it from the waypoints.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="Cursor indication">
	`CRSR` shows that the right cursor is on. It stays on at every step of the selection, so you do not press the right
	<kbd>CRSR</kbd>.
</Field>
