<Field id="title" title="Procedure selected">
	The procedure you are about to load: its name, a dash and `SID` for a departure, a small `STAR` for an arrival, or
	the approach name on APT 8.
</Field>

<Field id="question" title="Question to add the airport">
	The airport of the procedure is not in FPL 0, and the unit offers to add it together with the procedure. A
	departure's airport becomes the first waypoint of FPL 0, with the departure after it; an arrival's or an approach's
	airport becomes the last waypoint, with the procedure in front of it. For an arrival the fourth row reads
	`AND STAR TO`, for an approach `AND APPR TO`.
</Field>

<Field id="approve" title="Approval">
	`APPROVE?` under the cursor. Press <kbd>ENT</kbd> to add the airport and load the procedure, or <kbd>CLR</kbd> to go
	back to the waypoints of the procedure without changing FPL 0.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="Cursor indication">
	`CRSR` shows that the right cursor is on. It stays on at every step of the selection, so you do not press the right
	<kbd>CRSR</kbd>.
</Field>
