<Field id="title" title="Procedure selected">
	The procedure you selected: its name, a dash and `SID` for a departure, or a small `STAR` for an arrival. It heads
	every step of the selection until the procedure is loaded.
</Field>

<Field id="heading" title="Transition list heading">
	`TRANSITION` asks for the <Term t="Transition">transition</Term>, the part of the procedure that leads to the en
	route fix where a departure ends or from the fix where an arrival begins. The unit asks when the procedure has two or
	more transitions. The list has no entry without a transition: you must select one.
</Field>

<Field id="arrie" title="Transition under the cursor">
	A transition with its number in the list, named after its en route fix. Press <kbd>ENT</kbd> to select it, or
	<kbd>CLR</kbd> to go back one step.
</Field>

<Field id="pangl" title="Other transition">
	Another transition of the procedure, with its number. The list has four rows; a longer list scrolls, and its bottom
	row always shows the last transition.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="Cursor indication">
	`CRSR` shows that the right cursor is on. It stays on at every step of the selection, so you do not press the right
	<kbd>CRSR</kbd>.
</Field>
