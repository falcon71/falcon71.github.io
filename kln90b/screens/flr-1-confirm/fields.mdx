<Field id="mtbf" title="Mean time between random failures">
	`MTBF:` and the setting for random failures, `OFF` or 1 to 999 hours. Service leaves it as it is.
</Field>

<Field id="battery" title="Charge of the internal battery">
	`BATTERY` and the charge of the internal battery in percent. Service sets it to 100.
</Field>

<Field id="confirm" title="Confirmation of the service">
	`CONFIRM?` stands in the place of `SERVICE?` after you pressed <kbd>ENT</kbd> on it. Press <kbd>ENT</kbd> again to have the unit serviced, or <kbd>CLR</kbd> to withdraw the question. [Service](/kln90b/operation/failures/#service) lists what service does.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`CRSR` in place of the page name tells you that the left cursor is on.
</Field>
