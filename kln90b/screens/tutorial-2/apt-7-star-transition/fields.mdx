<Field id="title" title="Procedure header">
	The procedure you selected: its name, a dash and `STAR` for a STAR. It heads every step of the selection until the procedure is loaded.
</Field>

<Field id="transition" title="Transition list heading">
	`TRANSITION` asks for the <Term t="Transition">transition</Term>, the part of the procedure that leads from the en route fix where a STAR begins. The unit asks when the procedure has two or more transitions. The list has no entry without a transition: you must select one.
</Field>

<Field id="pdt" title="Transition in the list">
	A transition of the procedure with its number in the list, named after the en route fix where it begins.
</Field>

<Field id="suned" title="Transition in the list">
	Another transition of the procedure, with its number. The list has four rows; a longer list scrolls, and its bottom row always shows the last transition.
</Field>

<Field id="ykm" title="Transition under the cursor">
	The transition the cursor marks. Press <kbd>ENT</kbd> to select it, or <kbd>CLR</kbd> to go back one step.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	`CRSR` shows that the right cursor is on. It stays on at every step of the selection, so you do not press the right <kbd>CRSR</kbd>.
</Field>
