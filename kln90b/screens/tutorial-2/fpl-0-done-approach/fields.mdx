<Field id="chins" title="First waypoint of the STAR">
	A waypoint of the <Term t="STAR" />, with a period after the number.
</Field>

<Field id="raddy" title="Last waypoint of the STAR">
	The last waypoint of the STAR. The approach follows it.
</Field>

<Field id="header" title="Approach header">
	The header of the approach: the first letter of its kind, `R` for <Term t="RNAV" />, the runway, a dash and the airport. A header counts as no waypoint and has no number ([The approach in FPL 0](/kln90b/operation/approaches/#the-approach-in-fpl-0)).
</Field>

<Field id="iaf" title="Initial approach fix">
	The <Term t="IAF" /> you selected, the first waypoint of the approach, with the suffix `-I` after the identifier. A waypoint of an approach has a blank instead of the colon after its number.
</Field>

<Field id="covog" title="Waypoint under the cursor">
	A waypoint of the approach without a role of its own and without a suffix, marked by the left cursor. The cursor can rest on the waypoints of an approach, but they cannot be deleted or changed.
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Last waypoint">
	The last waypoint of FPL 0, the airport, which follows the approach as an en route waypoint. While the list is longer than the page, the last waypoint stays in the bottom row and the rows between scroll.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>
