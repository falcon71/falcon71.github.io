<Field id="distance" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	The direct distance to the active waypoint in NM, never the distance along the route. It shows one decimal below 100 NM and whole NM from 100 NM. Past the waypoint it counts up again.
</Field>

<Field id="ident" title="Active waypoint">
	The identifier of the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint), followed by the suffix of an approach waypoint where it has one: `-I` for an initial approach fix, `-F` for the final approach fix, `-M` for the missed approach point and `-H` for the missed approach holding point. It flashes during [waypoint alerting](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#waypoint-alerting). The unit does not sequence past the <Term t="MAP">missed approach point</Term>, so it stays the active waypoint after you pass it ([The missed approach](/kln90b/operation/approaches/#the-missed-approach)).
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode of the unit, which Super NAV 5 shows here because it has no status line: <Term t="APR-LEG">`APR-LEG`</Term> is Approach Active-Leg mode: the approach is active ([Activating the approach](/kln90b/operation/approaches/#activating-the-approach)). [The mode annunciations](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-mode-annunciations) explain the modes.
</Field>

<Field id="groundspeed" title="Groundspeed">
	Your groundspeed in whole knots.
</Field>

<Field id="xtk" title="Field 1: cross-track error">
	Field 1 of the left side of Super NAV 5 with the choice `XTK`: the <Term t="XTK">cross-track error</Term> from the active leg: hundredths of a NM below 1 NM, tenths from 1 NM and whole NM from 10 NM, then `NM` and an arrow that points to the side you must fly to for getting back on the course. With the left cursor on the field, the inner knob changes it to `ETE` or `VNAV` ([The left side of Super NAV 5](/kln90b/pages/nav/#the-left-side-of-super-nav-5)).
</Field>

<Field id="dtk" title="Field 2: desired track">
	Field 2 of the left side of Super NAV 5 with the choice `DTK`: the <Term t="DTK">desired track</Term> as a magnetic course in whole degrees. The number flashes while an external course indicator that the unit reads is more than 10° off it. With the left cursor on the field, the inner knob changes it to `BRG` or `RAD` ([The left side of Super NAV 5](/kln90b/pages/nav/#the-left-side-of-super-nav-5)).
</Field>

<Field id="track" title="Field 3: track">
	Field 3 of the left side of Super NAV 5 with the choice `TK`: your actual track as a magnetic course in whole degrees, which points up on the map in actual track up. It shows `---°` below a groundspeed of 2 kt. With the left cursor on the field, the inner knob changes it to `BRG` or `RAD` ([The left side of Super NAV 5](/kln90b/pages/nav/#the-left-side-of-super-nav-5)).
</Field>

<Field id="range" title="Range scale">
	The range scale in NM: the distance from the aircraft to the top of the map. The `AUTO` range scale is chosen here, and with the cursor off the field shows the range scale the unit uses at the moment, the smallest that holds the active waypoint and, where one follows, the next waypoint. In actual track up the map reaches a third of that distance behind the aircraft. The left cursor comes on at this field ([The range scale and AUTO](/kln90b/pages/nav/#the-range-scale-and-auto)).
</Field>

<Field id="runways" title="Runways of Boeing Field">
	Boeing Field drawn as its runways, each a line, as the map draws an airport of FPL 0 at a range scale of 2 NM or less. At 2 NM only the longest runway shows its numbers, here the number of one of its ends ([Runway diagrams](/kln90b/pages/nav/#runway-diagrams)).
</Field>

<Field id="airport" title="Airport of the flight plan">
	KBFI, the airport at the end of FPL 0, as a small square followed by its identifier.
</Field>

<Field id="aircraft" title="Aircraft">
	The aircraft, an aircraft symbol three quarters of the way down the map in actual track up. In North up and desired track up it is a diamond.
</Field>
