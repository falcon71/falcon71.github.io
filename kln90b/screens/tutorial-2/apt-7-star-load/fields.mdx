<Field id="title" title="Procedure header">
	The procedure you selected: its name, a dash and `STAR` for a STAR. FPL 0 shows the same name above the procedure's waypoints once it is loaded.
</Field>

<Field id="ykm" title="First waypoint of the STAR">
	The first waypoint of the STAR, where the transition you selected begins, numbered 1.
</Field>

<Field id="chins" title="Waypoint of the STAR">
	A waypoint of the STAR, in the order you fly them.
</Field>

<Field id="raddy" title="Waypoint of the STAR">
	A waypoint of the STAR. Turn the right outer knob counterclockwise to move the cursor up into the list and scroll it; the entries cannot be selected or changed here.
</Field>

<Field id="aubrn" title="Last waypoint of the STAR">
	The last waypoint of the procedure. When the list is longer than the page, the bottom row shows the last waypoint; its number tells you how many waypoints the procedure adds to FPL 0. Legs that end at no waypoint, such as vectors after the last fix, are not in the list.
</Field>

<Field id="load" title="Load prompt">
	`LOAD IN FPL` loads the procedure into FPL 0 when you press <kbd>ENT</kbd>. The cursor is on it when the waypoints appear. If the airport is not in FPL 0, or FPL 0 holds a STAR already, the unit first asks a question on this page.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	`CRSR` shows that the right cursor is on. It stays on at every step of the selection, so you do not press the right <kbd>CRSR</kbd>.
</Field>
