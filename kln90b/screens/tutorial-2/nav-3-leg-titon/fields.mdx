<Field id="leg" title="Active leg">
	The waypoint the active leg starts from, the `›` and the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint). The top line is the one of NAV 1.
</Field>

<Field id="dtk" title="Desired track">
	<Term t="DTK">`DTK`</Term> is the magnetic course of the active leg at the point abeam the aircraft, in whole degrees, measured with the magnetic variation at the position of the aircraft. Where the unit reads the external indicator, it flashes while the indicator is more than 10° off it.
</Field>

<Field id="tk" title="Actual track">
	`TK` is your actual track, magnetic, in whole degrees. It shows `---°` without a position and at a groundspeed below 2 kt.
</Field>

<Field id="fly" title="Direction to fly and cross-track error">
	The direction to fly to get back on the course, `L` or `R`, and the <Term t="XTK">cross-track error</Term>. `0.00`, with either letter, is exactly on course. The distance shows hundredths of a NM below 1 NM, tenths from 1 NM and whole NM from 10 NM.
</Field>

<Field id="msa" title="Minimum safe altitude">
	The minimum safe altitude of the one-degree sector the aircraft is in, in whole hundreds of feet, with a clearance above the terrain of the sector. [Minimum safe altitudes](/kln90b/pages/nav/#minimum-safe-altitudes) explains the sectors.
</Field>

<Field id="esa" title="Minimum en route safe altitude">
	The minimum en route safe altitude: the highest minimum safe altitude of every sector between the aircraft and the active waypoint and, in leg mode on a leg of FPL 0, along the rest of FPL 0 to the destination or the missed approach point. It is never lower than `MSA`. [Minimum safe altitudes](/kln90b/pages/nav/#minimum-safe-altitudes) explains the sectors.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the left side: `NAV 3`. NAV 3 is the same page on the left and on the right side.
</Field>
