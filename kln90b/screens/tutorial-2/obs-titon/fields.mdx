<Field id="to" title="Active waypoint">
	The arrow and the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint), as on NAV 1. In OBS mode the waypoint stays active when you pass it, and the place in front of the arrow stays blank.
</Field>

<Field id="obs" title="Selected course">
	In OBS mode `OBS` and the [selected course](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-selected-course) take the place of <Term t="DTK">`DTK`</Term>, in whole degrees magnetic, measured with the magnetic variation of the active waypoint. Without a colon after `OBS` the unit takes the course from the external indicator: turn the indicator to change it. With a colon you set it on the unit. The number flashes while the indicator is more than 10° off it.
</Field>

<Field id="tk" title="Actual track">
	`TK` is your actual track, magnetic, in whole degrees. In OBS mode the unit measures it with the magnetic variation of the active waypoint, so that it compares with the selected course.
</Field>

<Field id="fly" title="Direction to fly and cross-track error">
	The direction to fly to get back on the course, `L` or `R`, and the <Term t="XTK">cross-track error</Term> from the selected course. The distance shows hundredths of a NM below 1 NM, tenths from 1 NM and whole NM from 10 NM.
</Field>

<Field id="msa" title="Minimum safe altitude">
	The minimum safe altitude of the one-degree sector the aircraft is in, in whole hundreds of feet. [Minimum safe altitudes](/kln90b/pages/nav/#minimum-safe-altitudes) explains the sectors.
</Field>

<Field id="esa" title="Minimum en route safe altitude">
	The minimum en route safe altitude: the highest minimum safe altitude along your way. In OBS mode it covers only the way to the active waypoint, so it can be lower than in leg mode. [Minimum safe altitudes](/kln90b/pages/nav/#minimum-safe-altitudes) explains it.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the left side: `NAV 3`. NAV 3 is the same page on the left and on the right side.
</Field>
