<Field id="title" title="Approach header">
	The approach you selected, named as FPL 0 names it: the first letter of its kind, `R` for <Term t="RNAV" />, the runway, a dash and the airport.
</Field>

<Field id="iaf" title="IAF list label">
	`IAF` labels the list of the approach's transitions, each beginning at an initial approach fix (<Term t="IAF" />) or at a fix before one. The approach always starts at an IAF, and you must select one entry.
</Field>

<Field id="arvad" title="Transition in the list">
	A transition of the approach with its number, named after its first waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="humpp" title="Transition under the cursor">
	The entry the cursor marks. Press <kbd>ENT</kbd> to select it and see the waypoints of the approach from there, or <kbd>CLR</kbd> to go back to the list of approaches.
</Field>

<Field id="woodi" title="Last transition in the list">
	The last entry of the list. When the list is longer than the page, the bottom row shows the last entry, and `IAF` stays on the top row.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	`CRSR` shows that the right cursor is on. It stays on at every step of the selection, so you do not press the right <kbd>CRSR</kbd>.
</Field>
