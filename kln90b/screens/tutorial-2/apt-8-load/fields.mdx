<Field id="title" title="Approach header">
	The approach you selected: the first letter of its kind, the runway, a dash and the airport. FPL 0 shows the same name above the approach's waypoints once it is loaded.
</Field>

<Field id="humpp" title="Initial approach fix">
	The <Term t="IAF" /> you selected, with the suffix `-I` after the identifier ([The approach in FPL 0](/kln90b/operation/approaches/#the-approach-in-fpl-0) lists the suffixes).
</Field>

<Field id="covog" title="Waypoint of the approach">
	A waypoint between the IAF and the final approach fix, without a suffix.
</Field>

<Field id="aubrn" title="Waypoint of the approach">
	The next waypoint of the approach. Turn the right outer knob counterclockwise to move the cursor up into the list and scroll it; the entries cannot be selected or changed here.
</Field>

<Field id="lofal" title="Missed approach holding point">
	The last waypoint of the approach, with the suffix `-H` that marks it as the <Term t="MAHP" />, where the missed approach ends in a holding pattern. When the list is longer than the page, the bottom row shows the last waypoint, so its number tells you how many waypoints the approach adds to FPL 0.
</Field>

<Field id="load" title="Load prompt">
	`LOAD IN FPL` loads the approach into FPL 0 when you press <kbd>ENT</kbd>. The cursor is on it when the waypoints appear. If the airport is not in FPL 0, or FPL 0 holds an approach already, the unit first asks a question on this page.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	`CRSR` shows that the right cursor is on. It stays on at every step of the selection, so you do not press the right <kbd>CRSR</kbd>.
</Field>
