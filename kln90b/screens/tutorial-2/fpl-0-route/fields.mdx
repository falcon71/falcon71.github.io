<Field id="from" title="From waypoint">
	The first waypoint of FPL 0, with the tail of the [active leg symbol](/kln90b/pages/fpl/#the-active-leg-symbol) in front of it: the active leg starts here. The colon after the number shows an en route waypoint, one that you entered.
</Field>

<Field id="to" title="Active waypoint">
	The [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint), with the head of the active leg symbol in front of it. The unit makes a leg of FPL 0 active as soon as FPL 0 holds two waypoints and nothing else is active.
</Field>

<Field id="enroute" title="Further en route waypoints">
	The waypoints after the active one, each with its number in the flight plan and a colon.
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Last waypoint">
	The last waypoint of FPL 0, the destination waypoint. While the list is longer than the page, the last waypoint stays in the bottom row and the rows between scroll.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`FPL 0`, the active flight plan.
</Field>
