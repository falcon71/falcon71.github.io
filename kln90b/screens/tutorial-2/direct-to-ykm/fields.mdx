<Field id="dto-title" title="DIRECT TO annunciation">
	`DIRECT TO:` at the top of the Direct To page shows that the page makes the waypoint you approve the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="dto-ident" title="Default waypoint">
	The [default waypoint](/kln90b/operation/direct-to/#the-default-waypoint), flashing in inverse video while the unit waits for <kbd>ENT</kbd> to approve the waypoint page on the right side. <kbd>ENT</kbd> makes the direct-to. The left inner knob starts a new entry, which replaces the waypoint and drops the approval.
</Field>

<Field id="ident" title="Identifier of the waypoint shown for approval">
	The identifier of the waypoint page shown for approval. The arrow in front of it marks the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint). The cursor cannot change the identifier here.
</Field>

<Field id="dme" title="DME of the station">
	`D` means that the station has distance measuring equipment: a VOR/DME, a VORTAC or a DME without a VOR.
</Field>

<Field id="name" title="Station name">
	The name of the station, cut to fit the line.
</Field>

<Field id="class" title="Class of the station">
	The class of the station: `T` for a terminal VOR, `L` for a low altitude VOR and `H` for a high altitude VOR, `U` for undefined.
</Field>

<Field id="freq" title="Frequency">
	The frequency of the station in MHz.
</Field>

<Field id="magvar" title="Published magnetic variation">
	The magnetic variation that the station carries, in whole degrees and followed by `E` or `W`. Its radials are measured with it, and so is a selected course through the station in OBS mode ([The selected course](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-selected-course)). It can differ from the variation that the unit uses at the position of the aircraft.
</Field>

<Field id="pos" title="Position of the station">
	The position of the station: latitude above, longitude below, in degrees, minutes and hundredths of a minute.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on. The Direct To page opens with its cursor on.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation, which shows the mode of the unit. <Term t="ENR-LEG">`ENR-LEG`</Term> is Enroute-Leg mode.
</Field>

<Field id="ent" title="Enter prompt">
	<Term t="ENT">`ENT`</Term> flashes while the unit waits for <kbd>ENT</kbd> to approve what it shows, such as a waypoint page shown for approval.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side: the VOR page of the waypoint shown for approval.
</Field>
