<Field id="ident" title="Airport identifier">
	The airport whose approaches APT 8 shows, the same airport as on the other APT pages.
</Field>

<Field id="title" title="Instrument approach procedures">
	`IAP`, for instrument approach procedures, after the identifier names the page.
</Field>

<Field id="rnav32l" title="Approach under the cursor">
	An approach with its number in the list: the kind of approach, such as <Term t="RNAV" />, and the runway. The letter of an approach such as RNAV (GPS) Y is not shown. The list holds only the approaches the unit can fly. Press <kbd>ENT</kbd> to select the approach.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	`CRSR` shows that the right cursor is on. It stays on at every step of the selection, so you do not press the right <kbd>CRSR</kbd>.
</Field>
