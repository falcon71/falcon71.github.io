<Field id="chins-enroute" title="En route waypoint">
	The last en route waypoint before the <Term t="STAR" />, with a colon after its number.
</Field>

<Field id="header" title="Procedure header">
	The header of a <Term t="STAR" />: its name, a dash and `STAR`. The STAR stands in front of its airport, after the en route waypoints. A header counts as no waypoint and has no number.
</Field>

<Field id="ykm" title="First waypoint of the STAR">
	The first waypoint of the <Term t="Transition">transition</Term> of the STAR, with a period after the number.
</Field>

<Field id="chins-star" title="The en route waypoint again, in the STAR">
	A waypoint of the STAR, with a period after the number. A waypoint of a procedure that FPL 0 also holds as an en route waypoint stands in FPL 0 twice, once with a colon and once with a period.
</Field>

<Field id="raddy" title="Waypoint under the cursor">
	A waypoint of the STAR, marked by the left cursor. <kbd>CLR</kbd> on a waypoint asks whether to delete it ([Deleting a waypoint](/kln90b/operation/flight-plans/#deleting-a-waypoint)).
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Destination airport">
	The airport of the STAR, the last waypoint of FPL 0. While the list is longer than the page, the last waypoint stays in the bottom row and the rows between scroll.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>
