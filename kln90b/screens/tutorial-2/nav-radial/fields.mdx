<Field id="to" title="Active waypoint">
	The `›` and the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint), as on NAV 1. In OBS mode the waypoint stays active when you pass it, and the place in front of the arrow stays blank.
</Field>

<Field id="obs" title="Selected course">
	In OBS mode `OBS` and the [selected course](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-selected-course) take the place of <Term t="DTK">`DTK`</Term>, in whole degrees magnetic, measured with the magnetic variation of the active waypoint. Without a colon after `OBS` the unit takes the course from the external indicator: turn the indicator to change it. With a colon you set it on the unit. The number flashes while the indicator is more than 10° off it.
</Field>

<Field id="tk" title="Actual track">
	`TK` is your actual track, magnetic, in whole degrees. In OBS mode the unit measures it with the magnetic variation of the active waypoint, so that it compares with the selected course.
</Field>

<Field id="fly" title="Direction to fly and cross-track error">
	The direction to fly to get back on the course, `L` or `R`, and the <Term t="XTK">cross-track error</Term> from the selected course. The distance shows hundredths of a NM below 1 NM, tenths from 1 NM and whole NM from 10 NM.
</Field>

<Field id="msa" title="Minimum safe altitude">
	The minimum safe altitude of the one-degree sector the aircraft is in, in whole hundreds of feet. [Minimum safe altitudes](/kln90b/pages/nav/#minimum-safe-altitudes) explains the sectors.
</Field>

<Field id="esa" title="Minimum en route safe altitude">
	The highest minimum safe altitude along your way. In OBS mode it covers only the way to the active waypoint, so it can be lower than in leg mode. [Minimum safe altitudes](/kln90b/pages/nav/#minimum-safe-altitudes) explains it.
</Field>

<Field id="r-to" title="Active waypoint">
	The `›` and the identifier of the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint). In OBS mode the place in front of the arrow stays blank, because the selected course passes through the active waypoint and starts at no waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="r-cdi" title="D-bar">
	The D-bar shows the cross-track error from the selected course: the bar stands on the side the course lies on, so you fly toward it. Each of the five dots on either side is a fifth of the CDI scale factor, and the outer dot is full scale. The TO/FROM indicator in the middle shows TO while the active waypoint lies ahead along the selected course, FROM once it lies behind.
</Field>

<Field id="r-dis" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	The distance to the active waypoint, in NM.
</Field>

<Field id="r-gs" title="Groundspeed">
	The groundspeed, in kt.
</Field>

<Field id="r-ete" title="Estimated time en route">
	The time to the active waypoint at the present groundspeed, as hours and minutes. Below one hour the hours stay blank.
</Field>

<Field id="r-brg" title="Bearing to the active waypoint">
	The magnetic bearing to the active waypoint. In OBS mode the unit measures it at the active waypoint, with the magnetic variation there, so that it equals the selected course while you are on the course line.
</Field>

<Field id="lpage" title="Page identification of the left side">
	The page identification of the left page, `NAV 3`. NAV 3 is the same page on the left and on the right side.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	<Term t="ENR:">`ENR:`</Term> and a three-digit course: Enroute-OBS mode, with the selected course as a magnetic course ([The mode annunciations](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-mode-annunciations)).
</Field>

<Field id="rpage" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification of the right page, `NAV 1`.
</Field>
