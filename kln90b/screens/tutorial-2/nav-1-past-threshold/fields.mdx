<Field id="r-leg" title="Active leg">
	The waypoint the active leg starts at, an arrow and the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint). A waypoint of an approach shows without its suffix. The unit does not sequence past the <Term t="MAP">missed approach point</Term>, so it stays the active waypoint after you pass it ([The missed approach](/kln90b/operation/approaches/#the-missed-approach)).
</Field>

<Field id="r-cdi" title="D-bar">
	The D-bar shows the cross-track error from the desired track: the bar stands on the side the course lies on, so you fly toward it. Each of the five dots on either side is a fifth of the CDI scale factor, which the approach modes narrow by themselves ([The CDI scale along the approach](/kln90b/operation/approaches/#the-cdi-scale-along-the-approach)). The TO/FROM indicator in the middle shows TO while the active waypoint lies ahead, FROM once it lies behind. `F L A G` in place of the dots means that the unit cannot navigate.
</Field>

<Field id="r-dis" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	The distance to the active waypoint, in NM. Past the waypoint it counts up again.
</Field>

<Field id="r-gs" title="Groundspeed">
	The groundspeed, in kt.
</Field>

<Field id="r-ete" title="Estimated time en route">
	The time to the active waypoint at the present groundspeed, as hours and minutes. Below one hour the hours stay blank. Past the waypoint it is the time back to it at the present groundspeed, so it counts up as the distance does.
</Field>

<Field id="r-brg" title="Bearing to the active waypoint">
	The magnetic bearing from the aircraft to the active waypoint. Past the waypoint it points back to it.
</Field>

<Field id="rpage" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the right side: `NAV 1`.
</Field>
