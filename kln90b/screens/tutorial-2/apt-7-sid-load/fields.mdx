<Field id="title" title="Procedure header">
	The procedure you selected: its name, a dash and `SID` for a SID. FPL 0 shows the same name above the procedure's waypoints once it is loaded.
</Field>

<Field id="ykm" title="First waypoint of the SID">
	The first waypoint that the unit loads into FPL 0, numbered 1. Legs that end at no waypoint, such as a climb on runway heading to an altitude, are not in the list ([What the unit keeps of a procedure](/kln90b/operation/departures-arrivals/#what-the-unit-keeps-of-a-procedure)).
</Field>

<Field id="titon" title="Last waypoint of the SID">
	The last waypoint of the SID. When the list is longer than the page, the bottom row shows the last waypoint; its number tells you how many waypoints the procedure adds to FPL 0.
</Field>

<Field id="load" title="Load prompt">
	`LOAD IN FPL` loads the procedure into FPL 0 when you press <kbd>ENT</kbd>. The cursor is on it when the waypoints appear. If the airport is not in FPL 0, or FPL 0 holds a SID already, the unit first asks a question on this page.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	`CRSR` shows that the right cursor is on. It stays on at every step of the selection, so you do not press the right <kbd>CRSR</kbd>.
</Field>
