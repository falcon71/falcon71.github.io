<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`USER WPTS` heads OTH 3, the list of user waypoints.
</Field>

<Field id="waypoints" title="Other user waypoints">
	The other user waypoints of the list, with the waypoint type and the flight plan that holds each.
</Field>

<Field id="question" title="Question to delete a waypoint">
	`DEL CAMP  ?` replaces the row of the waypoint under the cursor and flashes in inverse video. <kbd>ENT</kbd> deletes the waypoint. <kbd>CLR</kbd> withdraws the question, and so does moving the cursor with the outer knob or turning the cursor off.
</Field>

<Field id="ident" title="Identifier of the waypoint in question">
	The identifier of the waypoint that the question is about. The right page is the waypoint page of that waypoint, so that you can check that you are deleting the right one. It closes with the question.
</Field>

<Field id="waypoint-page" title="Rest of the waypoint page">
	The rest of the waypoint page that the question shows on the right side. For a supplemental waypoint these are the reference waypoint, radial and distance (shown once the unit has found the nearest VOR) and the position of the waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="ent" title="Enter prompt">
	`ENT` flashes while the unit waits for <kbd>ENT</kbd> to delete the waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification of the waypoint page on the right side. A user-defined airport shows APT 1, a VOR the VOR page, an NDB the NDB page, an intersection the INT page and a supplemental waypoint the SUP page.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>
