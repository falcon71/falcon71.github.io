<Field id="message" title="RAIM not available">
	The receiver cannot check its position for errors, because it has too few measurements or the satellites stand in a poor geometry. The unit posts it en route or in the approach arm mode, once RAIM has been missing for more than 10 seconds; in the approach active mode another message takes its place. The position is still used for navigation; cross-check it with other navigation. The message stays while the condition lasts. See [RAIM](/kln90b/operation/gps-receiver/#raim).
</Field>
<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The navigation mode; `ENR-LEG` is Enroute-Leg mode. It stays in the status line while the MSG page is shown.
</Field>
<Field id="msg" title="Message prompt">
	<Term t="MSG" /> in inverse video: the unit holds messages. It is steady once you have read them all and flashes while one is unread. Pressing <kbd>MSG</kbd> shows the next page of messages or closes the MSG page.
</Field>
