<Field id="state" title="GPS state">
	`STATE` and the GPS state. With a lower antenna gain the unit keeps navigating as long as four measurements remain, here with the altitude input (`NAV A`).
</Field>

<Field id="list" title="Satellites">
	The satellites with their numbers, signal strengths and elevations. With a lower antenna gain every satellite reads lower than usual by the same amount, so that even a satellite high in the sky reads in the mid-30s, and the low ones are lost (`*` and `00`). The loss does not change with the place, the height or the attitude of the aircraft.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the left side. `STA+1` means that the list continues on a second page.
</Field>
