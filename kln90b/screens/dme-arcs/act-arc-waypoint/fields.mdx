<Field id="top" title="Top line of the ACT page">
	From the left: the arrow that marks the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint), the number of the waypoint in FPL 0, its identifier, and `S` for a supplemental waypoint. A DME arc waypoint shows on the SUP page, as every waypoint that the unit creates by itself.
</Field>

<Field id="ref" title="Reference VOR of the arc">
	The reference VOR of the arc, the VOR that the DME arc is flown around and that the radial and the distance below are measured from.
</Field>

<Field id="rad" title="Radial from the VOR">
	The magnetic radial of the DME arc waypoint from the reference VOR of the arc, in degrees and tenths, with the published magnetic variation of the VOR. The name of the waypoint carries the same radial in whole degrees.
</Field>

<Field id="dis" title="Distance from the VOR">
	The distance of the DME arc waypoint from the VOR in NM, to a tenth: the radius of the arc. The letter at the end of the name stands for the same radius in whole NM.
</Field>

<Field id="lat" title="Latitude">
	The latitude of the DME arc waypoint, in degrees and minutes to hundredths.
</Field>

<Field id="lon" title="Longitude">
	The longitude of the DME arc waypoint, in degrees and minutes to hundredths.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`ACT`, the ACT page of a waypoint type with a single page.
</Field>
