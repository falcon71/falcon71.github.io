<Field id="from" title="From waypoint">
	The en route waypoint in front of the approach. While the question is open, the page shows no active leg symbol.
</Field>

<Field id="header" title="Approach header">
	The [approach header](/kln90b/pages/fpl/#procedure-headers), the line that names the approach.
</Field>

<Field id="move" title="MOVE question">
	`MOVE` and a question mark replace the DME arc waypoint of the approach under the cursor. <kbd>ENT</kbd> moves the waypoint to where your actual track meets the arc, or shows `NO INTRCEPT` where it does not ([Moving the DME arc waypoint](/kln90b/operation/dme-arcs/#moving-the-dme-arc-waypoint)). <kbd>CLR</kbd> again withdraws the question, and so does a turn of a left knob or turning the cursor off.
</Field>

<Field id="approach" title="Waypoints of the approach">
	The next waypoints of the approach, each with its number and a blank in place of the colon. The first is the end of the arc.
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Last waypoint">
	The last waypoint of FPL 0, the destination airport. While the list is longer than the page, the last waypoint stays in the bottom row and the rows between scroll.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>
