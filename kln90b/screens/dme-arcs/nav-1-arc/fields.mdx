<Field id="leg" title="Active leg">
	The active leg, from the DME arc waypoint where the arc starts, through the arrow, to the end fix of the arc, the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint). The arrow flashes during [waypoint alerting](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#waypoint-alerting).
</Field>

<Field id="cdi" title="D-bar">
	The D-bar shows how far the aircraft is from the arc, the curved active leg, and stands on the side you must fly to. Full deflection is the [CDI scale factor](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-cdi-scale-factor). The center of the scale is the TO/FROM indicator.
</Field>

<Field id="dis" title="Distance to the end fix">
	`DIS` is the direct distance to the end fix of the arc in NM, measured straight across the arc and not along it, so it is shorter than the way you still fly. It shows one decimal below 100 NM.
</Field>

<Field id="gs" title="Groundspeed">
	<Term t="GS">Your groundspeed</Term> in kt, in whole knots.
</Field>

<Field id="ete" title="Estimated time en route">
	The <Term t="ETE">time to the end fix</Term> at your present groundspeed, worked out from the direct distance, so it is shorter than the time the arc takes. Below one hour the hours stay blank, and `:06` is 6 minutes.
</Field>

<Field id="brg" title="Bearing to the end fix">
	<Term t="BRG">`BRG`</Term> is the magnetic bearing from your position straight to the end fix of the arc, in whole degrees. On an arc it differs from the desired track, which follows the arc, by up to half the angle of the arc that is still ahead.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`NAV 1`, the page on the right side.
</Field>
