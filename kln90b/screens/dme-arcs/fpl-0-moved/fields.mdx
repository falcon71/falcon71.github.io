<Field id="from" title="From waypoint">
	The airport of the SID, with the tail of the active leg symbol in front of it. The active leg still starts here and runs as a straight leg to the moved DME arc waypoint, not along your track.
</Field>

<Field id="header" title="Procedure header">
	The header of the SID: the name of the <Term t="SID" />, a dash and `SID`. The shaft of the active leg symbol in front of it shows that the active leg runs through the header.
</Field>

<Field id="arc-waypoint" title="Moved DME arc waypoint">
	The DME arc waypoint after `MOVE ?`: the point where the present track met the arc, with a new name that carries the radial of that point ([Moving the DME arc waypoint](/kln90b/operation/dme-arcs/#moving-the-dme-arc-waypoint)). It is still the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint) and keeps its number.
</Field>

<Field id="end-fix" title="End fix of the arc">
	The fix where the DME arc ends. The arc now starts at the moved DME arc waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="papps" title="Last waypoint of the SID">
	The last waypoint of the SID and of FPL 0.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`FPL 0`, the active flight plan.
</Field>
