<Field id="leg" title="Active waypoint">
	The end of the arc, still the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint) after the change to OBS mode. The place of the from waypoint is blank, because the course line starts at no waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="obs" title="Selected course">
	The [selected course](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-selected-course), magnetic, in place of <Term t="DTK">`DTK`</Term>. The unit lays it as a straight line through the end of the arc, not along the arc.
</Field>

<Field id="tk" title="Actual track">
	`TK` is your actual track, magnetic, in whole degrees.
</Field>

<Field id="fly" title="Direction to fly and cross-track error">
	The direction to fly, `L` or `R`, and the <Term t="XTK">cross-track error</Term> from the straight course line through the end of the arc, in whole NM from 10 NM. On an arc that line lies far from you, so the D-bar is at full deflection.
</Field>

<Field id="msa" title="Minimum safe altitude">
	The minimum safe altitude of the one-degree sector the aircraft is in, in whole hundreds of feet ([Minimum safe altitudes](/kln90b/pages/nav/#minimum-safe-altitudes)).
</Field>

<Field id="esa" title="Minimum en route safe altitude">
	The minimum en route safe altitude, in OBS mode for the way to the active waypoint only ([Minimum safe altitudes](/kln90b/pages/nav/#minimum-safe-altitudes)).
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`NAV 3`, the page on the left side.
</Field>
