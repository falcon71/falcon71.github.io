<Field id="title" title="Title">
	The title of the page. The rows below give the position of the aircraft as a radial and a distance from a VOR and as latitude and longitude.
</Field>

<Field id="vor" title="Reference VOR of the arc ahead and radial">
	The reference VOR of a DME arc ahead on FPL 0, and the magnetic radial from it to the aircraft, in whole degrees (`FR` is from). It takes the place of the nearby VOR even where another VOR is nearer; [NAV 2 and Super NAV 5 on an arc](/kln90b/operation/dme-arcs/#nav-2-and-super-nav-5-on-an-arc) says how far ahead the arc may lie.
</Field>

<Field id="dist" title="Distance from the reference VOR of the arc">
	The direct distance from the aircraft to the reference VOR of the arc, in NM with tenths. Compare it with the radius of the arc to see how far you are from it.
</Field>

<Field id="lat" title="Latitude">
	The latitude of the present position as hemisphere, degrees and minutes to hundredths.
</Field>

<Field id="lon" title="Longitude">
	The longitude of the present position as hemisphere, degrees and minutes to hundredths.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`NAV 2`, the page on the left side.
</Field>
