<Field id="title" title="Title">
	The title of the page. The rows below give the position of the aircraft as a radial and a distance from a VOR and as latitude and longitude.
</Field>

<Field id="vor" title="Reference VOR of the arc and radial">
	The reference VOR of the DME arc and the magnetic radial from it to the aircraft, in whole degrees (`fr` is from), measured at the VOR with its published magnetic variation. While the active leg is a DME arc, or the DME arc waypoint of an arc lies within 30 NM ahead on FPL 0, the reference VOR of the arc takes the place of the nearby VOR ([NAV 2 and Super NAV 5 on an arc](/kln90b/operation/dme-arcs/#nav-2-and-super-nav-5-on-an-arc)).
</Field>

<Field id="dist" title="Distance from the reference VOR of the arc">
	The direct distance from the aircraft to the reference VOR of the arc, in NM with tenths. On the arc it equals the radius of the arc, and you can fly the arc by it as by a DME.
</Field>

<Field id="lat" title="Latitude">
	The latitude of the present position as hemisphere, degrees and minutes to hundredths.
</Field>

<Field id="lon" title="Longitude">
	The longitude of the present position as hemisphere, degrees and minutes to hundredths.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`NAV 2`, the page on the left side.
</Field>
