<Field id="from" title="From waypoint">
	The airport of the <Term t="SID" />, an en route waypoint, with the tail of the active leg symbol in front of it. The active leg runs from it through the header to the DME arc waypoint, as a straight leg.
</Field>

<Field id="header" title="Procedure header">
	The header of the SID: the name of the <Term t="SID" />, a dash and `SID`. The shaft of the active leg symbol in front of it shows that the active leg runs through the header. A header counts as no waypoint and has no number.
</Field>

<Field id="arc-waypoint" title="DME arc waypoint">
	The [DME arc waypoint](/kln90b/operation/dme-arcs/#the-dme-arc-waypoint) where the arc starts, here the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint), with the head of the active leg symbol in front of it. The period after the number shows that it belongs to the <Term t="SID" />. <kbd>CLR</kbd> on the DME arc waypoint of a SID or a <Term t="STAR" /> asks `DEL` as on any waypoint; [Moving the DME arc waypoint](/kln90b/operation/dme-arcs/#moving-the-dme-arc-waypoint) says where to move it instead.
</Field>

<Field id="end-fix" title="End of the arc">
	The fix at the end of the DME arc. The leg from the DME arc waypoint to this fix is the arc.
</Field>

<Field id="papps" title="Last waypoint of the SID">
	The last waypoint of the <Term t="SID" /> and of FPL 0. The leg to it from the end of the arc is a straight leg.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`FPL 0`, the active flight plan.
</Field>
