<Field id="title" title="Procedure header">
	The procedure you selected: the name of the <Term t="SID" />, a dash and `SID`.
</Field>

<Field id="arc-waypoint" title="DME arc waypoint">
	The waypoint where the unit starts the DME arc. The unit computes it when this list appears, from where the aircraft is at that moment, and it takes the place of the published start fix of the arc, which is not in the list. Its name is `D`, the magnetic radial from the VOR of the arc in three digits and a letter for the radius of the arc, `I` for 9 NM ([The name of a DME arc waypoint](/kln90b/operation/dme-arcs/#the-name-of-a-dme-arc-waypoint)).
</Field>

<Field id="end-fix" title="End fix of the arc">
	The fix where the DME arc ends. The unit flies the leg from the DME arc waypoint to this fix along the arc.
</Field>

<Field id="papps" title="Last waypoint of the procedure">
	The waypoint of the transition after the arc, the last waypoint of the SID. The leg to it from the end fix is a straight leg.
</Field>

<Field id="load" title="Load prompt">
	`LOAD IN FPL` loads the procedure into FPL 0 with the waypoints shown, the DME arc waypoint included, when you press <kbd>ENT</kbd>.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the right cursor is on.
</Field>
