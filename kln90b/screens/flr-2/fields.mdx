<Field id="heading" title="Heading of the list">
	`ACTIVE` heads the list of the failures that are active now, one per row, by name. The list is sorted by the characters of the names, so digits come before letters. Five rows are visible; with more failures the list scrolls under the cursor. The entries shown depend on the equipment of the aircraft.
</Field>

<Field id="first" title="An active failure">
	`429 OUTPUT`, the ARINC 429 output of the unit has failed. [What can fail](/kln90b/operation/failures/#what-can-fail) explains every name.
</Field>

<Field id="second" title="An active failure">
	`ALTITUDE`, the altitude input from the aircraft has failed.
</Field>

<Field id="third" title="An active failure">
	`RMI OUTPUT`, the output that drives the RMI has failed.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the left side.
</Field>
