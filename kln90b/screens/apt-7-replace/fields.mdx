<Field id="title" title="Procedure selected">
	The procedure you are about to load: its name, a dash and `SID` for a departure, a small `STAR` for an arrival, or
	the approach name on APT 8.
</Field>

<Field id="question" title="Question to replace a procedure">
	FPL 0 holds a procedure of the same kind already, and FPL 0 takes only one departure, one arrival and one approach.
	The unit asks before the new procedure replaces the old one, also when both are the same. The row above
	`APPROVE?` names the kind: `SID`, `STAR` or, on APT 8, `APPROACH`.
</Field>

<Field id="approve" title="Approval">
	`APPROVE?` under the cursor. Press <kbd>ENT</kbd> to remove the old procedure from FPL 0 and load the new one, or
	<kbd>CLR</kbd> to go back to the waypoints of the procedure and keep FPL 0 as it is.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="Cursor indication">
	`CRSR` shows that the right cursor is on. It stays on at every step of the selection, so you do not press the right
	<kbd>CRSR</kbd>.
</Field>
