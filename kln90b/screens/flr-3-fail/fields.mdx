<Field id="heading" title="Heading of the list">
	`INACTIVE` heads the list of the failures that you can fail now. The entries shown depend on the equipment of the aircraft.
</Field>

<Field id="question" title="Fail question">
	`FAIL` and a question mark, flashing in the place of the failure's name. Press <kbd>ENT</kbd> to fail the part, which then moves to the list of the active failures, or <kbd>CLR</kbd> to withdraw the question. Moving the cursor away, turning it off or a change of the list from elsewhere withdraws it too.
</Field>

<Field id="entry-2" title="An inactive failure">
	`ALTITUDE`, the altitude input from the aircraft. FLR offers it only in an aircraft whose unit has an altitude input and no air data input.
</Field>

<Field id="entry-3" title="An inactive failure">
	`ANT GAIN`, the gain of the GPS antenna.
</Field>

<Field id="entry-4" title="An inactive failure">
	`BATTERY`, the internal battery of the unit.
</Field>

<Field id="entry-5" title="An inactive failure">
	`EEPROM`, the memory that holds the calibration of the unit.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`CRSR` in place of the page name tells you that the left cursor is on.
</Field>
