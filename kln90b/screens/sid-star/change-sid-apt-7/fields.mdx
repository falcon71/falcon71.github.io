<Field id="plan" title="FPL 0">
	FPL 0 with its cursor off and the <Term t="SID" /> under its procedure header. The SID stays in FPL 0 until another SID replaces it or you delete it.
</Field>

<Field id="airport" title="Airport of the procedure">
	The airport of the <Term t="SID" />. `CHANGE SID?` puts it on the APT pages, whatever airport they showed before.
</Field>

<Field id="title" title="List heading">
	`SELECT SID` heads the list of the <Term t="SID" />s of the airport. A <Term t="STAR" /> header opens the page of STARs, `SELECT STAR`.
</Field>

<Field id="list" title="SIDs of the airport">
	The <Term t="SID" />s the unit offers for the airport, numbered from 1; the bottom row shows the last one. The right cursor is off: press the right <kbd>CRSR</kbd> to select a SID.
</Field>

<Field id="page-l" title="Left page identification">
	`FPL 0`, the active flight plan.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation, which shows the mode of the unit. <Term t="ENR-LEG">`ENR-LEG`</Term> is Enroute-Leg mode.
</Field>

<Field id="page-r" title="Right page identification">
	`APT+7`: APT 7, and the `+` shows that the airport has a second APT 7 page, the page of STARs.
</Field>
