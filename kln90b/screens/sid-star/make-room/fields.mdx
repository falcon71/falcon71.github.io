<Field id="text" title="Question to make room for a procedure">
	The question the unit asks when a procedure you load does not fit into FPL 0. It counts the deletions the load needs, from 1: each takes the first waypoint of the plan, and an airport takes the <Term t="SID" /> waypoints behind it along in the same deletion. When the load needs more than one, each <kbd>ENT</kbd> moves the number on to the next.
</Field>

<Field id="continue" title="Confirmation">
	`CONTINUE?` flashes in inverse video. <kbd>ENT</kbd> on the last question deletes the waypoints and loads the procedure; nothing is deleted before. <kbd>CLR</kbd> or the left <kbd>CRSR</kbd> cancels the load and leaves FPL 0 as it was. The knobs do nothing while the question is shown.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>
