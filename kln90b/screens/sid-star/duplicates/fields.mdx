<Field id="from" title="From waypoint">
	An en route waypoint, the waypoint the active leg starts from, with the tail of the active leg symbol in front of it. The colon after the number shows that you entered it.
</Field>

<Field id="to" title="Active waypoint">
	The [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint), an en route waypoint, with the head of the active leg symbol in front of it.
</Field>

<Field id="header" title="Procedure header">
	The header of a <Term t="STAR" />: its name, a dash and `STAR`. The STAR stands in front of its airport, after the en route waypoints.
</Field>

<Field id="copies" title="The same waypoints in the STAR">
	The first waypoints of the <Term t="STAR" />, where its <Term t="Transition">transition</Term> begins. They repeat the two en route waypoints above, so FPL 0 holds each of them twice: with a colon as en route waypoints, with a period as waypoints of the STAR. Delete the copies you do not need.
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Destination airport">
	The airport of the <Term t="STAR" />, the last waypoint of FPL 0. While the list is longer than the page, the last waypoint stays in the bottom row and the rows between scroll.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`FPL 0`, the active flight plan.
</Field>
