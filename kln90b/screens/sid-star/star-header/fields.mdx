<Field id="from" title="From waypoint">
	The waypoint that the active leg starts from, with the tail of the active leg symbol in front of it.
</Field>

<Field id="header" title="Procedure header">
	The header of a <Term t="STAR" />: its name, a dash and `STAR`. A header counts as no waypoint and has no number.
</Field>

<Field id="to" title="Active waypoint">
	The first waypoint of the <Term t="STAR" />, where its <Term t="Transition">transition</Term> begins, as the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint), with the head of the active leg symbol in front of it. The shaft of the symbol runs through the header.
</Field>

<Field id="star" title="Waypoints of the STAR">
	The next waypoints of the <Term t="STAR" />, each with its number in the flight plan and a period.
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Destination airport">
	The airport of the <Term t="STAR" />, the last waypoint of FPL 0. The STAR stands in front of it.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`FPL 0`, the active flight plan.
</Field>
