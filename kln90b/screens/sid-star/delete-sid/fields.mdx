<Field id="plan" title="Waypoints of FPL 0">
	The waypoints around the header: the airport of the <Term t="SID" /> above it, the waypoints of the SID below it, and the last waypoint of FPL 0 in the bottom row. While the question is open, the unit shows no active leg symbol.
</Field>

<Field id="delete" title="Question to delete the procedure">
	`DELETE SID?`, `DELETE STAR?` or `DELETE APR?` replaces the header after <kbd>CLR</kbd> and flashes in inverse video. <kbd>ENT</kbd> deletes every waypoint of the procedure from FPL 0, and its header with them; the airport stays. A second <kbd>CLR</kbd>, or moving the cursor off the row, withdraws the question.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>
