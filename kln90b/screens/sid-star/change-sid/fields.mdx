<Field id="plan" title="Waypoints of FPL 0">
	The waypoints around the header: the airport of the <Term t="SID" /> above it, the waypoints of the SID below it with a period after their numbers, and the last waypoint of FPL 0 in the bottom row. The active leg symbol keeps its tail and its head while the cursor rests on the header, but the header carries no shaft.
</Field>

<Field id="change" title="Question to change the procedure">
	The cursor on a procedure header replaces the name with `CHANGE SID?`, `CHANGE STAR?` or `CHANGE APR?`, flashing in inverse video. <kbd>ENT</kbd> turns the left cursor off and shows on the right side the APT 7 page of the airport of the procedure, at its <Term t="SID" />s or its <Term t="STAR" />s, or APT 8 for an approach, so that you can select the procedure again. <kbd>CLR</kbd> turns the row into the question to delete the procedure, and a step of the left inner knob opens a new en route waypoint in front of the header.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>
