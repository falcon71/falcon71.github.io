<Field id="ident" title="Airport identifier">
	The airport of the APT pages. To look at another airport, turn the cursor on and enter its identifier here.
</Field>

<Field id="none" title="No SIDs or STARs">
	The database has no SID and no STAR that the unit can fly for this airport. Procedures the unit cannot fly
	are left out, so an airport that has only such procedures shows this text too.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`APT 7` names the page. It has no `+`, because an airport without SIDs and STARs has one APT 7 page.
</Field>
