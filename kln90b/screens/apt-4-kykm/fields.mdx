<Field id="ident" title="Airport identifier">
	The identifier of the airport. It is the same on every APT page and is changed in the same way as on APT 1.
</Field>

<Field id="approach" title="Approach control frequency">
	The frequency of approach control, labeled `APR`, in MHz with two decimals (`123.80`).
</Field>

<Field id="atis" title="ATIS frequency">
	The frequency of the <Term t="ATIS" />, labeled `ATIS`, in MHz with two decimals.
</Field>

<Field id="clearance" title="Clearance delivery frequency">
	The frequency of clearance delivery, labeled `CLR`, in MHz with two decimals. The label also covers remote clearance delivery.
</Field>

<Field id="ctaf" title="CTAF frequency">
	The common traffic advisory frequency of the airport, labeled <Term t="CTAF" />, in MHz with two decimals.
</Field>

<Field id="ground" title="Ground control frequency">
	The frequency of ground control, labeled `GRND`, in MHz with two decimals.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page name">
	The name of the page on the right side. `APT+4` means that APT 4 has more than one subpage, because the airport has more than five frequencies.
</Field>
