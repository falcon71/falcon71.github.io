<Field id="ident" title="Airport identifier">
	The identifier of the airport. It is the same on every APT page and is changed in the same way as on APT 1.
</Field>

<Field id="approach" title="Approach control frequency">
	The frequency of approach control (frequency type `APR`), in MHz with two decimals (`123.80`).
</Field>

<Field id="atis" title="ATIS frequency">
	The frequency of the <Term t="ATIS" /> (frequency type `ATIS`), in MHz with two decimals.
</Field>

<Field id="clearance" title="Clearance delivery frequency">
	The frequency of clearance delivery (frequency type `CLR`), in MHz with two decimals. The frequency type also covers remote clearance delivery.
</Field>

<Field id="ctaf" title="CTAF frequency">
	The common traffic advisory frequency of the airport (frequency type <Term t="CTAF" />), in MHz with two decimals.
</Field>

<Field id="ground" title="Ground control frequency">
	The frequency of ground control (frequency type `GRND`), in MHz with two decimals.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side. `APT+4` means that APT 4 has more than one page, because the airport has more than five frequencies.
</Field>
