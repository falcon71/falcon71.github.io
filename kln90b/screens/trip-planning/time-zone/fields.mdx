<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`DATE/TIME` heads the page: the clock of the unit and its time zone.
</Field>

<Field id="date" title="Date">
	The date of the unit, in the time zone shown below it.
</Field>

<Field id="time" title="Time">
	The time of the unit in hours and minutes, 00:00 to 23:59, in the time zone beside it. It changes with every step of the time zone.
</Field>

<Field id="seconds" title="Seconds">
	The seconds of the time. They count on by themselves and cannot be entered.
</Field>

<Field id="zone" title="Time zone, with the cursor on">
	The code of the time zone in which the unit shows the date and the time, with the cursor on it. The left inner knob steps through the zones around the list, and the change takes effect at once, so you need no <kbd>ENT</kbd>. The same zone applies on every page that shows a time.
</Field>

<Field id="zonename" title="Name of the time zone">
	The name of the time zone in words, such as `CORD UNIV/Z` for `UTC` and `PACIFIC STD` for `PST`.
</Field>

<Field id="cursor" title="The CRSR annunciation">
	`CRSR` takes the place of the page identification of the left side while that side's cursor is on.
</Field>
