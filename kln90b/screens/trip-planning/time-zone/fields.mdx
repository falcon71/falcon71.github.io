<Field id="dest" title="The destination waypoint">
	The identifier of the destination waypoint, the last waypoint of the active flight plan or, with an approach in FPL 0, its <Term t="MAP">missed approach point</Term>; with a direct-to outside the flight plan, the direct-to waypoint. While the destination waypoint is the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint), an arrow stands in front of it, and during a direct-to to the destination waypoint the Direct To symbol stands there instead. The identifier is blank while the unit has no destination waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="zone" title="Time zone, with the cursor on">
	The time zone of the departure time, the present time and the ETA, with the cursor on it. The right inner knob chooses the next zone clockwise and the previous one counterclockwise, around the list, and the times change with every step. You need no <kbd>ENT</kbd>.
</Field>

<Field id="dep" title="Departure time">
	`DEP` is the time at which the flight timer started, in the time zone of the field at the top. It shows dashes until then, and the unit keeps the time until it is turned off.
</Field>

<Field id="time" title="Present time">
	`TIME` is the present time, as hours and minutes of a 24-hour clock, in the time zone of the field at the top.
</Field>

<Field id="eta" title="ETA at the destination waypoint">
	<Term t="ETA">`ETA`</Term> is the estimated time of arrival at the destination waypoint, in the time zone of the field at the top.
</Field>

<Field id="flt" title="Flight time">
	`FLT` is the time the flight timer has counted, as hours and minutes. It does not depend on the time zone.
</Field>

<Field id="ete" title="Time to the destination waypoint">
	<Term t="ETE">`ETE`</Term> is the estimated time en route to the destination waypoint. It does not depend on the time zone.
</Field>

<Field id="rname" title="The CRSR annunciation">
	`CRSR` takes the place of the page identification of the right side while that side's cursor is on.
</Field>
