<Field id="message" title="Navigation flagged in the approach">
	The unit has flagged its navigation in approach active mode, for a lost position, an error that RAIM found, or a lack of RAIM. Press the GPS APR switch to return to approach arm mode and get navigation back for the missed approach. See [`PRESS GPS APR FOR NAV`](/kln90b/operation/approaches/#press-gps-apr-for-nav).
</Field>

<Field id="raim" title="RAIM lost in approach active mode">
	RAIM has not been available for a while in approach active mode, whether for too few satellites or for their geometry. See [Messages of the receiver](/kln90b/operation/gps-receiver/#messages-of-the-receiver).
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	<Term t="APR-LEG">`APR-LEG`</Term>: Approach Active-Leg mode, the approach is active. See [Activating the approach](/kln90b/operation/approaches/#activating-the-approach).
</Field>

<Field id="msg" title="Message prompt">
	<Term t="MSG">`MSG`</Term> in inverse video: the unit holds messages. <kbd>MSG</kbd> shows the next one or closes the page after the last.
</Field>
