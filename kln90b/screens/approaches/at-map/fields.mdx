<Field id="faf" title="Final approach fix">
	The <Term t="FAF" /> of the approach, with the suffix `-F`. The tail of the [active leg symbol](/kln90b/pages/fpl/#the-active-leg-symbol) in front of it marks it as the waypoint the active leg starts from.
</Field>

<Field id="map" title="Missed approach point">
	The <Term t="MAP" /> of the approach, here the threshold of runway 17, with the suffix `-M`. The head of the [active leg symbol](/kln90b/pages/fpl/#the-active-leg-symbol) marks it as the active waypoint. The unit does not sequence past it, so it stays the active waypoint after you pass it.
</Field>

<Field id="fence" title="No waypoint sequencing">
	The fence: `*NO WPT SEQ` follows the missed approach point. The unit does not sequence on to the waypoints behind it by itself; a direct-to takes you into the missed approach ([The missed approach](/kln90b/operation/approaches/#the-missed-approach)).
</Field>

<Field id="missed" title="Waypoint of the missed approach">
	The first waypoint of the missed approach, which has no role of its own and no suffix. After the missed approach point the Direct To page offers it.
</Field>

<Field id="mahp" title="Missed approach holding point">
	The <Term t="MAHP" />, where the missed approach ends in a holding pattern, with the suffix `-H`. The unit does not fly the holding pattern; near the fix it raises `IF REQUIRED SELECT OBS`.
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Last waypoint of FPL 0">
	The last waypoint of FPL 0, here the destination airport, which follows the approach as an en route waypoint. While the list is longer than the page, the last waypoint stays in the bottom row and the rows between scroll.
</Field>

<Field id="r-leg" title="Active leg">
	The waypoint the active leg starts at, an arrow and the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint). After a direct-to the leg starts at the Direct To symbol. A waypoint of an approach shows without its suffix.
</Field>

<Field id="r-cdi" title="D-bar">
	The D-bar shows the cross-track error from the desired track: the bar stands on the side the course lies on, so you fly toward it. Each of the five dots on either side is a fifth of the CDI scale factor, which the approach modes narrow by themselves ([The CDI scale factor along the approach](/kln90b/operation/approaches/#the-cdi-scale-factor-along-the-approach)). The TO/FROM indicator in the middle shows TO while the active waypoint lies ahead, FROM once it lies behind. `F L A G` in place of the dots means that the unit cannot navigate.
</Field>

<Field id="r-dis" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	The distance to the active waypoint, in NM. Past the waypoint it is the distance back to it.
</Field>

<Field id="r-gs" title="Groundspeed">
	The groundspeed, in kt.
</Field>

<Field id="r-ete" title="Estimated time en route">
	The time to the active waypoint at the present groundspeed, as hours and minutes. Below one hour the hours stay blank. Past the waypoint it is the time back to it at the present groundspeed, so it grows as you fly on.
</Field>

<Field id="r-brg" title="Bearing to the active waypoint">
	The magnetic bearing from the aircraft to the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="lpage" title="Page identification of the left side">
	The page identification of the left page, `FPL 0`.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	<Term t="APR-LEG">`APR-LEG`</Term>: Approach Active-Leg mode, the approach is active. See [Activating the approach](/kln90b/operation/approaches/#activating-the-approach).
</Field>

<Field id="rpage" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification of the right page, `NAV 1`.
</Field>
