<Field id="ident" title="Airport identifier">
	The airport whose approaches APT 8 shows, the same airport as on the other APT pages, followed by `IAP` for instrument approach procedures. An arrow in front of it means that the airport is the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint).
</Field>

<Field id="approaches" title="Approaches in the list">
	The approaches the unit offers for the airport, numbered from 1 in the order of the database. Each name has the kind of approach (<Term t="RNAV" />, `GPS`, `VOR`, `VORD` for VOR/DME, `NDB`) and the runway. ILS approaches and the others the unit leaves out are not in the list ([Which approaches the unit offers](/kln90b/operation/approaches/#which-approaches-the-unit-offers)). Press the right <kbd>CRSR</kbd> to put the cursor on the first approach, and <kbd>ENT</kbd> to select one.
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Last approach in the list">
	The last approach the unit offers for the airport. On a list longer than the page, the bottom row always shows the last approach, so its number tells you how many there are.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`APT 8` names the page. With the cursor on, this field shows `CRSR`.
</Field>
