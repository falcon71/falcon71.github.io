<Field id="dto-title" title="DIRECT TO annunciation">
	`DIRECT TO:` at the top of the Direct To page shows that the page makes the waypoint you approve the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="dto-ident" title="Default waypoint">
	The waypoint that the page offers, flashing in inverse video while the unit waits for <kbd>ENT</kbd> to approve the waypoint page on the right side. Past the missed approach point it is the first waypoint of the missed approach ([The missed approach](/kln90b/operation/approaches/#the-missed-approach)). The left inner knob starts a new entry, and <kbd>CLR</kbd> blanks the identifier.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>
