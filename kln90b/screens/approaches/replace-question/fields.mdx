<Field id="title" title="Approach header">
	The approach you are about to load, named as FPL 0 names it: the first letter of the kind of approach, the runway, a dash and the airport ([APT 8: approaches](/kln90b/pages/apt/#apt-8-approaches) lists the forms).
</Field>

<Field id="question" title="Question to replace the approach">
	FPL 0 holds an approach already, and it takes only one. The unit asks before the new approach replaces the old one, also when both are the same approach.
</Field>

<Field id="approve" title="Approval">
	`APPROVE?` under the cursor. Press <kbd>ENT</kbd> to remove the old approach from FPL 0 and load the new one, or <kbd>CLR</kbd> to go back to the waypoints of the approach and keep FPL 0 as it is.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	`CRSR` shows that the right cursor is on. It stays on at every step of the selection, so you do not press the right <kbd>CRSR</kbd>.
</Field>
