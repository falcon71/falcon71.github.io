<Field id="title" title="Mode is active">
	`ACTIVE MODE` shows that the unit navigates in [leg mode](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#leg-mode), the course mode of this page. In Approach Arm-Leg mode and Approach Active-Leg mode the page shows it as well.
</Field>

<Field id="leg" title="Leg mode">
	`LEG` names the course mode of this page. MOD 1 is the page of leg mode, MOD 2 the page of [OBS mode](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#obs-mode).
</Field>

<Field id="scale" title="CDI scale factor">
	The [CDI scale factor](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-cdi-scale-factor), at which the D-bar and the external indicator reach full scale. Approach arm mode sets ±1.00 NM by itself near the airport; with the cursor on it, the left inner knob selects ±0.30 NM instead.
</Field>

<Field id="r-leg" title="Active leg">
	The waypoint the active leg starts at, an arrow and the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint). After a direct-to the leg starts at the Direct To symbol. A waypoint of an approach shows without its suffix.
</Field>

<Field id="r-cdi" title="D-bar">
	The D-bar shows the cross-track error from the desired track: the bar stands on the side the course lies on, so you fly toward it. Each of the five dots on either side is a fifth of the CDI scale factor, which the approach modes narrow by themselves ([The CDI scale factor along the approach](/kln90b/operation/approaches/#the-cdi-scale-factor-along-the-approach)). The TO/FROM indicator in the middle shows TO while the active waypoint lies ahead, FROM once it lies behind. `F L A G` in place of the dots means that the unit cannot navigate.
</Field>

<Field id="r-dis" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	The distance to the active waypoint, in NM.
</Field>

<Field id="r-gs" title="Groundspeed">
	The groundspeed, in kt.
</Field>

<Field id="r-ete" title="Estimated time en route">
	The time to the active waypoint at the present groundspeed, as hours and minutes. Below one hour the hours stay blank.
</Field>

<Field id="r-brg" title="Bearing to the active waypoint">
	The magnetic bearing from the aircraft to the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="lpage" title="Page identification of the left side">
	The page identification of the left page, `MOD 1`.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	<Term t="ARM-LEG">`ARM-LEG`</Term>: Approach Arm-Leg mode, the approach is armed. See [Arming the approach](/kln90b/operation/approaches/#arming-the-approach).
</Field>

<Field id="rpage" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification of the right page, `NAV 1`.
</Field>
