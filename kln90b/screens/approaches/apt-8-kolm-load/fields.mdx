<Field id="title" title="Approach header">
	The approach you selected, named as FPL 0 names it: the first letter of its kind (`R` for <Term t="RNAV" />, `G` for GPS, `V` for VOR, `D` for VOR/DME, `N` for NDB), the runway, a dash and the airport.
</Field>

<Field id="iaf" title="Initial approach fix">
	The <Term t="IAF" /> you selected, with the suffix `-I` after the identifier. It is usually the first waypoint of the list; where the transition begins before the IAF, the suffix sits on a later waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="waypoints" title="Waypoint of the approach">
	A waypoint between the IAF and the final approach fix, here the intermediate fix, without a suffix. Turn the right outer knob counterclockwise to move the cursor up into the list and scroll it; the entries cannot be selected or changed here.
</Field>

<Field id="faf" title="Final approach fix">
	The <Term t="FAF" /> of the approach, with the suffix `-F`.
</Field>

<Field id="mahp" title="Missed approach holding point">
	The last waypoint of the approach, here the <Term t="MAHP" /> with the suffix `-H`, where the missed approach ends in a holding pattern. The bottom row always shows the last waypoint, so its number tells you how many waypoints the approach adds to FPL 0.
</Field>

<Field id="load" title="Load prompt">
	`LOAD IN FPL` loads the approach into FPL 0 when you press <kbd>ENT</kbd>. If the airport is not in FPL 0, or FPL 0 holds an approach already, the unit first asks a question on this page.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	`CRSR` shows that the right cursor is on. It stays on at every step of the selection, so you do not press the right <kbd>CRSR</kbd>.
</Field>
