<Field id="from" title="First waypoint of FPL 0">
	The first waypoint of FPL 0, here the departure airport, an en route waypoint with a colon after its number. While the delete question is shown, the page shows no active leg symbol.
</Field>

<Field id="delete" title="Question to delete the approach">
	`DELETE APR?`, flashing in inverse video, asks whether to delete the approach. <kbd>ENT</kbd> deletes every waypoint of the approach and its header; the airport stays in FPL 0. <kbd>CLR</kbd> again withdraws the question.
</Field>

<Field id="iaf" title="Initial approach fix">
	The <Term t="IAF" /> of the approach, with the suffix `-I`. The waypoints of an approach have a blank in place of the colon after the number.
</Field>

<Field id="if" title="Intermediate fix">
	A waypoint of the approach without a role of its own, here the intermediate fix. It has no suffix.
</Field>

<Field id="faf" title="Final approach fix">
	The <Term t="FAF" /> of the approach, with the suffix `-F`. The approach becomes active near it ([Activating the approach](/kln90b/operation/approaches/#activating-the-approach)).
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Last waypoint of FPL 0">
	The last waypoint of FPL 0, here the destination airport, which follows the approach as an en route waypoint. While the list is longer than the page, the last waypoint stays in the bottom row and the rows between scroll.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>
