<Field id="title" title="Approach header">
	The approach you are about to load, named as FPL 0 names it: the first letter of the kind of approach, the runway, a dash and the airport ([APT 8: approaches](/kln90b/pages/apt/#apt-8-approaches) lists the forms).
</Field>

<Field id="question" title="Question to add the airport">
	The airport of the approach is not in FPL 0. The unit asks before it adds the airport at the end of FPL 0, with the approach in front of it. `APPR` is short for approach.
</Field>

<Field id="approve" title="Approval">
	`APPROVE?` under the cursor. Press <kbd>ENT</kbd> to add the airport and the approach to FPL 0, or <kbd>CLR</kbd> to go back to the waypoints of the approach and keep FPL 0 as it is.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	`CRSR` shows that the right cursor is on. It stays on at every step of the selection, so you do not press the right <kbd>CRSR</kbd>.
</Field>
