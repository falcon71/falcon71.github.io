<Field id="message" title="Approach not activated for RAIM">
	The unit has not made the approach active, because RAIM is not available now, has found an error, or is predicted not available at the FAF or the MAP. The unit stays in approach arm mode. Predict RAIM on STA 5. See [`RAIM NOT AVAILABLE` `APR MODE INHIBITED`](/kln90b/operation/approaches/#raim-not-available-apr-mode-inhibited).
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	<Term t="ARM-LEG">`ARM-LEG`</Term>: Approach Arm-Leg mode, the approach is armed. See [Arming the approach](/kln90b/operation/approaches/#arming-the-approach).
</Field>

<Field id="msg" title="Message prompt">
	<Term t="MSG">`MSG`</Term> in inverse video: the unit holds messages. <kbd>MSG</kbd> shows the next one or closes the page after the last.
</Field>
