<Field id="title" title="Approach header">
	The approach you selected, named as FPL 0 names it: the first letter of the kind of approach, the runway, a dash and the airport ([APT 8: approaches](/kln90b/pages/apt/#apt-8-approaches) lists the forms).
</Field>

<Field id="label" title="IAF list label">
	`IAF` labels the list of the transitions of the approach, each beginning at an initial approach fix (<Term t="IAF" />) or at a fix before one. You must select one entry; no entry starts the approach later.
</Field>

<Field id="carro" title="Transition under the cursor">
	The entry the cursor marks, named after the first waypoint of its transition. Press <kbd>ENT</kbd> to select it and see the waypoints of the approach from there, or <kbd>CLR</kbd> to go back to the list of approaches.
</Field>

<Field id="iafs" title="Transitions in the list">
	The other transitions of the approach, each with its number and named after its first waypoint. Where that waypoint is a VOR before the IAF, such as `SEA`, the IAF suffix appears on a later waypoint of the approach.
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Last transition in the list">
	The last transition of the approach. On a list longer than the page, `IAF` stays on the top row and the bottom row always shows the last entry.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	`CRSR` shows that the right cursor is on. It stays on at every step of the selection, so you do not press the right <kbd>CRSR</kbd>.
</Field>
