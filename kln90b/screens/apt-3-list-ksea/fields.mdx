<Field id="ident" title="Airport identifier">
	The identifier of the airport. It is the same on every APT page and is changed in the same way as on APT 1.
</Field>

<Field id="designations" title="Runway designations">
	The designations of the two ends of a runway, each padded to three characters and separated by `/` (`16L/34R`). An end without a letter keeps a blank in its place (`03 /21 `).
</Field>

<Field id="lighting" title="Runway lighting">
	The lighting of the runway: `L` for full-time lighting, `LPC` for lighting that you turn on by radio, `LPT` for part-time lighting. It is blank when the runway has no lighting or the lighting is not known.
</Field>

<Field id="length" title="Runway length">
	The length of the runway in feet, as a whole number of feet, followed by `'`.
</Field>

<Field id="surface" title="Runway surface">
	The surface of the runway as a three-letter code:

	- `HRD`: concrete, asphalt, bituminous, brick, tarmac or macadam
	- `TRF`: grass or turf
	- `SNW`: snow
	- `ICE`: ice
	- `DRT`: dirt
	- `GRV`: gravel
	- `MAT`: steel mats
	- `SND`: sand
	- `SHL`: shale

	Every other surface, water included, shows nothing.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page name">
	The name of the page on the right side. `APT+3` means that APT 3 has more than one subpage.
</Field>
