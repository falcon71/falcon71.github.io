<Field id="ident" title="Airport identifier">
	The airport whose approaches APT 8 shows, followed by <Term t="IAP" />, and the same airport as on the other APT
	pages. To change it, turn the cursor on, turn the right outer knob counterclockwise from the first approach until the
	cursor is on the identifier, and enter the new one.
</Field>

<Field id="approaches" title="Approaches in the list">
	The approaches the unit offers for the airport, numbered from 1 in the order of the database. Each name has the kind
	of approach (<Term t="RNAV" />, `GPS`, `VOR`, <Term t="VORD" />, `NDB`) and the runway; an approach without a runway shows a
	name such as `VOR-A`. The letter of an approach such as RNAV (GPS) Y is not shown. Press the right <kbd>CRSR</kbd> to
	put the cursor on the first approach, and <kbd>ENT</kbd> to select one.
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Last approach in the list">
	The bottom row always shows the last approach of the list, so its number tells you how many there are. The rows
	above it scroll as you move the cursor with the right outer knob.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page name">
	`APT 8` names the page. With the cursor on, this field shows `CRSR`.
</Field>
