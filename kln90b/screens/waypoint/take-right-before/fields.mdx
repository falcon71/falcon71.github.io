<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`INIT POSN` heads SET 1, where you give the unit a position.
</Field>

<Field id="lpos" title="Position the unit believes">
	The latitude and longitude that the unit believes it is at, as `N 47°26.99'` and `W122°18.71'`.
</Field>

<Field id="speed" title="Groundspeed and track">
	The groundspeed in knots and the track in degrees.
</Field>

<Field id="confirm" title="Confirmation of the position">
	`CONFIRM?` takes the position above as the position of the unit, once a waypoint or a position has been entered.
</Field>

<Field id="wpt" title="Waypoint for the position">
	The identifier of a waypoint whose position the unit takes as its own, with the label `WPT:`. With the cursor on it and no entry open, <kbd>ENT</kbd> takes the waypoint that the right page shows and shows its waypoint page for approval. The field is blank while it holds no waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="ident" title="Identifier of the airport">
	The identifier of the airport on the right page.
</Field>

<Field id="name" title="Airport name">
	The name of the airport in two rows of 11 characters, cut at the eleventh character even in the middle of a word.
</Field>

<Field id="class" title="Airspace of the airport">
	`CL B` shows that the airport lies inside the outline of a class B airspace.
</Field>

<Field id="pos" title="Airport position">
	The position of the airport: latitude above, longitude below, as `N 47°31.80'` and `W122°18.12'`.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation, which shows the mode of the unit. `ENR-LEG` is Enroute-Leg mode.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side.
</Field>
