<Field id="ident" title="Identifier of the waypoints">
	The identifier that you entered and that more than one waypoint has.
</Field>

<Field id="count" title="Number of waypoints">
	The number of waypoints that have the identifier.
</Field>

<Field id="header" title="Column titles">
	`TYPE` heads the waypoint type of each entry and `AREA` the country of its region.
</Field>

<Field id="first" title="Nearest waypoint of the list">
	The first entry of the list, the waypoint nearest to the present position. An entry shows its number in the list, its waypoint type (`APT`, `VOR`, `NDB`, `INT` or `SUP`) and its area, and ends with a question mark: the unit asks whether this is the waypoint you mean. The cursor is on the entry and flashes; <kbd>ENT</kbd> chooses it.

	The area is the country of the region the waypoint belongs to, `USA` for every region of the United States. It is blank for a user waypoint, which has no region of a country, and for a region that the unit has no country for.
</Field>

<Field id="second" title="Further waypoint of the list">
	An entry after the first: number, waypoint type, area and a question mark, as on the first entry. The outer knob of the side the page stands on moves the cursor to it, and <kbd>ENT</kbd> chooses it.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the cursor of this side is on. The Duplicate Waypoint page turns its cursor on by itself.
</Field>
