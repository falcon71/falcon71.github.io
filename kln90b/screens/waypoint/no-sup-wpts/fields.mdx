<Field id="ident" title="Identifier of the waypoint">
	The identifier of the waypoint that the SUP page shows. A unit without a user waypoint has nothing to show, and the page shows `0` in its place.
</Field>

<Field id="offer" title="Offer to create a waypoint">
	`CREATE NEW WPT AT:` offers to create a [user waypoint](/kln90b/operation/user-waypoints/) with the identifier above, which is `0` when the list is empty. Turn the right cursor on and enter an identifier to make a name of your own.
</Field>

<Field id="user" title="Create at a position you enter">
	`USER POS?` creates the waypoint at the latitude and longitude that you enter.
</Field>

<Field id="pres" title="Create at the present position">
	`PRES POS?` creates the waypoint at once at the position the unit holds, which is the position of the aircraft once the unit is NAV ready.
</Field>

<Field id="message" title="Status line message">
	`NO SUP WPTS` replaces the middle of the status line in inverse video when you select a waypoint page whose list holds no waypoint. The same message exists for the other waypoint types, as `NO APT WPTS`, `NO VOR WPTS`, `NO NDB WPTS` and `NO INT WPTS`.
</Field>

<Field id="other" title="Present position">
	The present position, as NAV 2 shows it: the radial and the distance from the nearby VOR, then the latitude and the longitude.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the left side.
</Field>
