<Field id="title" title="Approach selected">
	The approach you selected: the first letter of its kind, the runway, a dash and the airport. FPL 0 shows the same name
	above the approach's waypoints once it is loaded.
</Field>

<Field id="sondr" title="Initial approach fix">
	The <Term t="IAF" /> you selected, marked with `-i` after the identifier. It is usually the first waypoint of the
	list; where the transition begins before the IAF, the mark sits on a later waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="waypoints" title="Waypoints of the approach">
	Waypoints between the IAF and the final approach fix, in the order you fly them, without a mark. Turn the right outer
	knob counterclockwise to move the cursor up into the list and scroll it; the entries cannot be selected or changed
	here.
</Field>

<Field id="lofal" title="Missed approach holding point">
	The last waypoint of the approach. When the missed approach ends in a hold there, `-h` after the identifier marks it
	as the <Term t="MAHP" />. The bottom row of the list always shows the last waypoint, so its number
	tells you how many waypoints the approach adds to FPL 0.
</Field>

<Field id="load" title="Load prompt">
	`LOAD IN FPL` loads the approach into FPL 0 when you press <kbd>ENT</kbd>. The cursor is on it when the waypoints
	appear. If the airport is not in FPL 0, or FPL 0 holds an approach already, the unit first asks a question on this
	page.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="Cursor indication">
	`CRSR` shows that the right cursor is on. It stays on at every step of the selection, so you do not press the right
	<kbd>CRSR</kbd>.
</Field>
