<Field id="heading" title="Heading of the list">
	`ACTIVE` heads the list of the failures that are active now. The entries shown depend on the equipment of the aircraft.
</Field>

<Field id="question" title="Repair question">
	`REPAIR` and a question mark, flashing in the place of the failure's name. Press <kbd>ENT</kbd> to repair the failure, which then leaves the list, or <kbd>CLR</kbd> to withdraw the question. Moving the cursor away, turning it off or a change of the list from elsewhere withdraws it too.
</Field>

<Field id="second" title="An active failure">
	`ALTITUDE`, the altitude input from the aircraft has failed.
</Field>

<Field id="third" title="An active failure">
	`RMI OUTPUT`, the output that drives the RMI has failed.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`CRSR` in place of the page name tells you that the left cursor is on.
</Field>
