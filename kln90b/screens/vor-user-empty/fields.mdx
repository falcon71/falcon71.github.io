<Field id="ident" title="Identifier that no VOR has">
	The identifier you entered. No VOR has it, so the page shows no waypoint and holds what you typed. The VOR page makes no offer to create a waypoint: you define a user-defined VOR by entering its position. [Creating a user VOR or a user NDB](/kln90b/operation/user-waypoints/#creating-a-user-vor-or-a-user-ndb) explains the steps.
</Field>

<Field id="class" title="Class of the station">
	`U` for undefined, because the page shows no station.
</Field>

<Field id="freq" title="Frequency">
	Underscores: no frequency is entered. The frequency is optional, and you can enter it before or after the position. The cursor stands on this field, the first one after the identifier.
</Field>

<Field id="magvar" title="Published magnetic variation">
	Underscores: no variation is entered. The variation is optional too. If you leave it out, the unit takes the magnetic variation at the position of the waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="lat" title="Latitude">
	Underscores: no latitude is entered. The waypoint is created when you have entered both the latitude and the longitude.
</Field>

<Field id="lon" title="Longitude">
	Underscores: no longitude is entered. The waypoint is created when you have entered both the latitude and the longitude.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	`CRSR` in place of the right page identification means that the right cursor is on.
</Field>
