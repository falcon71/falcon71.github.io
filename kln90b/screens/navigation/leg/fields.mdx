<Field id="from" title="From waypoint">
	The waypoint the active leg starts at. After a direct-to it shows the Direct To symbol, because the leg starts where you were when you made the direct-to. In OBS mode it is blank.
</Field>

<Field id="arrow" title="Active leg arrow">
	The `›` points from the waypoint the leg starts at to the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint).
</Field>

<Field id="to" title="Active waypoint">
	The identifier of the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint), the waypoint the unit navigates to.
</Field>

<Field id="cdi" title="D-bar">
	The D-bar shows the cross-track error from the desired track: the bar stands on the side the course lies on, so you fly toward it. Each of the five dots on either side is a fifth of the CDI scale factor, and the outer dot is full scale; beyond it the bar stays at the end. The TO/FROM indicator in the middle shows TO while the active waypoint lies ahead, FROM once it lies behind. `F L A G` in place of the dots means that the unit cannot navigate, for example without an active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="dis" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	The distance to the active waypoint, in NM. Dashes without an active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="gs" title="Groundspeed">
	The groundspeed, in kt.
</Field>

<Field id="ete" title="Estimated time en route">
	The time to the active waypoint at the present groundspeed, as hours and minutes. Below one hour the hours stay blank. Dashes without an active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="brg" title="Bearing to the active waypoint">
	The magnetic bearing from the aircraft to the active waypoint. Dashes without an active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="r-leg" title="Active leg">
	The waypoint the active leg starts at, the `›` and the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint), as on NAV 1. In OBS mode the waypoint the leg starts at is blank.
</Field>

<Field id="r-dtk" title="Desired track or selected course">
	In leg mode `DTK` and the desired track, the magnetic course of the active leg at the point abeam the aircraft. In OBS mode `OBS` and the selected course; a colon after `OBS` shows that the cursor can change the course here as on MOD 2. Where the unit reads the external indicator, the course flashes while the indicator is more than 10° off it. See [The selected course](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-selected-course).
</Field>

<Field id="r-tk" title="Actual track">
	The actual track of the aircraft over the ground, magnetic.
</Field>

<Field id="r-fly" title="Cross-track error">
	The cross-track error from the course, in NM, with `L` or `R` for the direction to fly to get back on it.
</Field>

<Field id="r-msa" title="Minimum safe altitude">
	The minimum safe altitude of the sector the aircraft is in, in ft. [Minimum safe altitudes](/kln90b/pages/nav/#minimum-safe-altitudes) explains it.
</Field>

<Field id="r-esa" title="Minimum en route safe altitude">
	The highest minimum safe altitude along the way, in ft. [Minimum safe altitudes](/kln90b/pages/nav/#minimum-safe-altitudes) explains which way it covers.
</Field>

<Field id="lpage" title="Page identification of the left side">
	The page identification of the left page, `NAV 1`.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	<Term t="ENR-LEG">`ENR-LEG`</Term>: Enroute-Leg mode. See [The mode annunciations](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-mode-annunciations).
</Field>

<Field id="rpage" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification of the right page, `NAV 3`.
</Field>
