<Field id="title" title="Prompt to change the mode">
	`PRESS ENT` `TO ACTIVATE`, in an aircraft without a GPS CRS switch. The unit is in [leg mode](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#leg-mode). <kbd>ENT</kbd> changes it to [OBS mode](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#obs-mode), with the cursor on or off, and the enter prompt `ENT` shows in the status line while this page is shown. Without an active waypoint the unit stays in leg mode and shows `NO ACTV WPT`. With a GPS CRS switch, the page shows `PRESS GPS` `CRS FOR`: the switch changes the mode, and <kbd>ENT</kbd> does nothing.
</Field>

<Field id="course" title="Selected course">
	In leg mode the course shows dashes, because only OBS mode has a selected course. The colon after `OBS` does not depend on the mode: it shows where you set the selected course on the unit, as in `OBS:---°`, and is missing where the unit reads it from the external indicator, as in `OBS ---°`. In an aircraft whose unit reads the indicator, the colon appears while the KLN 90B is not the displayed navigation source. See [The selected course](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-selected-course).
</Field>

<Field id="scale" title="CDI scale factor">
	The [CDI scale factor](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-cdi-scale-factor): the cross-track error at which the D-bar and the external indicator reach full scale. With the cursor on it, the left inner knob selects another one.
</Field>

<Field id="r-cdi" title="D-bar">
	The D-bar shows the cross-track error from the desired track: the bar stands on the side the course lies on, so you fly toward it. Each of the five dots on either side is a fifth of the CDI scale factor, and the outer dot is full scale; beyond it the bar stays at the end. The TO/FROM indicator in the middle shows TO while the active waypoint lies ahead, FROM once it lies behind. `F L A G` in place of the dots means that the unit cannot navigate, for example without an active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="r-dis" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	The distance to the active waypoint, in NM. Dashes without an active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="r-gs" title="Groundspeed">
	The groundspeed, in kt.
</Field>

<Field id="r-ete" title="Estimated time en route">
	The time to the active waypoint at the present groundspeed, as hours and minutes. Below one hour the hours stay blank. Dashes without an active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="r-brg" title="Bearing to the active waypoint">
	The magnetic bearing from the aircraft to the active waypoint. Dashes without an active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="lpage" title="Page identification of the left side">
	The page identification of the left page, `MOD 2`.
</Field>

<Field id="msg" title="Status line message">
	`NO ACTV WPT`: there is no active waypoint for OBS mode. It replaces the middle part of the status line for a moment.
</Field>

<Field id="rpage" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification of the right page, `NAV 1`.
</Field>
