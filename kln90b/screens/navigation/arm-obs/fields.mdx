<Field id="title" title="Mode is active">
	`ACTIVE MODE` shows that the unit navigates in [OBS mode](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#obs-mode), the course mode of this page.
</Field>

<Field id="course" title="Selected course">
	The selected course of OBS mode, as a magnetic course. Without a colon after `OBS` it is the course of the external indicator, which the unit reads: turn the indicator to change it. The cursor skips it. See [The selected course](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-selected-course).
</Field>

<Field id="scale" title="CDI scale factor">
	The [CDI scale factor](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-cdi-scale-factor): the cross-track error at which the D-bar and the external indicator reach full scale. With the cursor on it, the left inner knob selects another one.
</Field>

<Field id="r-leg" title="Active leg">
	The waypoint the active leg starts at, the `›` and the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint). After a direct-to the leg starts at the Direct To symbol, and in OBS mode it starts blank.
</Field>

<Field id="r-cdi" title="D-bar">
	The D-bar shows the cross-track error from the selected course: the bar stands on the side the course lies on, so you fly toward it. Each of the five dots on either side is a fifth of the CDI scale factor, and the outer dot is full scale; beyond it the bar stays at the end. The TO/FROM indicator in the middle shows TO while the active waypoint lies ahead, FROM once it lies behind. `F L A G` in place of the dots means that the unit cannot navigate, for example without an active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="r-dis" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	The distance to the active waypoint, in NM. Dashes without an active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="r-gs" title="Groundspeed">
	The groundspeed, in kt.
</Field>

<Field id="r-ete" title="Estimated time en route">
	The time to the active waypoint at the present groundspeed, as hours and minutes. Below one hour the hours stay blank. Dashes without an active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="r-brg" title="Bearing to the active waypoint">
	The magnetic bearing from the aircraft to the active waypoint. In OBS mode the unit measures it at the active waypoint, with the magnetic variation there, so that you can compare it with the selected course. Dashes without an active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="lpage" title="Page identification of the left side">
	The page identification of the left page, `MOD 2`.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	<Term t="ARM:">`ARM:`</Term> and a three-digit course: Approach Arm-OBS mode, with the selected course as a magnetic course. See [The mode annunciations](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-mode-annunciations).
</Field>

<Field id="rpage" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification of the right page, `NAV 1`.
</Field>
