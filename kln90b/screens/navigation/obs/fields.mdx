<Field id="to" title="Active waypoint">
	The `›` and the identifier of the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint). In OBS mode the waypoint the leg starts at stays blank, because the selected course passes through the active waypoint from no waypoint in particular.
</Field>

<Field id="cdi" title="D-bar">
	The D-bar shows the cross-track distance from the selected course: the bar stands on the side the course lies on, so you fly toward it. Each of the five dots on either side is a fifth of the CDI scale factor, and the outer dot is full scale; beyond it the bar stays at the end. The TO/FROM indicator in the middle shows TO while the active waypoint lies ahead, FROM once it lies behind. `F L A G` in place of the dots means that the unit cannot navigate, for example without an active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="dis" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	The distance to the active waypoint, in NM. Dashes without an active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="gs" title="Groundspeed">
	The groundspeed, in kt.
</Field>

<Field id="ete" title="Estimated time en route">
	The time to the active waypoint at the present groundspeed, as hours and minutes. Below one hour the hours stay blank. Dashes without an active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="brg" title="Bearing to the active waypoint">
	The magnetic bearing from the aircraft to the active waypoint. In OBS mode the unit measures it at the active waypoint, with the magnetic variation there, so that you can compare it with the selected course. Dashes without an active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="r-leg" title="Active leg">
	The waypoint the active leg starts at, the `›` and the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint), as on NAV 1. In OBS mode the waypoint the leg starts at is blank.
</Field>

<Field id="r-dtk" title="Desired track or selected course">
	In leg mode `DTK` and the desired track, the magnetic course of the active leg at the point abeam the aircraft. In OBS mode `OBS` and the selected course; a colon after `OBS` shows that the cursor can change the course here as on MOD 2. Where the unit reads the external indicator, the course flashes while the indicator is more than 10° off it. See [The selected course](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-selected-course).
</Field>

<Field id="r-tk" title="Track">
	The magnetic track of the aircraft over the ground.
</Field>

<Field id="r-fly" title="Cross-track distance">
	The cross-track distance from the course, in NM, with `L` or `R` for the direction to fly to get back on it.
</Field>

<Field id="r-msa" title="Minimum safe altitude">
	The minimum safe altitude of the area around the aircraft, in ft.
</Field>

<Field id="r-esa" title="En route safe altitude">
	The en route safe altitude to the active waypoint and, in leg mode, along the rest of FPL 0, in ft. In OBS mode it covers the way to the active waypoint only.
</Field>

<Field id="lpage" title="Page identification of the left side">
	The page identification of the left page, `NAV 1`.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	<Term t="ENR:">`ENR:`</Term> and a three-digit course: Enroute-OBS mode, with the selected course as a magnetic course. See [The mode annunciations](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-mode-annunciations).
</Field>

<Field id="rpage" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification of the right page, `NAV 3`.
</Field>
