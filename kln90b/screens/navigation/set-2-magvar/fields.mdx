<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`DATE/TIME` heads the page: the clock of the unit and its time zone.
</Field>

<Field id="date" title="Date">
	The date of the unit: day, month and year in two digits, in the time zone shown below it. The cursor can change it only while the unit has no position.
</Field>

<Field id="time" title="Time">
	The time of the unit in hours and minutes, in the time zone beside it. The cursor can change it only while the unit has no position.
</Field>

<Field id="seconds" title="Seconds">
	The seconds of the time.
</Field>

<Field id="zone" title="Time zone">
	The code of the time zone in which the unit shows the date and the time.
</Field>

<Field id="zonename" title="Name of the time zone">
	The name of the time zone in words, such as `CORD UNIV/Z` for `UTC`.
</Field>

<Field id="magvar" title="Magnetic variation">
	`MAG V` and the magnetic variation you entered, 0° to 99° east (`E`) or west (`W`). The line shows only while the unit has no magnetic variation of its own, outside the primary coverage area; `0°` means that none is entered, and the unit shows true courses. With the cursor on it, the left inner knob opens an entry, which takes effect with <kbd>ENT</kbd>. See [Magnetic variation](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#magnetic-variation).
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the left page, `SET 2`.
</Field>
