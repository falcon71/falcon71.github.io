<Field id="title" title="Approach selected">
	The approach you selected, named as FPL 0 names it: the first letter of its kind (`R` for <Term t="RNAV" />, `G` for
	GPS, `V` for VOR, `D` for VOR/DME, `N` for NDB), the runway, a dash and the airport. An approach without a runway
	shows its name instead, such as `VOR-A-KYKM`.
</Field>

<Field id="label" title="IAF list label">
	`IAF` labels the list of the approach's transitions, each beginning at an initial approach fix (<Term t="IAF" />) or
	at a fix before one. The approach always starts at an IAF, and you must select one entry.
</Field>

<Field id="cidug" title="Initial approach fix">
	A transition of the approach with its number, named after its first waypoint, which is an <Term t="IAF" /> or a fix
	before one. The list has five
	rows; a longer list scrolls with `IAF` staying on the top row, and its bottom row always shows the last IAF.
</Field>

<Field id="sondr" title="IAF under the cursor">
	The <Term t="IAF" /> the cursor marks. Press <kbd>ENT</kbd> to select it and see the waypoints of the approach from there, or
	<kbd>CLR</kbd> to go back to the list of approaches.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="Cursor indication">
	`CRSR` shows that the right cursor is on. It stays on at every step of the selection, so you do not press the right
	<kbd>CRSR</kbd>.
</Field>
