<Field id="present" title="Present position heading">
	`PRESENT POS` heads the left page, which shows where the aircraft is.
</Field>

<Field id="vor" title="Bearing from the nearest VOR">
	The radial of the nearest VOR that the aircraft is on, as the VOR's identifier and the course from the VOR in degrees magnetic, followed by `fr`.
</Field>

<Field id="vor-distance" title="Distance from the nearest VOR">
	The distance of the aircraft from that VOR in NM.
</Field>

<Field id="present-pos" title="Present position">
	The latitude and the longitude of the aircraft.
</Field>

<Field id="ident" title="Identifier of the new airport">
	The identifier that you entered for the airport that is being created. The airport does not exist until you have entered both coordinates.
</Field>

<Field id="lat" title="Latitude of the new airport">
	The latitude of the airport as `N`/`S`, two digits of degrees, two digits of minutes and two digits of hundredths of a minute. The first click of the right inner knob starts the entry and shows `N` (or `E` for the longitude); the right outer knob moves between the cells, and the right inner knob changes the cell. The degrees stop at 89, and the tens of minutes at 5. <kbd>ENT</kbd> accepts the latitude and moves the cursor to the longitude. The status line then shows `ENT LAT/LON`, a reminder that the airport is created only when both coordinates are entered. <kbd>CLR</kbd> with an entry open cancels it.
</Field>

<Field id="lon" title="Longitude of the new airport">
	The longitude of the airport as `E`/`W`, three digits of degrees, two digits of minutes and two digits of hundredths of a minute. It shows dashes until you enter it. A longitude beyond 180° is refused with `INVALID ENT`. <kbd>ENT</kbd> on the longitude creates the airport, shows it on APT 1 and turns the cursor off.
</Field>

<Field id="left-page" title="Page name of the left side">
	The name of the page on the left side.
</Field>

<Field id="message" title="Reminder to enter the position">
	`ENT LAT/LON` reminds you that the airport is created only when both the latitude and the longitude are entered. It replaces the navigation mode on the status line for about 5 seconds after you press <kbd>ENT</kbd> on one coordinate while the other is still empty, which includes the first <kbd>ENT</kbd> of the normal entry. It is not an error.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="Cursor indication">
	`CRSR` replaces the name of the page while the right cursor is on.
</Field>
