<Field id="ident" title="Identifier that no airport has">
	The characters you entered, which match no airport of the database and no user airport. The cursor is on the identifier, so that you can correct it.
</Field>

<Field id="offer" title="Offer to create a waypoint">
	`CREATE NEW WPT AT:` offers to create a <Term t="User waypoint">user airport</Term> with the identifier you entered. It appears on every APT page, and it hides the rest of the page. The two choices below it are fields of the cursor.
</Field>

<Field id="user" title="Create at a position you enter">
	`USER POS?` opens APT 1 with the cursor in the latitude field, so that you enter latitude and longitude of the new airport.
</Field>

<Field id="pres" title="Create at the present position">
	`PRES POS?` creates the airport at the position of the aircraft and shows it on APT 1.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="Cursor indication">
	`CRSR` replaces the name of the page while the right cursor is on.
</Field>
