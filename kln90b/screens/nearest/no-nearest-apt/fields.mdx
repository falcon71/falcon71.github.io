<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation; `ENR-LEG` is Enroute-Leg mode. It stays in the status line while the MSG page is shown.
</Field>

<Field id="msg" title="Message prompt">
	<Term t="MSG" /> stands in normal video, without the inverse of the [message prompt](/kln90b/operation/messages/#the-message-prompt), while the MSG page is open and holds no message. It stands in the status line while the MSG page is shown, also when the unit holds no message, and the page above it is blank. Pressing <kbd>MSG</kbd> again closes the page.
</Field>
