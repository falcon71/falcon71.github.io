<Field id="sv" title="Satellite number">
	The number of the fifth to eighth satellite of the list, under the heading `SV`, which this page repeats. The marks around the number are those of the first page.
</Field>

<Field id="snr" title="Signal-to-noise ratio">
	The signal strength of the satellite, as on the first page: two digits, and `00` for a satellite that the position does not use.
</Field>

<Field id="ele" title="Elevation of the satellite">
	The height of the satellite above the horizon in whole degrees, as on the first page.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the left side. `STA+1` is the name of both pages of STA 1: the left inner knob, while the cursor is off, steps from one to the other and then on to STA 2. When the list falls back to four satellites or fewer while this page is shown, the unit shows the first page again.
</Field>
