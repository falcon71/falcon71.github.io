<Field id="ident" title="Identifier of the user airport">
	The identifier of a [user airport](/kln90b/operation/waypoints/#user-waypoints).
</Field>

<Field id="title" title="Runway of the user airport">
	`RWY LEN` labels the one runway that a user airport has. Its length and surface are the two entries below it.
</Field>

<Field id="length" title="Runway length of the user airport">
	The length of the runway in feet. It shows `_____` until you enter a value. The range is 0 to 99,999 ft, in steps of 1 ft. With the cursor on the field, the right inner knob starts the entry and changes a digit, the right outer knob moves between the digits, and <kbd>ENT</kbd> stores the length and moves the cursor to the surface. The user airport appears in the nearest list only when the length meets the minimum that SET 3 sets.
</Field>

<Field id="surface" title="Runway surface of the user airport">
	The surface of the runway: <Term t="HRD">`HRD`</Term> for a hard surface or <Term t="SFT">`SFT`</Term> for a soft one. The right inner knob switches between the two with the cursor on the field, and <kbd>ENT</kbd> stores the choice and leaves the cursor on it. A soft runway shows as <Term t="TRF">`TRF`</Term> in the nearest list of APT 1. A runway whose surface you have not entered counts as soft, so with SET 3 set to hard surfaces only, the airport joins the nearest list after you choose `HRD`.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page name">
	The name of the page on the right side.
</Field>
