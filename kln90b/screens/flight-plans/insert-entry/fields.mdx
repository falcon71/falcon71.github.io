<Field id="use" title="Questions to activate the plan">
	`USE? INVRT?` stands on the top line of a flight plan that holds waypoints.
</Field>

<Field id="before" title="Waypoint before the new row">
	A waypoint of the plan that stays where it is.
</Field>

<Field id="entry" title="New waypoint being entered">
	A new row in front of a waypoint. The cursor marks the identifier, and the plan does not hold it yet. The waypoints behind it move down by one when it is approved.
</Field>

<Field id="after" title="Waypoints behind the new row">
	The waypoints that the new waypoint will stand in front of. Their numbers count up by one when the waypoint is approved.
</Field>

<Field id="blank" title="Blank waypoint position">
	The blank waypoint position after the last waypoint, where a waypoint is added at the end.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>
