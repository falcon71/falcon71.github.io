<Field id="text" title="Question to delete a waypoint">
	The question the unit asks when an addition does not fit into FPL 0. It names the waypoint that goes, always waypoint 1: the first waypoint of the plan, with its <Term t="SID" /> waypoints if it is an airport with a SID loaded.
</Field>

<Field id="continue" title="Confirmation">
	`CONTINUE?` flashes in inverse video. <kbd>ENT</kbd> deletes the first waypoint and, if the plan still has no room, asks again. <kbd>CLR</kbd> or the left <kbd>CRSR</kbd> cancels the addition and leaves the plan as it was.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>
