<Field id="count" title="Number of new waypoints">
	The number of new center waypoints that the unit computed for the flight plan on the left side. `NEW WPT` is the singular.
</Field>

<Field id="prompt" title="Prompt to insert the waypoints">
	<kbd>ENT</kbd> inserts the new waypoints into the flight plan. Until then the flight plan is as it was.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side. CTR 1 computes center waypoints for the flight plan on the left side.
</Field>
