<Field id="wpts" title="Waypoints of the flight plan">
	The waypoints of the plan, one per row, each with its number. The cursor marks the waypoint that the refused waypoint was to go in front of.
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Last waypoint of the flight plan">
	The last waypoint of the plan stays in the bottom row, and the rows between are skipped. It is the last waypoint a flight plan holds.
</Field>

<Field id="title" title="Title">
	The title of the page. The rows below give the position of the aircraft as a radial and a distance from a VOR and as latitude and longitude.
</Field>

<Field id="vor" title="Nearby VOR and radial">
	The nearby VOR that the position is related to and the magnetic radial from that VOR to the aircraft, in whole degrees (`fr` is from).
</Field>

<Field id="dist" title="Distance to the nearby VOR">
	The direct distance from the aircraft to the nearby VOR, in NM with tenths.
</Field>

<Field id="lat" title="Latitude">
	The latitude of the present position as hemisphere, degrees and minutes to hundredths.
</Field>

<Field id="lon" title="Longitude">
	The longitude of the present position as hemisphere, degrees and minutes to hundredths.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>

<Field id="message" title="Status line message">
	`FPL FULL` says that the plan holds as many waypoints as it can and refused the waypoint that you approved. The message shows for a few seconds, and the plan is as it was.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side. NAV 2 is the same page on the left and on the right side.
</Field>
