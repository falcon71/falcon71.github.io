<Field id="from" title="From waypoint">
	The tail of the active leg symbol stands at the from waypoint, the waypoint that the active leg comes from.
</Field>

<Field id="active" title="Active waypoint">
	The head of the active leg symbol stands at the active waypoint, the waypoint that the unit navigates to. It flashes during [waypoint alerting](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#waypoint-alerting).
</Field>

<Field id="blank" title="Blank waypoint position">
	The blank waypoint position after the last waypoint, where a waypoint is added at the end.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the left side. FPL 0 is the active flight plan.
</Field>
