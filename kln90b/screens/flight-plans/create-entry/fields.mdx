<Field id="load" title="Question to copy FPL 0">
	`LOAD FPL 0?` stands on the top line of an empty flight plan. <kbd>ENT</kbd> on it copies the en route waypoints of FPL 0 into the plan.
</Field>

<Field id="entry" title="Waypoint being entered">
	The number of the position and the identifier that is being entered. The cursor marks the identifier, and the plan does not hold it yet. <kbd>ENT</kbd> shows the waypoint page of the identifier on the right side for approval.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>
