<Field id="use" title="Questions to activate the plan">
	`USE? INVRT?` stands on the top line of a flight plan that holds waypoints.
</Field>

<Field id="before" title="Waypoint before the center waypoint">
	A waypoint of the plan that stays where it is.
</Field>

<Field id="center" title="Center waypoint">
	The waypoint that the unit inserted where the leg crosses the boundary of a Center. Its identifier is the identifier of the nearest VOR and a two-digit number.
</Field>

<Field id="after" title="Waypoint behind the center waypoint">
	The waypoint at the end of the leg that the center waypoint lies on.
</Field>

<Field id="blank" title="Blank waypoint position">
	The blank waypoint position after the last waypoint, where a waypoint is added at the end.
</Field>

<Field id="done" title="Message that the insertion is complete">
	The unit has inserted the center waypoints into the flight plan on the left side.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation, which shows the mode of the unit. `ENR-LEG` is Enroute-Leg mode.
</Field>

<Field id="left-page" title="Page identification of the left side">
	The page identification on the left side. FPL 1 is a numbered flight plan.
</Field>

<Field id="right-page" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification on the right side. CTR 1 computes center waypoints for the flight plan on the left side.
</Field>
