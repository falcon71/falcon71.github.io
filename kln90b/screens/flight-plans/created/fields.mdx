<Field id="use" title="Questions to activate the plan">
	`USE? INVRT?` stands on the top line of a flight plan that holds waypoints. <kbd>ENT</kbd> on `USE?` copies the plan into FPL 0, and <kbd>ENT</kbd> on `INVRT?` copies it in reverse order.
</Field>

<Field id="wpts" title="Waypoints of the flight plan">
	The waypoints of the plan, one per row, each with its number. The plan is stored as soon as a waypoint is approved.
</Field>

<Field id="blank" title="Blank waypoint position">
	The blank waypoint position after the last waypoint, where the next waypoint is added. A full plan has none.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>
