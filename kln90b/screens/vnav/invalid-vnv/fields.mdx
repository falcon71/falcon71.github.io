<Field id="title" title="VNAV state">
	`VNV ARMED`: VNAV is armed, and the descent point, or the point where a climb starts, is still too far ahead for the countdown ([Flying the vertical path](/kln90b/operation/vnav/#flying-the-vertical-path)).
</Field>

<Field id="ind" title="Indicated altitude">
	The indicated altitude from the altitude input of the aircraft, with the [baro setting](/kln90b/operation/alerts/#the-baro-setting) applied, rounded to 100 ft. It shows five digits and `00000` below 0 ft, and you cannot change it. While VNAV is armed, the unit works out the descent point from it.
</Field>

<Field id="sel" title="Selected altitude">
	The altitude the descent or climb ends at. Changing it stops VNAV. The altitude alert reads the selected altitude too ([Altitude alerting](/kln90b/operation/alerts/#altitude-alerting)).
</Field>


<Field id="dist" title="Offset">
	How far before the VNAV waypoint the descent or climb ends, in NM. Changing it stops VNAV.
</Field>

<Field id="angle" title="Angle">
	The angle of the vertical path VNAV uses, negative for a descent. While VNAV is armed or active, moving the cursor onto it changes nothing, and a digit you enter starts VNAV again at the new angle ([The angle](/kln90b/operation/vnav/#the-angle)).
</Field>
<Field id="wpt" title="VNAV waypoint">
	The waypoint the descent or climb is planned to, the active waypoint until you enter another one. A waypoint the field does not take gives `INVALID VNV` in the status line, and the old waypoint stays ([Which waypoint VNAV can use](/kln90b/operation/vnav/#which-waypoint-vnav-can-use)). A different waypoint stops VNAV.
</Field>

<Field id="from" title="From waypoint">
	The waypoint at the start of the active leg.
</Field>

<Field id="arrow" title="Active waypoint arrow">
	The arrow marks the waypoint after it as the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint).
</Field>

<Field id="to" title="Active waypoint">
	The active waypoint. The distance, the time and the bearing of the page refer to it.
</Field>

<Field id="cdi" title="D-bar">
	The D-bar shows how far the aircraft is from the active leg, on a scale of five dots on each side of the center. The center of the scale is the TO/FROM indicator.
</Field>

<Field id="dis" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	`DIS` is the direct distance to the active waypoint in NM.
</Field>

<Field id="gs" title="Groundspeed">
	Your groundspeed in kt, from the GPS.
</Field>

<Field id="ete" title="Estimated time en route">
	The time to the active waypoint at your present groundspeed. Below one hour the hours stay blank, and `:22` is 22 minutes.
</Field>

<Field id="brg" title="Bearing to the active waypoint">
	`BRG` is the magnetic bearing from your position to the active waypoint, in whole degrees.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> shows that the left cursor is on.
</Field>

<Field id="msg" title="INVALID VNV">
	`INVALID VNV`: the VNAV waypoint you entered is one that VNAV cannot use. The old waypoint stays, and VNAV goes on as before. The message replaces the middle part of the status line for a few seconds ([`INVALID VNV`](/kln90b/operation/vnav/#invalid-vnv)).
</Field>

<Field id="rpage" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification on the right side: `NAV 1`.
</Field>
