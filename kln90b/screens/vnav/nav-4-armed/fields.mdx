<Field id="title" title="VNAV armed">
	`VNV ARMED`: VNAV is armed, and the descent point, or the point where a climb starts, is still too far ahead for the countdown, which then takes its place ([Flying the vertical path](/kln90b/operation/vnav/#flying-the-vertical-path)).
</Field>

<Field id="ind" title="Indicated altitude">
	The indicated altitude from the altitude input of the aircraft, with the <Term t="BARO">altimeter baro setting</Term> applied, rounded to 100 ft. It shows five digits and `00000` below 0 ft, and you cannot change it. While VNAV is armed, the unit works out the descent point from it.
</Field>

<Field id="sel" title="Selected altitude">
	The altitude the descent or climb ends at. Changing it stops VNAV. The altitude alert reads the selected altitude too ([Altitude alerting](/kln90b/operation/alerts/#altitude-alerting)).
</Field>

<Field id="wpt" title="VNAV waypoint">
	The waypoint the descent or climb is planned to, the active waypoint until you enter another one. [Which waypoint VNAV can use](/kln90b/operation/vnav/#which-waypoint-vnav-can-use) says which waypoints the field takes. A different waypoint stops VNAV.
</Field>

<Field id="dist" title="Offset">
	How far before the VNAV waypoint the descent or climb ends, in NM. Changing it stops VNAV.
</Field>

<Field id="angle" title="Angle">
	The angle of the vertical path VNAV uses, negative for a descent. While VNAV is armed or active, moving the cursor onto it changes nothing, and a digit you enter starts VNAV again at the new angle ([The angle](/kln90b/operation/vnav/#the-angle)).
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification of the side that shows the page: `NAV 4`. NAV 4 is the same page on the left and on the right side.
</Field>
