<Field id="dto-title" title="DIRECT TO annunciation">
	`DIRECT TO:` at the top of the Direct To page shows that the page makes the waypoint you approve the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="dto-ident" title="Default waypoint">
	The waypoint that the page offers, flashing in inverse video while the unit waits for <kbd>ENT</kbd> to approve the waypoint page on the right side. <kbd>ENT</kbd> makes the direct-to. The left inner knob starts a new entry, which replaces the waypoint and drops the approval. <kbd>CLR</kbd> blanks the identifier.
</Field>

<Field id="ident" title="Identifier of the waypoint shown for approval">
	The identifier of the waypoint page shown for approval. A `›` before the identifier marks the [active waypoint](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-active-waypoint). The cursor cannot change the identifier here.
</Field>

<Field id="name" title="Airport name">
	The name of the airport in two rows. Long names are shortened, and a user-defined airport has no name.
</Field>

<Field id="class" title="Airspace and type of the airport">
	The row under the name: the class of the airspace the airport lies in (`CL B`, `CL C`, `TMA`, `CTA`), and at the right end `MILTRY` or `PRIVAT` for a military or a private airport.
</Field>

<Field id="pos" title="Airport position">
	The position of the airport: latitude above, longitude below, in degrees, minutes and hundredths of a minute.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="CRSR annunciation">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode annunciation, which shows the mode of the unit. `ENR-LEG` is Enroute-Leg mode.
</Field>

<Field id="ent" title="Enter prompt">
	`ENT` flashes while the unit waits for <kbd>ENT</kbd> to approve what it shows, such as a waypoint page shown for approval.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side. The right inner knob turns to the other pages of the waypoint before you approve it.
</Field>
