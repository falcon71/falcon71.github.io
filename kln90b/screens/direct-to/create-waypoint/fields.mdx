<Field id="title" title="Page title">
	`DIRECT TO:` shows that the page makes the waypoint you enter the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="left-ident" title="Identifier that no waypoint has">
	The identifier you entered. It matches no waypoint of any type. The entry stays open and keeps the identifier, with the left cursor on it. <kbd>CLR</kbd> blanks it and creates nothing.
</Field>

<Field id="ident" title="Identifier of the waypoint to create">
	The identifier of the [user waypoint](/kln90b/operation/user-waypoints/) that the unit offers to create. The right page is the SUP page, so the new waypoint is a supplemental waypoint. You change the identifier on the Direct To page, not here.
</Field>

<Field id="offer" title="Offer to create a waypoint">
	`CREATE NEW WPT AT:` offers to create a waypoint with the identifier on the left. The two choices below it are fields of the right cursor, which is already on the first.
</Field>

<Field id="user" title="Create at a position you enter">
	`USER POS?` opens the fields of the SUP page for a position: latitude and longitude, or a reference waypoint with a radial and a distance. The cursor is on this choice. <kbd>ENT</kbd> accepts it.
</Field>

<Field id="pres" title="Create at the present position">
	`PRES POS?` creates the waypoint at once at the present position the unit holds, which is the position of the aircraft once the unit is NAV ready. The right outer knob moves the cursor to it.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr-left" title="CRSR annunciation of the left side">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the left cursor is on.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation and enter prompt">
	`ENR-LEG` is the mode annunciation: Enroute-Leg mode. `ENT` flashes while the left entry waits for <kbd>ENT</kbd>.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr-right" title="CRSR annunciation of the right side">
	<Term t="CRSR">`CRSR`</Term> replaces the page identification while the right cursor is on.
</Field>
