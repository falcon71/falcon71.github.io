<Field id="from" title="Direct To symbol">
	The Direct To symbol stands in place of the from waypoint when the active leg is a direct-to that began at the position where you made it. The `›` marks the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="to" title="Active waypoint">
	The identifier of the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="cdi" title="D-bar">
	The D-bar moves along the scale with the cross-track distance from the desired track. On the center mark it shows that the aircraft is on the desired track, which is where a direct-to starts. The center mark shows whether the active waypoint lies ahead of the aircraft (to) or behind it (from).
</Field>

<Field id="dis" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	The distance to the active waypoint, in NM.
</Field>

<Field id="gs" title="Groundspeed">
	The groundspeed, in kt.
</Field>

<Field id="ete" title="Estimated time en route">
	The time to the active waypoint at the present groundspeed, as hours and minutes. Below one hour the hours stay blank.
</Field>

<Field id="brg" title="Bearing to the active waypoint">
	The magnetic bearing from the aircraft to the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the right side.
</Field>
