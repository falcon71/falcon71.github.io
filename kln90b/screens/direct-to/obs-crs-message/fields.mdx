<Field id="plan" title="Flight plan">
	The waypoints of FPL 0, one per row. The blank row at the end is where a waypoint is added.
</Field>

<Field id="active" title="Active waypoint">
	The identifier of the active waypoint, with the `›` that marks it.
</Field>

<Field id="cdi" title="D-bar">
	The D-bar moves along the scale with the cross-track error from the selected course. The center mark shows whether the active waypoint lies ahead of the aircraft (to) or behind it (from).
</Field>

<Field id="dis" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	The distance to the active waypoint, in NM.
</Field>

<Field id="gs" title="Groundspeed">
	The groundspeed, in kt.
</Field>

<Field id="ete" title="Estimated time en route">
	The time to the active waypoint at the present groundspeed, as hours and minutes. Below one hour the hours stay blank.
</Field>

<Field id="brg" title="Bearing to the active waypoint">
	The magnetic bearing from the aircraft to the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="lpage" title="Page identification of the left side">
	The page identification on the left side.
</Field>

<Field id="msg" title="Course message">
	The Direct To symbol followed by `CRS` and a course, as a magnetic course. It replaces the mode annunciation in the status line for a moment.
</Field>

<Field id="rpage" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification on the right side.
</Field>
