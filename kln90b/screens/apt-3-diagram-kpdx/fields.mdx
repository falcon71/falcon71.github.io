<Field id="runways" title="Runways">
	One line for each runway of the airport, drawn between the two ends of the runway. North is up. The unit scales the drawing so that all runways fit on the page; it shows no scale, no north arrow and no airport symbol.
</Field>

<Field id="labels" title="Runway designations">
	The designation of each runway end (`10R`, `28L`, `03`), written at the end of the line that it names.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page name">
	The name of the page on the right side. `APT+3` means that APT 3 has more than one subpage: this diagram first, and then the runway list.
</Field>
