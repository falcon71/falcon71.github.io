<Field id="mtbf" title="Mean time between random failures">
	`MTBF:` and the setting for random failures, `OFF` or 1 to 999 hours. Service does not change it.
</Field>

<Field id="battery" title="Charge of the internal battery">
	`BATTERY` and the charge of the internal battery in percent. Service sets it to 100.
</Field>

<Field id="service" title="Service button">
	The button `SERVICE?` is back after the service, and the cursor is off.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the left side.
</Field>

<Field id="right" title="Page identification of the right side">
	The page identification on the right side. The FLR pages replace only the left page.
</Field>

<Field id="serviced" title="Message that the unit was serviced">
	`SERVICED`, inverse, in the middle of the status line in place of the mode annunciation and of `msg` and `ent`, for 5 seconds after you confirmed the service.
</Field>
