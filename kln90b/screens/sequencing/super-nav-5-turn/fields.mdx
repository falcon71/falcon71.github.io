<Field id="distance" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	The distance to the active waypoint, in NM.
</Field>

<Field id="ident" title="Active waypoint">
	The identifier of the active waypoint. It flashes during [waypoint alerting](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#waypoint-alerting) before a turn and is steady from the beginning of turn anticipation.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	The mode of the unit, here <Term t="ENR-LEG">`ENR-LEG`</Term>: en route in leg mode.
</Field>

<Field id="groundspeed" title="Groundspeed">
	The groundspeed, in kt.
</Field>

<Field id="ete" title="Estimated time en route">
	The time to the active waypoint at the present groundspeed, as hours and minutes.
</Field>

<Field id="dtk" title="Desired track">
	The <Term t="DTK">desired track</Term>, as a magnetic course. From the beginning of turn anticipation it is the course of the next leg.
</Field>

<Field id="track" title="Track">
	The track the aircraft flies over the ground, as a magnetic course.
</Field>

<Field id="range" title="Map range scale">
	The range scale of the map in NM.
</Field>

<Field id="active-leg" title="Active leg">
	The active leg of FPL 0, with an arrow that points to the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="next-leg" title="Next leg">
	The next leg of FPL 0.
</Field>

<Field id="waypoints" title="Waypoints of the flight plan">
	The waypoints of FPL 0 on the map, each with its identifier.
</Field>

<Field id="aircraft" title="Aircraft">
	The position of the aircraft.
</Field>
