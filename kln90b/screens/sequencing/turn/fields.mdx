<Field id="leg" title="Active leg">
	The waypoint the active leg starts from, and the active waypoint after the `›`. The `›` flashes during [waypoint alerting](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#waypoint-alerting), and in an anticipated turn until the transition ends.
</Field>

<Field id="dtk" title="Desired track">
	The <Term t="DTK">desired track</Term>, as a magnetic course. From the beginning of turn anticipation it is the course of the next leg, although the waypoint you turn at is still the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="tk" title="Track">
	The track the aircraft flies over the ground, as a magnetic course.
</Field>

<Field id="fly" title="Direction and distance to the desired track">
	Which way to fly, `L` or `R`, and how far in NM, to get back onto the desired track. During turn anticipation it is measured from the transition onto the next leg.
</Field>

<Field id="msa" title="Minimum safe altitude">
	The minimum safe altitude, in ft, of the area the aircraft is in.
</Field>

<Field id="esa" title="En route safe altitude">
	The en route safe altitude, in ft: the highest minimum safe altitude along the way from the aircraft over the active waypoint and the rest of FPL 0.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the left side.
</Field>
