<Field id="leg" title="Active leg">
	The waypoint the active leg starts from, and the active waypoint after the arrow. The arrow flashes during [waypoint alerting](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#waypoint-alerting), and in an anticipated turn until the transition ends.
</Field>

<Field id="dtk" title="Desired track">
	The <Term t="DTK">desired track</Term>, as a magnetic course. From the beginning of turn anticipation it is the course of the next leg, although the waypoint you turn at is still the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="tk" title="Actual track">
	The actual track of the aircraft over the ground, as a magnetic course.
</Field>

<Field id="fly" title="Direction and distance to the desired track">
	Which way to fly, `L` or `R`, and how far in NM, to get back onto the desired track. During turn anticipation it is measured from the transition onto the next leg.
</Field>

<Field id="msa" title="Minimum safe altitude">
	The minimum safe altitude, in ft, of the sector the aircraft is in. [Minimum safe altitudes](/kln90b/pages/nav/#minimum-safe-altitudes) explains it.
</Field>

<Field id="esa" title="Minimum en route safe altitude">
	The highest minimum safe altitude along the way, in ft. [Minimum safe altitudes](/kln90b/pages/nav/#minimum-safe-altitudes) explains which way it covers.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the left side.
</Field>
