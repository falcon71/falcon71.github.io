<Field id="plan" title="Flight plan">
	The waypoints of FPL 0, one per row. The tail of the [active leg symbol](/kln90b/pages/fpl/#the-active-leg-symbol) in front of KPDX marks the waypoint the active leg starts from, and its head in front of KOLM the active waypoint. During [waypoint alerting](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#waypoint-alerting) the head flashes. The blank row at the end is where a waypoint is added.
</Field>

<Field id="from" title="From waypoint">
	The waypoint the active leg starts from.
</Field>

<Field id="to" title="Active waypoint">
	The identifier of the active waypoint. The arrow in front of it marks it as the active waypoint and flashes during [waypoint alerting](/kln90b/operation/sequencing/#waypoint-alerting).
</Field>

<Field id="cdi" title="D-bar">
	The D-bar shows the cross-track error from the desired track: the bar stands on the side the course lies on, so you fly toward it. Each of the five dots on either side is a fifth of the CDI scale factor, and the outer dot is full scale; beyond it the bar stays at the end. The TO/FROM indicator in the middle shows TO while the active waypoint lies ahead, FROM once it lies behind.
</Field>

<Field id="dis" title="Distance to the active waypoint">
	The distance to the active waypoint, in NM.
</Field>

<Field id="gs" title="Groundspeed">
	The groundspeed, in kt.
</Field>

<Field id="ete" title="Estimated time en route">
	The time to the active waypoint at the present groundspeed, as hours and minutes. Below one hour the hours stay blank, and below one minute the page shows `:00`.
</Field>

<Field id="brg" title="Bearing to the active waypoint">
	The magnetic bearing from the aircraft to the active waypoint.
</Field>

<Field id="left-page" title="Left page identification">
	The page identification on the left side. FPL 0 is the active flight plan.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	<Term t="ENR-LEG">`ENR-LEG`</Term>: the unit navigates en route in leg mode, along the legs of FPL 0.
</Field>

<Field id="right-page" title="Right page identification">
	The page identification on the right side.
</Field>
