<Field id="ident" title="Airport identifier">
	The airport whose departures and arrivals APT 7 shows, the same airport as on the other APT pages. To change it,
	turn the cursor on, turn the right outer knob counterclockwise from the first entry of the list until the cursor is on
	the identifier, and enter the new one.
</Field>

<Field id="heading" title="List heading">
	`SELECT STAR` heads the list of the airport's arrivals (<Term t="STAR" />s). On the page of departures it reads
	`SELECT SID`.
</Field>

<Field id="stars" title="Arrivals in the list">
	The arrivals the unit offers for the airport, numbered from 1 in the order of the database. Press the right
	<kbd>CRSR</kbd> to put the cursor on the first entry, turn the right outer knob to move it, and press <kbd>ENT</kbd>
	to select an arrival. Arrivals the unit cannot fly are not listed and get no number.
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Last arrival in the list">
	The bottom row always shows the last arrival of the list, so its number tells you how many there are. The four rows
	of the list hold the first three arrivals and this one until you scroll.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page name">
	`APT+7` is APT 7 with a second page: the airport has departures and arrivals, and the `+` says that the right inner
	knob leads to the other list. An airport with only one kind of procedure, or none, shows `APT 7`.
</Field>
