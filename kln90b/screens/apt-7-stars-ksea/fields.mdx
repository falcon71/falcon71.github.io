<Field id="ident" title="Airport identifier">
	The airport whose SIDs and STARs APT 7 shows, the same airport as on the other APT pages. To change it,
	turn the cursor on, turn the right outer knob counterclockwise from the first entry of the list until the cursor is on
	the identifier, and enter the new one.
</Field>

<Field id="heading" title="List heading">
	`SELECT STAR` heads the list of the airport's <Term t="STAR" />s. On the page of SIDs it reads
	`SELECT SID`.
</Field>

<Field id="stars" title="STARs in the list">
	The STARs the unit offers for the airport, numbered from 1 in the order of the database. Press the right
	<kbd>CRSR</kbd> to put the cursor on the first entry, turn the right outer knob to move it, and press <kbd>ENT</kbd>
	to select a STAR. STARs the unit cannot fly are not listed and get no number.
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Last STAR in the list">
	The bottom row always shows the last STAR of the list, so its number tells you how many there are. The four rows
	of the list hold the first three STARs and this one until you scroll.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	`APT+7` is APT 7 with a second page: the airport has SIDs and STARs, and the `+` says that the right inner
	knob leads to the other list. An airport with only one kind of procedure, or none, shows `APT 7`.
</Field>
