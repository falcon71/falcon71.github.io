<Field id="title" title="Procedure selected">
	The procedure you selected: its name, a dash and `SID` for a departure, or a small `STAR` for an arrival. It heads
	every step of the selection until the procedure is loaded.
</Field>

<Field id="heading" title="Runway list heading">
	`RUNWAY` asks for the runway the departure starts from or the arrival ends at. The unit asks only when the runways of
	the procedure lead to different waypoints.
</Field>

<Field id="runways" title="Runways in the list">
	The runways of the procedure, each with its number and `RW` before the runway. Parallel runways with the same
	waypoints share one entry with `B` in place of L, C or R (`RW10B`). The list has four rows and scrolls as you turn the
	right outer knob; here the first entry is above the page.
</Field>

<Field id="rw34c" title="Runway under the cursor">
	A runway of the procedure, marked by the cursor. Press <kbd>ENT</kbd> to select it, or <kbd>CLR</kbd> to go back to
	the list of procedures.
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Last runway in the list">
	The bottom row always shows the last runway of the list, so its number tells you how many entries there are.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="Cursor indication">
	`CRSR` shows that the right cursor is on. It stays on at every step of the selection, so you do not press the right
	<kbd>CRSR</kbd>.
</Field>
