<Field id="title" title="Procedure selected">
	The procedure you selected: its name, a dash and `SID` for a departure, or a small `STAR` for an arrival. FPL 0 shows
	the same name above the procedure's waypoints once it is loaded.
</Field>

<Field id="waypoints" title="Waypoints of the procedure">
	The waypoints the unit loads into FPL 0, in the order you fly them, numbered from 1. Legs that end at no waypoint,
	such as a climb on runway heading to an altitude, are not in the list. Turn the right outer knob counterclockwise to
	move the cursor up into the list and scroll it; the entries cannot be selected or changed here.
</Field>

<Field id="last" title="Last waypoint of the procedure">
	The bottom row of the list always shows the last waypoint of the procedure; its number tells you how many waypoints
	the procedure adds to FPL 0.
</Field>

<Field id="load" title="Load prompt">
	`LOAD IN FPL` loads the procedure into FPL 0 when you press <kbd>ENT</kbd>. The cursor is on it when the waypoints
	appear. If the airport is not in FPL 0, or FPL 0 holds a procedure of the same kind, the unit first asks a question
	on this page.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="Cursor indication">
	`CRSR` shows that the right cursor is on. It stays on at every step of the selection, so you do not press the right
	<kbd>CRSR</kbd>.
</Field>
