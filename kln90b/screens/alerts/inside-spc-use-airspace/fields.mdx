<Field id="message" title="Inside special use airspace">
	The first line of the message. You are inside an area of special use airspace, laterally and, with the vertical buffer, vertically. The next two lines name the area and give its limits. [`INSIDE SPC USE AIRSPACE`](/kln90b/operation/alerts/#inside-spc-use-airspace) explains the message.
</Field>

<Field id="name" title="Name of the area">
	The name of the area, cut to fit the line.
</Field>

<Field id="type" title="Type of the area">
	The type of the area at the right end of the line: `CL B` for Class B, `CL C` for Class C, `ALRT` for an alert area, `DNGR` for a danger area, `MOA`, `PROH` for a prohibited area, `REST` for a restricted area, `TRNG` for a training area and `WARN` for a warning area.
</Field>

<Field id="limits" title="Limits of the area">
	The vertical limits of the area in feet above mean sea level: `1000ft to 18000ft` with floor and ceiling, `ABOVE 1000ft` with a floor only, `BELOW 7000ft` with a ceiling only. Class B and C show the ceiling only. The line is missing where the area has no limit above mean sea level.
</Field>

<Field id="mode" title="Mode annunciation">
	<Term t="ENR-LEG">`ENR-LEG`</Term>: Enroute-Leg mode. See [The mode annunciations](/kln90b/operation/modes-of-operation/#the-mode-annunciations).
</Field>

<Field id="msg" title="Message prompt">
	<Term t="MSG">`MSG`</Term> in inverse video: the unit holds messages. <kbd>MSG</kbd> shows the next one or closes the page after the last.
</Field>
