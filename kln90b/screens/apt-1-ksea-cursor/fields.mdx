<Field id="ident" title="Airport identifier">
	The identifier of the airport, with the cursor on it. The right outer knob moves the cursor from one character to the next, and from the last character on to the next field of the page. The airport changes with every character you enter.
</Field>

<Field id="char" title="Character under the cursor">
	The character that the right inner knob changes. The change takes effect at once and needs no <kbd>ENT</kbd>. The knob steps through `0` to `9`, a blank and `A` to `Z`, and the first character never offers the blank. After each change the unit fills in the characters to the right of the cursor with the first airport that matches.
</Field>

<Field id="name" title="Airport name">
	The name of the airport, in two rows of 11 characters.
</Field>

<Field id="pos" title="Airport position">
	The position of the airport, latitude above and longitude below. You cannot change it for an airport of the database.
</Field>

<Field id="class" title="Airspace and type of the airport">
	The airspace the airport lies in (`CL B`, `CL C`, `TMA` or `CTA`) on the left, and `MILTRY` or `PRIVAT` on the right where they apply.
</Field>

<Field id="crsr" title="Cursor indication">
	`CRSR` replaces the name of the page while the right cursor is on. Pressing the right <kbd>CRSR</kbd> key turns the cursor off again, and the page name returns.
</Field>
