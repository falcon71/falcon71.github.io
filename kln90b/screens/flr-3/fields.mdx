<Field id="heading" title="Heading of the list">
	`INACTIVE` heads the list of the inactive failures: every failure that the equipment of the aircraft allows and that is not active. The list is sorted by the characters of the names and not grouped. Five rows are visible; with more failures the list scrolls under the cursor. The entries shown depend on the equipment of the aircraft.
</Field>

<Field id="entry-1" title="An inactive failure">
	`429 OUTPUT`, the ARINC 429 output of the unit. [What can fail](/kln90b/operation/failures/#what-can-fail) explains every name.
</Field>

<Field id="entry-2" title="An inactive failure">
	`ALTITUDE`, the altitude input from the aircraft. FLR offers it only in an aircraft whose unit has an altitude input and no air data input.
</Field>

<Field id="entry-3" title="An inactive failure">
	`ANT GAIN`, the gain of the GPS antenna.
</Field>

<Field id="entry-4" title="An inactive failure">
	`BATTERY`, the internal battery of the unit.
</Field>

<Field id="entry-5" title="An inactive failure">
	`EEPROM`, the memory that holds the calibration of the unit.
</Field>

<Field id="page" title="Page identification">
	The page identification on the left side.
</Field>
