---
title: Contact
description: Where to report a bug in the simulation, ask a question, or point out an error in this manual.
---

You report bugs and ask questions on GitHub or on Discord. Errors in this manual go to the issues of the manual's own repository.

- **Bugs in the simulation:** open an issue in the [kln90b repository](https://github.com/falcon71/kln90b/issues), or tell the community on [Discord](https://discord.gg/ZdQ6htedtT). Search the open and the closed issues first, because your bug may be known. Say what you did, what the unit showed and what you expected, and name the aircraft and the version of the KLN 90B package, because the unit behaves differently in different aircraft.
- **Questions:** ask in the [issues](https://github.com/falcon71/kln90b/issues) of the same repository, or on the [Discord community](https://discord.gg/ZdQ6htedtT), where other pilots answer too.
- **Errors in this manual:** a wrong statement, a broken link or a screen that does not match the unit goes to the [issues of the manual's repository](https://github.com/falcon71/falcon71.github.io/issues).
