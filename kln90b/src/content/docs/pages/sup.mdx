The SUP page of the KLN 90B shows one supplemental waypoint: a user waypoint that is no airport, VOR, NDB or intersection. It is also the page on which a new waypoint is defined, from the page itself, from the Direct To page or from a flight plan.

You enter an identifier, scan through the list, deal with duplicate identifiers and approve the page as on every waypoint page; [Waypoints and the database](/kln90b/operation/waypoints/) explains all of it. This chapter covers what the SUP page shows and does differently.

## SUP page: supplemental waypoints

The SUP page shows the identifier of the waypoint, the reference waypoint with the radial and the distance of the waypoint from it, and the latitude and the longitude. A supplemental waypoint is always a [user waypoint](/kln90b/operation/user-waypoints/), so every field except the identifier can be changed with the cursor.

<KlnScreen screen="sup-user-waypoint" />

The reference works as on the INT page: the unit finds the VOR nearest to the waypoint after a search, and you can enter another waypoint as the reference ([The reference waypoint](/kln90b/pages/int/#the-reference-waypoint)).

The complete list of the SUP page holds every supplemental waypoint, mixed by identifier: the ones you created on this page, on the Direct To page or in a flight plan, and the ones the unit made for itself ([Waypoints the unit creates by itself](/kln90b/operation/user-waypoints/#waypoints-the-unit-creates-by-itself)). The user-defined airports, VORs, NDBs and intersections are not in it; they stand in the lists of their own pages. All of them are stored in the user database ([`USR DB FULL`](/kln90b/operation/user-waypoints/#usr-db-full)).

Three kinds of waypoint that the unit made show their reference, radial and distance at once, without the wait of the search. A reference waypoint that you made on the REF page shows the waypoint you entered there, which can be a waypoint of any type; a center waypoint and a DME arc waypoint show their VOR. On the ACT pages the unit marks a supplemental waypoint with an `S` ([ACT pages](/kln90b/pages/act/)).

### No supplemental waypoints stored

A unit without a supplemental waypoint shows `0` in place of the identifier, the offer to create a waypoint, and `NO SUP WPTS` on the status line each time you select the page. [The other empty lists](/kln90b/operation/waypoints/#no-sup-wpts-and-the-other-empty-lists) explains the message.

<KlnScreen screen="waypoint-no-sup-wpts" />

### Defining a new waypoint

When the identifier you enter has no match in the list, the SUP page shows `CREATE NEW WPT AT:` with `USER POS?` and `PRES POS?` instead of the fields; [Creating a user waypoint](/kln90b/operation/user-waypoints/#creating-a-user-waypoint) explains the two answers. The offer also appears on the SUP page when <kbd>ENT</kbd> on the Direct To page or an FPL page finds no waypoint of any type ([Creating a waypoint from the Direct To page or a flight plan](/kln90b/operation/user-waypoints/#creating-a-waypoint-from-the-direct-to-page-or-a-flight-plan)). You then cannot change the identifier on the SUP page, and the scan does nothing while the offer is shown.

<KlnScreen screen="direct-to-create-waypoint" />

## Where the SUP page is used

- [Waypoints and the database](/kln90b/operation/waypoints/): choosing a supplemental waypoint and the empty list.
- [User waypoints](/kln90b/operation/user-waypoints/): creating, editing and deleting a supplemental waypoint.
- [Direct-to](/kln90b/operation/direct-to/): the offer to create a waypoint that you want to fly to.
- [Flight plans](/kln90b/operation/flight-plans/): adding a new waypoint to a flight plan.
